# Staff Members: General Information {#_a1fbb368-7e06-49b3-9a2d-e68d7b879727 .concept}

In Acumatica ERP, a staff member is an employee or a vendor who performs the services specified in a service document, typically by visiting a customer’s location.

## Learning Objectives { .section}

In this chapter, you will learn how to define an employee as a staff member.

## Applicable Scenarios { .section}

You define employees as staff members in Acumatica ERP in any of the following cases:

-   When initially setting up the service management functionality in Acumatica ERP
-   When your organization hires a new employee who needs to be defined as a staff member in service management.

## Types of Staff Members {#_e97e4ef1-9014-4421-9a3b-e94b2c2e9504 .section}

In Acumatica ERP, you can define the following types of staff members:

-   **An employee** of your company can be defined as a staff member. You use the [Employees](../UserGuide/EP_20_30_00.md) \(EP203000\) form to add, view, and edit an employee's record. To define an employee as a staff member, select the **Staff Member in Service Management** check box on the **General Info** tab \(**Employee Settings** section\) of the form.
-   **A vendor**—an external service provider engaged by your company—can also be defined as a staff member in service management. You use the [Vendors](../UserGuide/AP_30_30_00.md) \(AP303000\) form to add, view, and edit vendor's account. To define a vendor as a staff member, select the **Staff Member in Service Management** check box on the **General** tab \(**Vendor Properties** section\) of the form.

You can view the list of staff members of both types on the [Staff](../UserGuide/FS_20_55_00.md) \(FS205500\) form.
