# Drivers {#_21ce639f-f2e1-4df2-8546-0f15ae97eec2 .concept}

In Acumatica ERP, you can define employees as staff members—individuals responsible for delivering the services your company offers to customers. By assigning a driving skill to staff members, you can designate them as drivers, indicating that they are capable of executing routes. This makes it easy to select the right person for specific route services. This chapter explains how to add drivers to the system.
