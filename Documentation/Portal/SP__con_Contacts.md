# Your Company's Contacts {#_9155f14f-de9b-47dc-a4de-9873fccf281e .concept}

In the Acumatica Self-Service Portal, *customer contacts* are the users of your company that have permission to access the system and work in it.

On the [Contact](SP_40_80_41.md) \(SP408041\) form, you can create customer contacts and enter their basic settings, such as the contact's name, position, and email address.

You can open this form in any of the following ways:

-   By clicking the **Add New** button on the [Contacts](SP_40_80_40.md) \(SP408040\) form.

    **Attention:** The [Contacts](SP_40_80_40.md) form is available only for users with the *Customer Admin*, *Administrator*, or *Portal Admin* roles.

-   By clicking a contact in the **Contact** column of the [Contacts](SP_40_80_40.md) form.
-   By clicking the [Contact](SP_40_80_41.md) form in the workspace or using the search.

If the contact will sign in to the Self-Service Portal, you add the settings of the user account of the contact, such as the time zone, user status and type, username, and password.

On the **User Info** tab of the [Contact](SP_40_80_41.md) form, you can specify information about the user account of the customer contact. A user with an active user account is allowed to access the Self-Service Portal. To prohibit the user from accessing the system, you disable the user account.

If employees of your organization who use the Self-Service Portal need to generate documents with the business date in a time zone that differs from the default time zone \(which is specified by the portal administrator\), you can change the time zone for each of these employees manually.

**Attention:** If your user account has the *Customer User* role assigned but none of the administrative roles, you can see contacts on the form, but can edit only your own contact settings.

For more information on adding contacts, see [To Create a Contact](SP__How_Adding_new_user.md).
