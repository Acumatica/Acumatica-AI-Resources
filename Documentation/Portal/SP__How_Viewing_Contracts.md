# To View Contract Details {#_bfb68495-a367-4121-91de-21e4d866a99a .task}

You can use the [My Contracts](SP_40_40_00.md) \(SP404000\) form to view the available contracts in the Acumatica Self-Service Portal. From this form, you can open the [Contract](SP_30_10_00.md) \(SP301000\) form to view the details of any listed contract. For more information about contracts, see [Viewing of Contracts](SP__con_Contracts.md).

**Attention:** This functionality appears in the Self-Service Portal, if the *Contract Management* feature is enabled on the Enable/Disable \(CS100000\) form in Acumatica ERP.

## To View Contract Details { .section}

1.  Open the [My Contracts](SP_40_40_00.md) \(SP404000\) form.
2.  In the **Status** box \(the Selection area\), select the status of the contract to narrow the range of contracts listed in the table.
3.  In the table, click the identifier of contract you want to see. The [Contract](SP_30_10_00.md) \(SP301000\) form opens.
4.  Review the details of the selected contract.
