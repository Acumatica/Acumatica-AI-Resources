# Using the Shopping Cart {#_050ad30b-e331-4aa7-b211-6813ae4f40b7 .concept}

On the [My Cart](SP_70_00_01.md) \(SP700001\) form of the Acumatica Self-Service Portal, you can review the items in your cart, return to the [Catalog](SP_70_00_00.md) \(SP700000\) form to continue shopping, or proceed to checkout.

You can open your shopping cart by clicking the **Open Cart** button on the [Catalog](SP_70_00_00.md) \(SP700000\) form.

**Attention:** This functionality appears in the Self-Service Portal, if the *B2B Ordering* feature is enabled on the Enable/Disable \(CS100000\) form in Acumatica ERP.

## Adding Items to the Cart { .section}

You place items in your shopping cart by using the [Catalog](SP_70_00_00.md) \(SP700000\) form. First, you select the check box in the **Add to Cart** column of each needed item to make preliminary selections. If needed, you change default settings in the rows with these items. Then you click the **Add to Cart** button on the table toolbar to add the items to your cart. For detailed instructions of this process, [To Add Items to the Shopping Cart](SP__how_Shopping_Add_to_Cart.md).

When you add items to the cart, the contents of the cart are automatically saved. Even if you sign out of the system, your cart will contain the items that you have added.

You can continue shopping and go back to the [Catalog](SP_70_00_00.md) form by clicking the **Continue Shopping** button on the form toolbar of the [My Cart](SP_70_00_01.md) \(SP700001\) form.

When you have finished adding items to your cart, you click the **Proceed to Checkout** button on the form toolbar. This opens the [Checkout](SP_70_00_02.md) \(SP700002\) form, where you can confirm your order and check out. For more information about the checkout process, see [To Add Items to the Shopping Cart](SP__how_Shopping_Add_to_Cart.md) and [Placing Orders](SP__con_Shopping_Orders.md).

## Modifying the Contents of the Cart { .section}

You can modify the contents of the shopping cart by deleting any added items and changing the quantities of the added items on the [My Cart](SP_70_00_01.md) \(SP700001\) form. You can add new items, delete any added items, and change the quantities of the added items on the [Catalog](SP_70_00_00.md) \(SP700000\) form.

If you want to remove all items in the cart, you should use the **Clear Cart** button on the [My Cart](SP_70_00_01.md) form.

For details, see [To Change the Contents of the Cart](SP__How_Shopping_Change_Cart.md) and [To Clear Your Cart](SP__how_Shopping_Clear_Cart.md).
