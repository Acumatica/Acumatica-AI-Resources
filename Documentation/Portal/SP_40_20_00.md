# My Documents {#_f6abf0ce-36d4-4c3a-9482-972b2ecef817 .reference}

Form ID: \(SP402000\)

On this form of the Acumatica Self-Service Portal, you can check your company's current balance and prepayment balance and track all the documents associated with your company.

**Attention:** All amounts are displayed in your company’s base currency as defined in its record on the Customers \(AR303000\) form of Acumatica ERP.

## Form Toolbar { .section}

The form toolbar includes standard and form-specific buttons. The form-specific buttons are listed below.

|Command|Description|
|-------|-----------|
|**Aging Report MC**|Opens the AR Aging MC \(AR631100\) report. This report shows your company’s released AR documents whose document dates are earlier than or the same as the aging date. You can print the report or export it to a PDF or to Microsoft Excel.

 The documents in the report are grouped by statement cycle and broken down by aging periods or financial periods, depending on the settings of the statement cycle specified for your company on the **Financial** tab of the Customers \(AR303000\) form in Acumatica ERP.

 The amounts are shown in the currency of the document and in the base currency for your company. The balance of the AR documents is calculated as the difference between the original amount of the documents and the application amounts of the documents that were applied no later than the specified aging date.

|
|**Aging Report**|Opens the AR Aging \(AR631000\) report. This report displays your company's outstanding balance broken down by days past due. You can print the report or export it to a PDF or to Microsoft Excel.

 The system uses the business date when calculating the aging categories for the documents. All the amounts are shown in your company’s base currency.

|
|**Print Account History**|Opens the AR Balance by Customer \(AR632500\) report. This report displays the outstanding balance of your company during the current financial period. You can print the report or export it to a PDF or to Microsoft Excel.

 The documents are grouped by account–subaccount pair and listed by reference number. You can easily see each document’s dates, original amount, and balance after payment application.

|
|**Print Invoice/Memo**|Opens a ready-to-print document on the Invoice/Memo \(AR641000\) report.

 The document provides the company information \(in the top left\), the ship-to and bill-to addresses, the pertinent shipping information, and the itemized information about the shipped items.

 You can print the document or export it to a PDF or to Microsoft Excel.

|

## Summary Area { .section}

You use the elements in this area to view outstanding balances as well as available credit.

|Element|Description|
|-------|-----------|
|**Outstanding Invoices and Memos**|The outstanding balance of your company \(the sum of its open invoices, credit and debit memos, and overdue charges\).|
|**Unapplied Payments**|The balance of your company's open payments \(the sum of unapplied payments and prepayments\).|
|**Net Balance**|The total balance of all open documents associated with your company, including the total balance of prepayments.|
|**Outstanding Invoices and Memos \(Currency\)**|Your company’s outstanding balance \(the sum of open invoices, credit and debit memos, and overdue charges\) in the specified currency.

 **Attention:** This box appears on the form only if the *Multicurrency Accounting* feature is enabled on the Enable/Disable \(CS101000\) form in Acumatica ERP.

|
|**Unapplied Payments \(Currency\)**|The balance of your company's open payments \(the sum of unapplied payments and prepayments\) in the specified currency.

 **Attention:** This box appears on the form only if the *Multicurrency Accounting* feature is enabled on the Enable/Disable \(CS101000\) form in Acumatica ERP.

|
|**Net Balance \(Currency\)**|The total balance of all open documents associated with your company, including the total balance of prepayments, in the specified currency.

 **Attention:**

This box appears on the form only if the *Multicurrency Accounting* feature is enabled on the Enable/Disable \(CS101000\) form in Acumatica ERP.

|
|**Credit Limit**|The credit limit that is set for your company.|
|**Available Credit**|The credit limit that is not used regarding the current balance of your company.|
|**Currency**|The currency of the documents to be included in the list.

 **Attention:** This box appears on the form only if the *Multicurrency Accounting* feature is enabled on the Enable/Disable \(CS101000\) form in Acumatica ERP.

|

## All Records Tab { .section}

This tab contains the list of documents associated with your company.

The table toolbar includes only standard buttons.

|Column|Description|
|------|-----------|
|**Type**|The document’s type.|
|**Reference Number**|The document’s reference number.|
|**Date**|The date when the document was created.|
|**Due Date**|The date when payment for the document is due.|
|**Status**|The status of the document.|
|**Currency**|The currency of the document.

 **Attention:** This column appears on the tab only if the *Multicurrency Accounting* feature is enabled on the Enable/Disable \(CS101000\) form in Acumatica ERP.

|
|**Document Total**|The original amount of the document before payment application.|
|**Currency Document Total**|The original amount of the document in the specified currency before payment application.

 This column appears on the tab only if the following conditions are met:

 -   The *Multicurrency Accounting* feature is enabled on the Enable/Disable \(CS101000\) form in Acumatica ERP.
-   The **Currency** box is cleared in the Summary area of the current form.

|
|**Open Balance**|The balance of the document if a payment was applied.|
|**Currency Open Balance**|The balance of the document in the specified currency if a payment was applied.

 This column appears on the tab only if the following conditions are met:

 -   The *Multicurrency Accounting* feature is enabled on the Enable/Disable \(CS101000\) form in Acumatica ERP.
-   The **Currency** box is cleared in the Summary area of the current form.

|
|**Description**|The description of the document.|
|**Branch**|The branch associated with the document.|
|**Customer**|The customer account associated with the document.|
