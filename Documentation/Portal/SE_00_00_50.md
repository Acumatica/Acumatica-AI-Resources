# Search Form {#_184efd14-3453-41c2-bb9f-5c948f5f282e .reference}

Form ID: \(SE000050\)

Searching in the Self-Service Portal gives you the ability to quickly open a form or find a help topic with the needed information.

## Search Form { .section}

You start a search by typing a word, form number, or phrase in the **Search** box, which is located in the top pane of the \(see the screenshot below\). The search results appear on the form specifically designed for searches. The following screenshot shows the **Search** box.
