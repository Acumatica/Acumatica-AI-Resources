# Placing Orders {#_d20377fb-280d-4aa9-8218-2a2527f36eb9 .concept}

After you have reviewed the contents of your cart, you can proceed to placing the order in the Self-Service Portal.

**Attention:** This functionality appears in the Self-Service Portal, if the *B2B Ordering* feature is enabled on the Enable/Disable \(CS100000\) form in Acumatica ERP.

## Order Placement Process { .section}

You can place the order by using the [Checkout](SP_70_00_02.md) \(SP700002\) form, which is a wizard that has multiple pages, one for each part of the checkout process. The form opens when you click the **Proceed to Checkout** button on the [My Cart](SP_70_00_01.md) \(SP700001\) form.

On the Shipping Details page of the wizard, which is the first page you see, you can review the order details and the shipping information. By default, the shipping information is automatically added from your contact information, but you can select the **Edit Address** check box on the [Checkout](SP_70_00_02.md) form and enter any location you want. The taxes and the shipping charges are automatically calculated, based on the shipping location, and added to the total cost of the order.

**Tip:** If you need to change the items and their quantities for the order, you should return to your cart by clicking the **Go Back to Cart** button on the form toolbar of the Shipping Details page of the wizard.

You continue placing your order by clicking the **Continue** button on the form toolbar of the Shipping Details page of the wizard. On the Shipping Information Page, which opens, you review and confirm the order information, including the order totals, the shipping information, and the items included in the order.

After you submit your order, a printable version of the created sales order is generated automatically on the Submitted Order Page of the wizard. You can print the sales order or export it to a PDF or to Microsoft Excel.

For detailed instructions, see [To Place an Order](SP__How_Shopping_Make_Order.md).
