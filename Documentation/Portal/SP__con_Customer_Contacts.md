# Managing Customer Accounts {#_dadf9d1a-6795-45e3-aef4-e9b2f65c34b0 .concept}

Acting as an employee of the company that uses the Acumatica ERP instance, in the Acumatica Self-Service Portal, you can use the [Customer Account](SP_40_80_32.md) \(SP408032\) form to create a customer contact and link it to a business account. In the Self-Service Portal, you can open the [Customer Account](SP_40_80_32.md) \(SP408032\) form by clicking a business account identifier on the [Customer Accounts](SP_40_80_33.md) \(SP408033\) form.

*Customer accounts* are business accounts of companies that can access the Self-Service Portal. *Customer contacts* are the employees of your company who have permission to access to the Self-Service Portal and work in it.

For each customer account, you create a customer contact, which is linked to a company's business account. Then you enter contact information for the customer's employee, which can be the contact's name, position, and email address.

**Attention:** You can create a customer contact by signing in to the Self-Service Portal instance with a user account that has the *Administrator* role assigned.
