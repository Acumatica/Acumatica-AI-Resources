# Managing Online Orders {#_d32d476f-7924-46fa-b072-86375370cab4 .concept}

In the Acumatica Self-Service Portal, you can order items online. You can browse the list of items, view detailed item information, place orders, and monitor their progress.

In this chapter, you can find information on how to use the catalog and the shopping cart, and then place and track your orders.
