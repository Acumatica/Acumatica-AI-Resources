# To Clear Your Cart {#_8abc7c19-0d96-4947-bdfe-bee07c93fdc7 .task}

In this topic, you will learn how to clear your shopping cart in the Acumatica Self-Service Portal.

**Attention:** You can view the [My Cart](SP_70_00_01.md) \(SP700001\) form if you are signed in to the Self-Service Portal with a user account that has the *Customer Admin* or *Customer User* role assigned.

## To Clear the Cart { .section}

1.  In the Self-Service Portal, open the [My Cart](SP_70_00_01.md) \(SP700001\) form. In the table, view the items that you have added to the shopping cart.
2.  On the form toolbar, click **Clear Cart**.
3.  In the **Clear Cart** dialog box, which opens, click **Yes** to confirm that you want to remove all items from the cart.

Your cart is cleared and you can begin the ordering process at your convenience. For more information, see [To Add Items to the Shopping Cart](SP__how_Shopping_Add_to_Cart.md).
