# Your Company's Information {#_22e32fa1-6cf9-4b95-a50f-6e6cd9c4297e .concept}

You can manage company-related information by using the Acumatica Self-Service Portal.

On the [Company Profile](SP_40_80_30.md) \(SP408030\) form, you can view and update your company's basic information, such as the official website, phone number, and main address. You can also fill in the **Attention** box to define the person or a department to whom emails or documents should be referred.

**Attention:** To edit the information on this form, you must be signed in to the Self-Service Portal with a user account that has the *Customer Admin*, *Administrator*, or *Portal Admin* role assigned to it.

For details, see [To Edit Your Company's Account Information](SP__How_Company_Edit_account.md).
