# Viewing of Contracts {#_a44565dd-1952-432e-bd5d-150c859ef0b5 .concept}

A *contract* represents an agreement between a vendor \(the seller of goods or provider of a service\) and a customer \(the recipient of the goods or the consumer of the service\). Contracts consist of different combinations of terms and items geared toward the particular needs and capabilities of the organizations involved.

For example, a contract may specify that your company will be provided certain services—such as cable TV service, Internet access, or web account hosting—from a vendor during a specified period of time. Other contracts may contain service agreements to maintain and repair a product during the warranty period of the manufacturer or a particular time period. Service contracts often include a number of cases that may be solved during a warranty period. A case includes the efforts, work time spent on problem resolution and the related phone calls and emails, to resolve a particular issue.

On the [My Contracts](SP_40_40_00.md) \(SP404000\) form, you can view the contracts associated with the customers included in your company account.

**Attention:** This functionality appears in the Self-Service Portal, if the *Contract Management* feature is enabled on the Enable/Disable \(CS100000\) form in Acumatica ERP. To see the [My Contracts](SP_40_40_00.md) form, you must be signed in to the Self-Service Portal with a user account that has the *Customer Admin* or *Administrator* role assigned to it.

You can check the contract settings on the [Contract](SP_30_10_00.md) \(SP301000\) form. The contract settings include such data as the contract status, the setup and expiration dates of the contract, the grace period, and the contract services.

Fore more information, see [To View Contract Details](SP__How_Viewing_Contracts.md).
