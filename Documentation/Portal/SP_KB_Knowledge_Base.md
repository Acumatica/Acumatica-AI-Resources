# Knowledge Base {#_ebc1a935-6379-4356-baad-bdb6871c9a16 .concept}

The Knowledge Base \(KB\) wiki is designed to contain support references, such as information on specific solutions, frequently asked questions \(FAQ\), and common troubleshooting issues and their resolutions. The KB articles are created by our support professionals to ensure that you have access to all useful information.

## Viewing a KB Article { .section}

A typical KB article includes the following information:

-   **Title**: The number and the title of the KB article
-   **Category**: The category the article affects
-   **Applies to**: The product the article is applied to
-   **Last modified**: The date and time of the last modification of the article
-   **Views**: The number of times the article has been viewed
-   **Rating**: The article rating
-   The contents of the article: The symptoms, the cause, and the resolution of the problem
-   **Related Links**: Optional; links to additional information you might find useful

## Searching for KB Articles { .section}

You can browse the wiki by using the navigation pane or search for specific articles. While searching for articles, you can use filters and sorting to get the results more quickly and easily. For more information, see [Search](SP_Search_Options_and_the_Search_Form.md).

## Rating Articles { .section}

You can rate the article you are reading to help us assess the quality of the articles, if available.

To rate an article:

-   On the feedback bar at the bottom of the article, in the **Rate this article** box, select the rate that corresponds to your thoughts about how helpful the article is, and click **Rate**.

## Submitting Feedback { .section}

We welcome any feedback you may have to help us improve. Any time you are seeking assistance or just browsing the wiki, you can provide comments. Also, if you don't find the information you are looking for, you can post a suggestion for a new article. The more information you provide about the information you need, the better we will be able to understand and assist you.

To submit feedback:

1.  On the feedback bar at the bottom of the article, click **Feedback**.
2.  In the **Feedback** dialog box, answer the available questions.
3.  Click **Submit** at the top of the dialog box.
