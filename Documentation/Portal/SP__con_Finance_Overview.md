# Your Company's Financial Documents {#_8c00abc6-aab8-4743-af76-71f7ba11188b .concept}

By using the Acumatica Self-Service Portal, you can keep track of your company’s account. You can view documents associated with all companies and branches that are available for the tenant, only a particular branch, or only a particular company. Also, you can view the statement history and check the outstanding, overdue, and prepayment balances.

**Attention:** All amounts are displayed in your company’s base currency as defined in its record on the Customers \(AR303000\) form in Acumatica ERP.

## Viewing Documents { .section}

By using the [My Documents](SP_40_20_00.md) \(SP402000\) form, you can do the following:

-   Check the outstanding and prepayment balances of invoices.
-   Check the balances of credit memos.
-   View documents—such as invoices, credit memos, or prepayments—.
-   View and print invoices or credit memos. For details, see [To Print an Invoice or Credit Memo](SP__how_Print_Invoice.md).
-   View, print, or export to a PDF or to Microsoft Excel any of the following reports: AR Aging \(AR631000\), AR Aging MC \(AR631100\), and AR Balance by Customer \(AR632500\).

**Attention:** The visibility of financial documents depends on the **Display Financial Documents** settings specified on the [Portal Preferences](SP_80_00_00.md) \(SP800000\) form. You access this form by signing in to your Self-Service Portal instance with a user account that has the *Customer Admin*, *Administrator*, or *Portal Admin* role assigned.

## Viewing Statements { .section}

On the [My Statements](SP_40_46_00.md) \(SP404600\) form, you can view the statement history and check the documents included in a selected statement.

To open the Customer Statement \(AR641500\) print form, you need to select the period in which the statements were generated, select the statement, and then click the **Print Statement** button on the form toolbar.
