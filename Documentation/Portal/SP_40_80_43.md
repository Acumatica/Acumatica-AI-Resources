# Customer Contacts {#_ab1faef2-d494-4638-ba58-b5c9890a394e .reference}

Form ID: \(SP408043\)

On this form, you can see the contacts of your customer account that can access the Acumatica Self-Service Portal and open their accounts for editing.

## Table { .section}

The table toolbar includes only standard buttons.

|Column|Description|
|------|-----------|
|**Contact**|The name of the contact, which is a combination of the names and the courtesy title.

 Click the contact name to open the [Customer Contact](SP_40_80_42.md) \(SP408042\) form to review and edit the contact information.

|
|**Job Title**|The job title or position of the contact in your company.|
|**Active**|A check box that indicates \(if selected\) that the contact is active.|
|**Account ID**|The identifier of the customer account the contact belongs to.|
|**Account Name**|The customer company the user belongs to.|
|**Email**|The email address of the contact.|
|**Phone 1**|The phone number of the contact.|
|**City**|The city of the contact.|
|**Country**|The two-letter code representing the country of the contact.|
