# My Contracts {#_ab30210d-8f78-4b65-9286-791b4c017162 .reference}

Form ID: \(SP404000\)

On this form, you can see the contracts associated with your company’s customer account.

**Tip:** Some of the company's contracts may not be listed on the form because you do not have sufficient access rights to view them.

## Selection Area { .section}

In this area of the Acumatica Self-Service Portal, you can specify the status of the contracts to be listed in the table.

|Element|Description|
|-------|-----------|
|**Status**|The status of the contract to be listed in the table. You can select one of the following options:

 -   *All* \(default\): The table will show all customer contracts created in the system.
-   *Draft*: The table will show contracts that have not been activated and cannot be used yet.
-   *Pending Approval*: The table will show contracts that are in the approval process.
-   *Pending Activation*: The table will show contracts that are set up and need to be activated.
-   *Active*: The table will show contracts that have been activated; your company will be billed based on these contracts.
-   *Expired*: The table will show contracts that have expired.
-   *Pending Upgrade*: The table will show contracts that are being prepared for upgrade.

|

## Table { .section}

This table contains the list of contracts.

The table toolbar includes only standard buttons.

|Column|Description|
|------|-----------|
|**Contract ID**|The identifier of the contract.

 You can click this identifier to open the [Contract](SP_30_10_00.md) \(SP301000\) form and view the settings of the contract.

|
|**Status**|The status of the contract, which is one of the following:

 -   *Draft*: The contract has not been activated and cannot be used yet.
-   *Pending Approval*: The contract is in the approval process.
-   *Pending Activation*: The contract has been set up and needs to be activated.
-   *Active*: The contract has been activated; your company will be billed based on it.
-   *Expired*: The contract has expired. A renewable contract with this status can be renewed within the grace period \(the period of time after the expiration date\).
-   *Pending Upgrade*: The contract is being prepared for upgrade.

|
|**Description**|The short description of the contract.|
|**Customer Name**|The customer with which the contract was signed.|
|**Account**|The specific business account that is to be billed for contract services.|
|**Setup Date**|The contract setup date.|
|**Expiration Date**|The date when the contract is scheduled to expire.|
