# Customer Accounts {#_958e5261-0d28-4813-aa7e-ba559052e1cd .reference}

Form ID: \(SP408033\)

On this form, acting as an employee of the company that uses the Acumatica ERP instance, you can see the customer accounts whose contacts can be set up to access the Acumatica Self-Service Portal. On this form, you can create customer contacts for business accounts of your customers \(customer accounts\).

*Customer contacts* are the employees of your company added by the administrator to the [Contacts](SP_40_80_40.md) \(SP408040\) form of the Self-Service Portal. *Customer accounts* are business accounts of your customers whose employees \(contacts\) can be set up to access the Self-Service Portal.

To enter or edit the information on this form, you must be signed in to the Self-Service Portal with a user account that has the *Administrator* role assigned to it.

## Table { .section}

The table toolbar includes only standard buttons.

|Column|Description|
|------|-----------|
|**Business Account**|The identifier of the customer's company.

 You can click the business account identifier to open the [Customer Account](SP_40_80_32.md) \(SP408032\) form. On this form, you can create a customer contact, linked to the business account of the customer.

|
|**Business Account Name**|The business name of the customer.|
|**Type**|The type of the business account.|
|**Class**|The identifier of the class that the business account belongs to.|
|**Email**|The email address of the customer.|
|**Phone 1**|The phone number of the customer.|
|**Address Line 1**|The address line of the customer.|
|**City**|The city of the customer.|
|**Country**|The two-letter code representing the country of the customer.|
