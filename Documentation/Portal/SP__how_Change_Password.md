# To Change Your Password {#_0c2ad6b9-3c6c-4ed8-bef1-5bb96f06da14 .task}

In this topic, you will learn how to change your password in the Self-Service Portal.

## To Change Your Password { .section}

In the Self-Service Portal, do the following to change your password:

1.  In the Info area \(in the top right corner of the Self-Service Portal screen\), click the User menu button \(where your name appears\).
2.  On the User menu, which opens, click the **My Profile** button. The [User Profile](SP_40_80_45.md) \(SP408045\) form opens.
3.  On the **User Info** tab, in the **Password** box, enter your new password.
4.  On the form toolbar, click **Save**. The next time you sign in to the system, you should use your new password.
