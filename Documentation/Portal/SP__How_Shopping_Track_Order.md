# To Track an Order {#_8027bef1-7979-4239-9fd3-473420d6d4f6 .task}

In this topic, you will learn how to track and print an order in the Acumatica Self-Service Portal by using the [My Orders](SP_70_00_03.md) \(SP700003\) form. You can view such information as the delivery date, the shipments, the quantities of items, and the total amount of the order.

## To Track and Print an Order { .section}

1.  In the Self-Service Portal, open the [My Orders](SP_70_00_03.md) \(SP700003\) form.

    All the orders placed with your company account are displayed in the table.

2.  Find the order you want to track, and note its status in the **Status** column.
3.  Review the listed settings of the order, such as the date, delivery date, ordered quantity, and total amount of the order. Click the row with the order.
4.  On the form toolbar, click **Print Order** to print the order or export it to a PDF or to Microsoft Excel.
