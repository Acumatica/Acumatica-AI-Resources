# My Orders {#_f9a76fe7-b26b-4295-9288-d1511f5c3040 .reference}

Form ID: \(SP700003\)

On this form of the Acumatica Self-Service Portal, you can see your orders associated with your company’s customer account, as well as the orders' statuses and totals and the delivery dates of items.

## Table { .section}

The table toolbar includes standard and table-specific buttons.

The table-specific buttons are listed below. Any of these buttons can be clicked after you select an order in the table.

|Button|Description|
|------|-----------|
|**Print Order**|Opens the order you have selected in the row as a print-ready document.

 The document displays the company information \(in the top left\), the ship-to and bill-to addresses, the pertinent shipping information, and the information about the quantities of the shipped items.

 You can print or export the document to a PDF or to Microsoft Excel.

|
|**View Shipments**|Opens the **Shipments** dialog box, which displays the shipment details.|
|**Copy Order to Cart**|Opens the [My Cart](SP_70_00_01.md) \(SP700001\) form and copies the items in the selected order to the cart.|
|**Cancel Order**|Cancels the selected order.

 This button is available if the selected order has the *Open* or *On Hold* status.

|

|Column|Description|
|------|-----------|
|**Order Nbr.**|The reference number of the document.

 Click the number to open the order in a print-ready format. The document provides the company information \(in the top left\), the ship-to and bill-to addresses, the pertinent shipping information, and the quantities and prices of the shipped items.

 You can print the document or export it to a PDF or to Microsoft Excel.

|
|**Status**|The status of the document, which is assigned automatically and can be one of the following options: *Order*, *On Hold*, *Credit Hold*, *Open*, *Back Order*, *Shipping*, *Canceled*, *Invoiced*, or *Completed*.|
|**Date**|The date of the document.|
|**Delivery Date**|The expected delivery date.|
|**Ordered Qty.**|The total quantity of items in the order.|
|**Order Total**|The total amount of the document.|
|**Currency**|The currency of the document.|
|**Location**|The customer location from which the items have been ordered.|
|**Full Name**|The user who created the order.|
|**Type**|The type of the document.|

|Button|Description|
|------|-----------|
|**Shipment Nbr.**|The unique reference number of the shipment confirmation.

 Click the number to open the print-ready form of the document on the [Shipment Confirmation](../UserGuide/SO_64_20_00.md) \(SO642000\) report. The document provides the company information \(in the top left\), the ship-to address, the pertinent shipping information, and the information about the quantities of the shipped items.

|
|**Status**|The status of the document, which can be one of the following options: *On Hold*, *Open*, *Confirmed*, or *Completed*.

 For details on shipments’ statuses, see [To Track a Shipment](SP__how_Shopping_Track_shipments.md).

|
|**Shipment Date**|The date of the shipment.|
|**Location**|The location to receive the shipment.|
