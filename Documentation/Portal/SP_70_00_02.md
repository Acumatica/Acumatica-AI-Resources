# Checkout {#_75b50bf4-13b3-47b4-8612-9fc862002445 .reference}

Form ID: \(SP700002\)

You can use this form, which is a wizard consisting of multiple pages, to review and submit your order.

The form opens when you click the **Proceed to Checkout** button on the form toolbar of the [My Cart](SP_70_00_01.md) \(SP700001\) form.

By using the wizard, you can do any of the following:

-   Specify or change the shipping information and confirm the order totals
-   Submit the order
-   Print the submitted order or export it to a PDF or to Microsoft Excel

## Shipping Details Page {#CheckoutStep1 .section}

On this page of the wizard, you specify and review the shipping settings, including the ship-to information and the totals associated with the order. These totals, including the shipping charges and taxes, are calculated in accordance with the shipping terms.

You can open this page by doing either of the following:

-   Clicking the **Proceed to Checkout** button on the [My Cart](SP_70_00_01.md) \(SP700001\) form. This is the first page you see in the wizard.
-   Clicking the **Return to Shipping Details** button on the Shipping Information page of the wizard.

|Button|Description|
|------|-----------|
|**Continue**|Goes to the Shipping Information page of the wizard, where you can confirm the details of your order and submit it.|
|**Go Back to Cart**|Closes the current form \(that is, the wizard\) and opens the [My Cart](SP_70_00_01.md) \(SP700001\) form, where you can change the item quantities, delete items, or clear the cart.|
|**Return to Shipping Detail**|Returns you to the current page so that you can make any needed changes. This button is available only on the Shipping Information page of the wizard.|

|Element|Description|
|-------|-----------|
|**Location**|The predefined location where you want the items to be delivered. You can select one of the predefined locations or select the **Edit Address** check box and enter another address.|
|**Edit Address**|A check box that you select to override the default contact information.|
|**Account Name**|The legal business name of your company, as registered with the appropriate tax agency. This is the name that will appear on the documents of the company.|
|**Attention**|The attention line as it is used in your company's business letters, which would be used to direct a letter to the intended recipient of a letter. The box may contain something like *Purchase Orders Department* or the recipient’s name.|
|**Phone 1**|The phone number of your company.|
|**Email**|Your company's email address.|
|**Address Line 1**|The first address line of the company's address. The default value is copied from the **Main Address** section of the [Company Profile](SP_40_80_30.md) \(SP408030\) form.|
|**Address Line 2**|The second address line of the company's address. The default value is copied from the **Main Address** section of the [Company Profile](SP_40_80_30.md) \(SP408030\) form.|
|**City**|The city of your company's address.|
|**Country**|The two-letter code representing the country of your company. You can clear the **Edit Address** check box and select a code representing the country from the predefined list.|
|**State**|The state of the company address. You can clear the **Edit Address** check box and select a code representing the state from the predefined list.|
|**Postal Code**|The postal code included in the company’s postal address.|

|Element|Description|
|-------|-----------|
|**Line Total**|The total amount on all lines of the order.|
|**Freight Price**|The freight amount calculated in accordance with the shipping terms.|
|**Tax Total**|The total amount of tax calculated for the order.|
|**Document Discounts**|The sum of the discounts that are calculated for items.|
|**Order Total**|The total amount of the order.|
|**Ship Via**|The list of codes to identify the carriers and services to be used for shipping items. You can select a code representing a carrier from the predefined list.|
|**Delivery Date**|The date of the planned delivery of the shipment. You can specify another delivery date in the calendar.|
|**Residential Delivery**|A check box that you select to indicate that the shipment will be delivered to a residential area.|
|**Use Customer's Account**|A check box that you select to indicate to the system that the carrier specified in the order will charge the customer, not your company, for shipping.|
|**Ship Complete**|The way the order should be fulfilled for this customer at this location. You can use one of the following options: -   *Completely*: The required quantity of all items should be delivered in one shipment.
-   *What's Available*: The ordered quantity of all items should be delivered in one shipment, but if the ordered quantity is not available, the available quantity is shipped and the remaining quantity is canceled.
-   *Available and Back Order*: The ordered quantity can be delivered in multiple shipments. If the ordered quantity is not available, the available quantity is shipped, and the remaining quantity \(open quantity\) can be shipped later. If this option is selected, when the partial quantity has been shipped, the system assigns the order the **Back Order** status.

|
|Comment|Your comments and additional notes to the order.|

## Shipping Information Page {#CheckoutStep2 .section}

On this page, you review and confirm the order information, including the order totals, the shipping information, and the items included in the order.

You open the current page by clicking the **Continue** button on the Shipping Details page of the wizard.

|Button|Description|
|------|-----------|
|**Submit Order**|Submits your order and goes to the Submitted Order page, where you can print or export the order.|
|**Return to Shipping Detail**|Returns you to the Shipping Details page, where you can review \(and correct, if needed\) the shipping information.|

|Element|Description|
|-------|-----------|
|Text box|This box contains the shipping information:-   The shipping address
-   The name of the company
-   The Ship Via code
-   The delivery date

|

|Element|Description|
|-------|-----------|
|**Cart Total**|The total amount on all lines of the order.|
|**Freight**|The freight amount, which has been calculated in accordance with the shipping terms.|
|**Tax Total**|The total amount of tax calculated for the order.|
|**Discount Total**|The sum of the discounts that are calculated for items.|
|**Total**|The total amount of the order.|

|Column|Description|
|------|-----------|
|**Inventory ID**|The unique identifier the system has assigned to the item.|
|**Description**|A brief description of the item.|
|**Qty.**|The quantity of the item in the cart, measured in the unit of measure specified in the **Unit** column.|
|**Price**|The price for a single unit of the specified item.|
|**Unit**|The unit of measure of the item.|
|**Discount Amt.**|The total amount of the discount that has been calculated for the order.|
|**Total**|The total amount of the order.|

## Submitted Order Page {#CheckoutStep3 .section}

On this page, the system displays the submitted sales order as a ready-to-print document. The document provides the company information \(in the top left\), the ship-to and bill-to addresses, the pertinent shipping information, and the itemized information about the items to be shipped.

You can print the sales order or export it to a PDF or to Microsoft Excel.

Also, at any later time, you can review and print the order on the [My Orders](SP_70_00_03.md) \(SP700003\) form.
