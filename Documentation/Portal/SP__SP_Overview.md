# Self-Service Portal Overview {#_414552d7-c156-461b-b064-4119de76c903 .concept}

The Acumatica Self-Service Portal is a site designed to give the employees of your company limited access to Acumatica ERP instance.

By using the Self-Service Portal, you can manage your contact and user information, place orders in Acumatica ERP, view shipments and all the relevant information about your interactions, create and view cases, and view financial reports.

## Your User Profile { .section}

In the Self-Service Portal, your personal information is stored, such as your name, organization, email address, phone number, and postal address.

You can view and edit this information any time you need to. You can also view information about your user account and change your password without contacting the portal system administrator. For details, see [Your User Profile](SP__con_Your_User_Account.md).

## User Interface { .section}

Employees in different positions use different sets of forms in the Self-Service Portal to perform their work tasks.

You can configure the user interface to have at hand the forms you frequently use. You can tailor the workspace menu items in the main menu so that the system displays only menu items related to your work.

For more information, see [Personalizing the Interface](SP__mng_Personalizing_Modern_UI.md).

## Your Company's Account { .section}

On the [Company Profile](SP_40_80_30.md) \(SP408030\) form of the Self-Service Portal, you can update your company's address information, official website, and main contact details. You can also update the attention line to define a person or a department to whom emails or documents of your company are referred. For more information, see [Your Company's Information](SP__con_Company_account.md).

**Attention:** To edit this information, you must be signed in to the Self-Service Portal with a user account that has the *Customer Admin*, *Administrator*, or *Portal Admin* role assigned to it.

## Online Ordering { .section}

In the Self-Service Portal, you can browse the catalog on the [Catalog](SP_70_00_00.md) \(SP700000\) form. The catalog contains the list of items that you can add to your shopping cart. You can search for specific items, filter items by category, and show or hide the items that are not available in stock. You can also view detailed information about any item. If an item is stored in different warehouses, you can select the default warehouse you want the item to be shipped from. On this form, you can also modify the contents of the shopping cart by adding new items, deleting items, and changing the quantities of the added items.

On the [My Cart](SP_70_00_01.md) \(SP700001\) form, you can review the items you plan to order, return to the [Catalog](SP_70_00_00.md) form to continue shopping, or proceed to checkout. You can confirm the order by using the wizard pages on the [Checkout](SP_70_00_02.md) \(SP700002\) form.

After you place an order, you can track the order and the order's shipments by using the [My Orders](SP_70_00_03.md) \(SP700003\) form. You can also cancel the order, if needed.

For more information, see [Managing Online Orders](SP__mng_Orders.md).

## Finance Overview { .section}

On the [My Documents](SP_40_20_00.md) \(SP402000\) form of the Self-Service Portal, you can view your company’s documents, such as invoices, credit memos, and payments. You can also view aging reports.

The visibility of these financial documents depends on the **Display Financial Documents** setting specified on the [Portal Preferences](SP_80_00_00.md) \(SP800000\) form. You can access this form if you are signed in to the Self-Service Portal with a user account that has the *Administrator* or *Portal Admin* role assigned.

Depending on the **Display Financial Documents** option that has been selected, it may be possible to view documents associated with all companies and branches that are available for your company tenant, only a particular branch, or only a particular company.

In the Self-Service Portal, you can print or export these documents to a PDF or to Microsoft Excel.

For more information, see [Your Company's Financial Documents](SP__con_Finance_Overview.md).

## Case Management { .section}

In the Self-Service Portal, you can create support cases with the vendor if these capabilities have been configured in the system. Through cases, you can submit requests, complaints, and questions about items that you have bought or are considering buying. You can also retrieve the latest case updates, add information, attach files, and close or reopen cases.

For more information, see [Case Management](SP__con_Case_Management.md).
