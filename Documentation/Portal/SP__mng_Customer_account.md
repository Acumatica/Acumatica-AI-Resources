# Managing Your Company Account {#_a48241d5-7e83-43a5-9f5c-b46a8f7b97ac .concept}

In the Acumatica Self-Service Portal, you can keep track of your company-related information. Your company is also referred to as a *customer* because you are a customer of the vendor that has provided you with access to the Self-Service Portal.

In the Self-Service Portal, *customer contacts* are the users of your company that have permission to access the system and work in it.

In this chapter, you will find information on managing your company’s contacts and keeping track of the company account, or viewing financial documents associated with your company.

-   **[Your Company's Information](../Portal/SP__con_Company_account.md)**  

-   **[Your Company's Financial Documents](../Portal/SP__con_Finance_Overview.md)**  

-   **[Your Company's Contacts](../Portal/SP__con_Contacts.md)**
