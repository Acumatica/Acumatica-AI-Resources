# To Print an Invoice or Credit Memo {#_8c3afed8-ef4e-4d43-a829-a752754769f4 .task}

In this topic, you will learn how to view and print a financial document—that is, an invoice or a credit memo—of your company in the Self-Service Portal.

## To View and Print Documents { .section}

1.  Open the [My Documents](SP_40_20_00.md) \(SP402000\) form.
2.  In the table, click the row with the invoice or credit memo you want to print, and then click **Print Invoice/Memo** on the form toolbar. A printable version of the document opens.
3.  View the settings of the document, which might include the billing information, shipping address, list of items, and item quantity and price.
4.  On the form toolbar, click **Print** to print the invoice or credit memo.

    **Tip:** You can export the document to a PDF or to Microsoft Excel by clicking **Export** on the form toolbar.
