# Contract {#_18428a93-a9de-445f-89f2-6e233e565686 .reference}

Form ID: \(SP301000\)

On this form, in the Acumatica Self-Service Portal, you can view the details of a particular contract that has been created in Acumatica ERP.

The contract settings are provided for your information only; you cannot edit them. Depending on the contract, you may see some or all of the form elements described below.

**Attention:** Some contracts may not be visible to you.

## Contract Information Area {#1 .section}

In this area, you can select an existing contract and view its primary information.

|Element|Description|
|-------|-----------|
|**Contract ID**|The identifier of a contract in Acumatica ERP. Select a contract ID in this box to view the details of the contract.|
|**Contract Template**|The contract template that this contract was based on. This box appears if your company’s customer account is billed for contract items.|
|**Status**|The status of the contract, which is one of the following options: -   *Draft*: The contract has not been activated and cannot be used.
-   *Pending Approval*: The contract is in the approval process.
-   *Pending Activation*: The contract is set up and needs to be activated so that you can start providing services and initiate recurring billing \(if required\).
-   *Active*: The contract is activated and should be used as the basis for billing.
-   *Expired*: The contract has expired. A renewable contract with this status can be renewed within the grace period \(the period of time after the expiration date\).
-   *Pending Upgrade*: The contract is being prepared for upgrade and can be edited or billed.

|
|**Customer**|The customer associated with the contract.|
|**Location**|The customer location that is specified in the **Location** box of the Customer Contracts \(CT301000\) form in Acumatica ERP.|
|**Description**|The description of the contract, which includes any related comments.|
|**Balance**|The sum of the open invoices associated with the contract. This box appears if the selected customer account is billed for contract services.|

## Summary Tab {#2 .section}

On this tab, you can view the summary settings of the selected contract.

|Element|Description|
|-------|-----------|
|**Setup Date**|The contract setup date.|
|**Activation Date**|The contract activation date. This box appears if the selected customer account is billed for this contract.|
|**Expiration Date**|The date when the contract expires.|
|**Termination Date**|The date when the contract was canceled, if applicable.|
|**Currency**|The base currency used in operations with your company, which you can select from the list of currencies defined in the system.

 **Attention:** This box appears on the form if the *Multicurrency Accounting* feature is enabled on the Enable/Disable \(CS100000\) form in Acumatica ERP.

|
|**Grace Period**|The period of time after the expiration date when services under the contract are provided and a contract may be concluded for a new term.|

|Element|Description|
|-------|-----------|
|**Account**|The specific business account that is to be billed for the current contract.|
|**Location**|Your company's location associated with the contract. This box appears if the customer account is defined to be billed for contract services.|

|Element|Description|
|-------|-----------|
|**Billing Schedule Starts On**|The start date of the first billing period.|
|**Billing Period**|The type of billing schedule, which can be one of the following options: *Week*, *Month*, *Quarter*, *Year*, or *On Demand*. The *On Demand* option means that billing is not scheduled and the contract can be billed on any date.

 The invoice date is calculated based on the selected option and the contract's start date.

|
|**Last Billing Date**|The date when the billing was performed most recently.|
|**Next Billing Date**|The date of the next billing invoice according to the billing schedule.|

## Details Tab {#3 .section}

On this tab, you can view the contract items provided under the contract.

|Element|Description|
|-------|-----------|
|**Effective From**|The date when the contract was set up.|
|**Promo Code**|The code of any discount to be applied.|
|**Pending Setup**|The sum of the prices of the included contract items for setup. The box is displayed if the contract has the *Draft* or *Pending Upgrade* status.|
|**Pending Recurring**|The sum of the prices of the included contract items that are recurring. The box is displayed if the contract has the *Draft* or *Pending Upgrade* status.|
|**Pending Renewal**|The sum of the prices of the included contract items for renewal. The box is displayed for a contract with the *Draft* or *Pending Upgrade* status.|
|**Total Pending**|The total amount of the pending contract items. The box is displayed for a contract with the *Draft* or *Pending Upgrade* status.|
|**Current Setup**|The sum of the prices of the included contract items for setup. The box is populated on contract activation.|
|**Current Recurring**|The sum of prices of the included contract items that are recurring. The box is populated on contract activation.|
|**Current Renewal**|The sum of prices of the included contract items for renewal. The box is populated on contract activation.|

|Column|Description|
|------|-----------|
|**Item Code**|The identifier of the contract item.|
|**Description**|A description of the item.|
|**Included**|The quantity of this contract item that is included in the contract by default.|
|**Difference**|The quantity difference in case of contract upgrade or downgrade. This box appears if the customer account is defined to be billed for contract services.|
|**Setup Price**|The contract item price for setup. This box appears if the customer account is defined to be billed for contract services.|
|**Setup Discount, %**|The discount percent applied to the setup price. This box appears if the customer account is defined to be billed for contract services.|
|**Recurring Price**|The contract item recurring price. This box appears if the customer account is defined to be billed for contract services.|
|**Recurring Discount, %**|The discount percent applied to the recurring price. This box appears if the customer account is defined to be billed for contract services.|
|**Renewal Price**|The contract item price for renewal. This box appears if the customer account is defined to be billed for contract services.|
|**Renewal Discount, %**|The discount percent applied to the renewal price. This box appears if the customer account is defined to be billed for contract services.|

## Recurring Summary Tab {#4 .section}

This tab lists the contract items that have a recurring price configured. The tab appears if the customer account is defined to be billed for contract services.

The table toolbar includes only standard buttons.

|Element|Description|
|-------|-----------|
|**Recurring Total**|The total sum to be billed during the current billing period.|
|**Extra Usage Total**|The total sum to be billed for extra usage during the current billing period.|
|**Total Due**|The total amount to be billed during the current billing period.|

|Column|Description|
|------|-----------|
|**Item Code**|The identifier of the contract item.|
|**Description**|A description of the item.|
|**Inventory ID**|The identifier of the non-stock item a customer is billed for on a recurrent basis.|
|**UOM**|The unit of measure used for billing.|
|**Billing Type**|The item billing policy of the contract item.|
|**Included**|The item quantity that is billed for at the recurring price.|
|**Recurring Price**|The recurring price of the item.|
|**Recurring Discount, %**|The discount percent applied to the recurring price.|
|**Extra Usage Price**|The item quantity that the customer is billed for at the extra usage price.|
|**Unbilled**|The unbilled item quantity.|
|**Used Total**|The item quantity used from contract activation through current business date.|

## Contract History Tab {#5 .section}

The tab displays a list of actions that have been performed during the duration of the contract. This tab appears if the customer account is defined to be billed for contract services.

The table toolbar includes only standard buttons.

|Column|Description|
|------|-----------|
|**Action**|The action that was performed.|
|**Date**|The date when the action was performed.|
|**User**|The user who performed the action.|
|**Related Contract**|The identifier of the related contract, if applicable. A related contract is either one that derived from the current contract or one that served as a source for the current contract.|

## AR History Tab {#7 .section}

The tab, which displays the list of documents related to the contract, appears if the customer account is defined to be billed for contract services.

The table toolbar includes only standard buttons.

|Column|Description|
|------|-----------|
|**Type**|The type of accounts receivable document.|
|**Reference Nbr.**|The reference number of the document.|
|**Post Period**|The financial period to which the document transactions are posted.|
|**Date**|The date of the document.|
|**Due Date**|The due date of the document.|
|**Status**|The status of the document.|
|**Amount**|The amount of the document.|
|**Balance**|The balance of the invoice, if it has been partially paid.|
|**Payment Method**|The default payment method for the customer.|
