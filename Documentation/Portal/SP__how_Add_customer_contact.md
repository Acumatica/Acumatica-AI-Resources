# To Create a Customer Contact {#_448c5a96-9209-4d50-90a5-36c5dc227203 .task}

Acting as an employee of the company that uses the Acumatica ERP instance, in the Acumatica Self-Service Portal, you can create customer contacts for business accounts of your customers \(customer accounts\).

*Customer accounts* are business accounts of companies that can access the Self-Service Portal. *Customer contacts* are the employees of your company who have permission to access to the Self-Service Portal and work in it.

In the Self-Service Portal, you can use the [Customer Account](SP_40_80_32.md) \(SP408032\) form to create a customer contact and link it to a business account.

**Attention:** You can create a customer contact by signing in to the Self-Service Portal instance with a user account that has the *Administrator* role assigned.

## To Create a Customer Contact { .section}

1.  In the Self-Service Portal, open the [Customer Accounts](SP_40_80_33.md) \(SP408033\) form.
2.  In the **Business Account** column of the table, click the business account identifier, which customer contact you want to create. The [Customer Account](SP_40_80_32.md) \(SP408032\) form opens.
3.  On the form toolbar, click **Create Contact**. The **Create Contact** dialog box opens.
4.  On the **Main** tab, enter the contact information of the customer contact, which can be contact's name, job title, phone number, email, or contact class.
5.  On the **Attributes** tab, enter the attributes, which are defined in the class you selected for the customer contact in the **Contact Class** box of the **Main** tab.
6.  Click **Create**.
