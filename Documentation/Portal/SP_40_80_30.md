# Company Profile {#_011b7c5f-db5a-466c-9155-c0066df5286d .reference}

Form ID: \(SP408030\)

On this form, you can check and update your company's contact information, such as the official website, phone number, and address.

**Attention:** To edit the information on this form, you must be signed in to the Self-Service Portal instance with a user account that has any of the following roles assigned to it: *Customer Admin*, *Administrator*, or *Portal Admin*.

## Form Toolbar { .section}

The form toolbar includes only standard buttons.

## Summary Area { .section}

In this area, you can specify or modify the basic settings of your company.

|Element|Description|
|-------|-----------|
|**Account Name**|The legal business name of your company, as registered with the appropriate tax agency. This is the name that appears on the documents of the company.|
|**Attention**|The attention line as it is used in your company's business letters, which would be used to direct mail to the intended recipient. The box may contain something like *Purchase Orders Department* or the recipient’s name.|
|**Email**|Your company's email address.|
|**Web**|The URL of your company's official website.|
|**Phone 1**|The primary phone number of your company.|
|**Phone 2**|The secondary phone number of your company.|
|**Fax**|The fax number of your company.|

|Element|Description|
|-------|-----------|
|**Address Line 1**|The first address line of your company.|
|**Address Line 2**|The second address line of your company.|
|**City**|The city of your company.|
|**Country**|The two-letter code representing the country of your company. You can select a code from the predefined list.|
|**State**|The two-letter code representing the state of your company’s address. You can select a code from the predefined list.|
|**Postal Code**|The postal code included in your company's address.|
