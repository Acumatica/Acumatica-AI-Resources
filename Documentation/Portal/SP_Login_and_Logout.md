# Signing In and Signing Out {#_76f30993-381d-4088-99c3-65b648d915fe .concept}

This article describes how to start and finish using the Self-Service Portal.

## Signing In { .section}

To start using the Self-Service Portal, you need to sign in. To do so, perform these steps:

1.  Open your browser.
2.  In the Address bar, type the URL of the Self-Service Portal instance and press Enter. This opens the Sign-In page.
3.  In the **Username** and **Password** boxes, type your username and password, respectively.
4.  Optional: In the **Interface Language** box, select the interface language you want to use.
5.  Click **Sign In**.

## Forgotten Password or Username { .section}

If you have forgotten your password, follow these instructions to recover it:

1.  On the Sign-In page, click **Forgot your password or username?**.
2.  On the **Password recovery** screen, type your username in the **Enter your username** box.
3.  Click **Submit**.
4.  Check your email for further instructions.
5.  In the email, click the password recovery link.

    This opens the Sign-In page for changing your password.

6.  In the **New Password** and **Confirm Password** boxes, type your new password.
7.  Click **Sign In**.

If you have forgotten your username, follow these steps to recover it:

1.  On the Sign-In page, click **Forgot your password or username?**.
2.  On the**Password recovery** screen, click **Forgot your username?**.
3.  On the **User recovery** screen, type your email address in the **Enter your email address** box.
4.  Click **Submit**.
5.  Check your email for an email that contains your username.

## Signing Out {#section_t1h_2w3_f3 .section}

When you have finished using the Self-Service Portal, remember to sign out to prevent unauthorized users from tampering with the system or your account.

**Note:** If you are inactive for more than one hour, you will be automatically signed out.

To sign out of the system, do the following:

-   In the info area \(in the upper-right corner of the screen\), click your username and click **Sign Out**.
