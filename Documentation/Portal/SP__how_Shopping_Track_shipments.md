# To Track a Shipment {#_5f0f24e6-063f-4edf-96fb-1e83c0173a69 .task}

In this topic, you will learn how to track a shipment in the Acumatica Self-Service Portal. You will also print a shipment confirmation.

## To Track a Shipment { .section}

1.  In the Self-Service Portal, open the [My Orders](SP_70_00_03.md) \(SP700003\) form.

    All the orders placed with your company account are displayed in the table.

2.  Click the row with the necessary order to select it, and then click **View Shipments** to see all shipments associated with the order. The **Shipments** dialog box opens.
3.  In the dialog box, review the shipment’s number, status, date, and location.
4.  In the **Shipment Nbr.** column, click the shipment identifier. On the [Shipment Confirmation](../UserGuide/SO_64_20_00.md) \(SO642000\) form, the printable version of the shipment confirmation opens.
5.  Review the main shipment information, such as the ship-to address, the warehouse from which items are shipped, and the itemized information about the quantities of the items shipped.
6.  On the form toolbar, click **Print** to print the shipment confirmation.

    You can export the shipment confirmation to a PDF or to Microsoft Excel by clicking **Export** on the form toolbar.
