# To Edit Your Company's Account Information {#_81e75963-ee8b-48b0-837f-3aa014e9cb84 .task}

In this topic, you will learn how to view and update your company's address and main contact settings in the Self-Service Portal.

**Attention:** You can edit the company’s account information by signing in to the Self-Service Portal instance with a user account that has the *Customer Admin*, *Administrator*, or *Portal Admin* role assigned.

For more information, see [Your Company's Information](SP__con_Company_account.md).

## To Edit Your Company's Information { .section}

1.  Open the [Company Profile](SP_40_80_30.md) \(SP408030\) form. In the **Main Contact** section, check the contact details of your company \(such as the official website, phone number, and main address\), and make any needed updates.
2.  In the **Attention** box, type the person or department who should receive emails and documents from your company.
3.  In the **Main Address** section, check the address of your company, and make updates, if needed.
4.  On the form toolbar, click **Save**.
