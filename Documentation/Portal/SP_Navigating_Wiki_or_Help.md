# Self-Service Portal Documentation {#_8110309c-f219-4490-a82c-4ff39694d887 .concept}

You can find helpful information in the **Documentation** section of the Self-Service Portal. All the articles are organized in wikis. The Self-Service Portal wiki includes the basic information on using the Self-Service Portal, and the other wikis contain other useful information.

## Wiki Overview { .section}

Any time you want to, you can refer to the online product documentation supplied with the Self-Service Portal. These materials can be found in the **Documentation** section of the application.

You can locate a wiki article in one of the following ways:

-   *By getting the reference article that describes the form you are viewing*: On the form toolbar of the active form, click **Help**.
-   *By searching in wikis*: In the navigation pane, in the **Search** box, type a word, part of a word, or a phrase; then click **Search**. You will see the search results in the [Search Form](SE_00_00_50.md) form; click an article you want to view. For more information, see [Search](SP_Search_Options_and_the_Search_Form.md).

## Knowledge Base Overview { .section}

The wikis may contain the information your vendor makes available for you, such as product specifications, surveys, and knowledge base \(KB\) articles. You can rate the articles and submit feedback if the wiki permits feedback. For more information about KB articles, see [Knowledge Base](SP_KB_Knowledge_Base.md).
