# To Change the Contents of the Cart {#_c96ab622-f229-4d25-847d-8bf2144787a2 .task}

In this topic, you will learn how to make changes in your shopping cart in the Acumatica Self-Service Portal.

**Attention:** You can view the [My Cart](SP_70_00_01.md) \(SP700001\) form if you are signed in to the Self-Service Portal with a user account that has the *Customer Admin* or *Customer User* role assigned.

## To Make Changes in the Cart { .section}

1.  In the Self-Service Portal, open the [My Cart](SP_70_00_01.md) \(SP700001\) form.

    The items that you have added to your cart are displayed in the table.

2.  To change the contents of the shopping cart, do any of the following:
    -   To change the quantity of an item in the cart, in the **Qty.** column, double-click the quantity of the selected item, type the new quantity, and then press Enter.
    -   To delete an obsolete item, select its row and click **Delete** on the table toolbar.
    -   To add new items, click **Continue Shopping** on the form toolbar.

        The [Catalog](SP_70_00_00.md) \(SP700000\) form opens. Now you can add new items to the shopping cart. For more information, see [To Add Items to the Shopping Cart](SP__how_Shopping_Add_to_Cart.md).


You have changed the contents in the shopping cart. Now you can proceed to checkout and place an order in the Self-Service Portal. For more information, see [Placing Orders](SP__con_Shopping_Orders.md).
