# To Personalize the Quick Menu {#_9b5146e6-3ff7-4a10-886a-ed56e530f194 .task}

In this topic, you will learn how to personalize the list of forms that are displayed on the quick menu of any workspace. The changes you make are effective for only your user account.

## To Configure the Quick Menu Items { .section}

To personalize the quick menu in the Self-Service Portal, do the following:

1.  Open the workspace whose quick menu you want to configure.
2.  On the workspace title bar, click **Configure Quick Menu** \(![](Images/Workspace_Toolbar_Configure.png)\) to switch to **Configuration** mode.
3.  For each form that you want to include in the quick menu, select the check box left of its title.
4.  For each currently included form that you want to exclude from the quick menu, clear the check box left of its title.
5.  On the workspace toolbar, click **Exit** to apply changes and exit the configuration mode.
