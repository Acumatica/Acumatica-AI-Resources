# Your User Profile {#_2415847a-eac4-4df0-9bd3-a6ad24de630d .concept}

In this topic, you will read about managing your personal information in the Acumatica Self-Service Portal.

## Viewing Your Personal Information { .section}

On the **Details** tab of the [User Profile](SP_40_80_45.md) \(SP408045\) form, you can view your personal information, such as your name, position in the company, email address, phone and fax numbers, and postal address.

**Tip:** You can open this form by doing either of the following:

-   Clicking your username in the Info area \(in the top right corner of the Self-Service Portal screen\) and then clicking **My Profile** \(for users with any role\).
-   Clicking the [User Profile](SP_40_80_45.md) form in the workspace or using the search.

An employee of your vendor's company or your company has initially entered the settings on this form. However, if any of your personal data changes, you can edit this information on your own.

To populate boxes with the default address data of your company, in the **Address** section of the **Details** tab, select the **Same as in Account** check box. The system fills in the address boxes with the data that is entered in the **Main Address** section of the [Company Profile](SP_40_80_30.md) \(SP408030\) form.

## Viewing and Changing Your User Account Settings { .section}

To access the Self-Service Portal, you use your user account, which has been created by an employee who has the *Customer Admin*, *Administrator*, or *Portal Admin* role assigned.

Your user account contains the following main settings: your username, your password, your user type, your default time zone, and personal settings.

On the **User Info** tab of the [User Profile](SP_40_80_45.md) \(SP408045\) form, you can change your user account information without contacting the portal system administrator as follows:

-   You can change your password or reset it. For details, see [To Change Your Password](SP__how_Change_Password.md).
-   You can generate a new password.
-   You can change your default time zone.
