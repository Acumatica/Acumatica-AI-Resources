# My Statements {#_584515a6-db19-4563-ad6f-b9b1ee53a519 .reference}

Form ID: \(SP404600\)

On this form of the Acumatica Self-Service Portal, you can view the statement history of your company’s customer account, get new statements, and print statements.

## Form Toolbar { .section}

The form toolbar includes standard and form-specific buttons. The form-specific buttons are listed below.

|Button|Description|
|------|-----------|
|**Print Statement**|Opens the statement you have selected \(by clicking the row\) as a print-friendly document.

 The document shows the vendor's and your company's contact information \(in the top left\), due dates and amounts, and the documents based on which the amounts included in the statement have arisen.

 You can print the statement or export it to a PDF or to Microsoft Excel.

|

## Selection Area {#1 .section}

You can use the elements in this area to specify the criteria of the statements you want to view.

|Element|Description|
|-------|-----------|
|**From Date**|The start date of the date range.|
|**To Date**|The end date of the date range.|

## Table {#2 .section}

This table contains the list of the company’s statements.

|Column|Description|
|------|-----------|
|**Statement Date**|The date of the statement.|
|**Statement Balance**|The balance as shown on the statement.|
|**Overdue Balance**|The overdue amount on a certain date, which you specified in the **To Date** box of the current form.|
|**Branch**|The branch associated with the statement.|
|**Currency**|The currency of the statement.

 **Attention:** This column appears on the form only if the *Multicurrency Accounting* feature is enabled on the Enable/Disable \(CS100000\) form in Acumatica ERP.

|
|**Currency Statement Balance**|The balance of the statement in the specified currency.

 **Attention:** This column appears on the form only if the *Multicurrency Accounting* feature is enabled on the Enable/Disable \(CS100000\) form in Acumatica ERP.

|
|**Currency Overdue Balance**|The overdue balance of the customer’s statement in the specified currency.

 **Attention:** This column appears on the form only if the *Multicurrency Accounting* feature is enabled on the Enable/Disable \(CS100000\) form in Acumatica ERP.

|
