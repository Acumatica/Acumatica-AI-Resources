# Labor Codes

Form ID: \(AM206500\)

Labor codes specify the labor credit GL accounts for direct labor and the credit and overhead accounts for indirect labor. Labor codes are used for labor reporting on the [Labor](AM_30_10_00.md) \(AM301000\) form and are defaulted from the code specified on the [Work Centers](AM_20_70_00.md) \(AM207000\) form. Labor can also be backflushed automatically when reporting operations. You must define at least one labor code with a type of *Direct* to support labor reporting and backflushing. Additional codes can be created for different credit and overhead accounts.
