# Tax Preferences

Form ID: \(TX103000\)

On this form, you can define the tax preferences to be used in the tax subledger.

If the *Net/Gross Entry Mode* feature has been enabled on the [Enable/Disable Features](CS_10_00_00.md) \(CS100000\) form, you need to specify the accounts and subaccounts \(if applicable\) where the amounts related to tax rounding gains and losses will be posted; that is, the boxes in the **Rounding Settings** section are mandatory.

## Form Toolbar

The form toolbar has only standard buttons.

## Rounding Settings Section

In this section, you specify the accounts and subaccounts to which the system will post the amounts of the tax rounding gains or losses.

If either the *Invoice Rounding* feature or the *Net/Gross Entry Mode* feature has been enabled \(or both features have been enabled\) on the [Enable/Disable Features](CS_10_00_00.md) \(CS100000\) form, causing the **Rounding Limit** box to appear on the [General Ledger Preferences](GL_10_20_00.md) \(GL102000\) form, the system will take into account the limit defined in this box when it posts the tax rounding gains or losses. Documents that exceed this limit will not be released.
