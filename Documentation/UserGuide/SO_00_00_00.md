# Order Management {#_f15b4416-7670-4988-954e-7460a8051671 .concept}

With the order management functionality, you can manage activities related to sales and purchases, such as entering and processing sales orders of various types, generating pick lists and shipment confirmations, creating shipments, processing drop shipments, performing customer and vendor returns, and receiving purchased items to inventory. You can configure order types to maintain your sales and purchase workflows, set up integration with nationwide carriers, configure freight calculation and landed costs, and process requests and requisitions. These capabilities can help you to easily maintain and efficiently carry out the specific business processes of your company.
