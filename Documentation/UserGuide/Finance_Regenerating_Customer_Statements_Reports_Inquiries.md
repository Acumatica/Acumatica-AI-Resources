# Regeneration of Statements: Related Report and Inquiry Forms {#_3d9a545f-24de-4584-8335-57bf1e2d722c .concept}

In the following sections, you can find details about the reports and inquiry forms you may want to review to gather information about customer statements.

**Attention:** If you don’t see a particular report or form that is described, you may have signed in to the system with a user account that doesn’t have access rights to the report or form. Contact your system administrator to obtain access to any needed reports or forms.

## Viewing Statements {#section_hpd_hjv_vxb .section}

You can preview a statement that you have printed or emailed by using the [Print Statements](AR_50_35_00.md) \(AR503500\) form.

Also, Acumatica ERP provides the following inquiries, which display detailed statement histories:

-   By statement cycle: [Statement History Summary](AR_40_40_00.md) \(AR404000\)
-   By customer: [Customer Statement History](AR_40_46_00.md) \(AR404600\)

## Printing Customer Statements {#section_kpd_hjv_vxb .section}

To prepare a printable form of the customer statement, you use the [Customer Statement](AR_64_15_00.md) \(AR641500\) report \(for local customers\) or the [Customer Statement MC](AR_64_20_00.md) \(AR642000\) report \(for foreign customers\). You can modify these reports to format the statements to better meet your needs.
