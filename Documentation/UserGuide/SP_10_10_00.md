# My Profile

Form ID: \(SP101000\)

**Who uses this form:** An authorized portal user of a company using the Modern Customer Portal.

A user with any role can open this form by clicking their username in the top right corner of the Modern Customer Portal screen and then clicking **My Profile**.

The system inserts the information it copies from the [Contacts](CR_30_20_00.md) \(CR302000\) form of Acumatica ERP by default. Any changes you make to your own profile are reflected on the [Contacts](CR_30_20_00.md) form of Acumatica ERP and on the [Company Contact](SP_20_10_20.md) \(SP201020\) form of the portal when you save these changes, and the reverse is also true.

On this form, you can:

-   View or edit your contact information, including your name, email address, address, phone number, and password.
-   Specify your time zone and the form you will see as the landing page when you sign in to the portal.

This form is available only if both of the following conditions are met for the portal owner:

-   The *Modern Customer Portal* feature is enabled on the [Enable/Disable Features](CS_10_00_00.md) \(CS100000\) form.
-   The portal has the **System** check box selected on the **General** tab of the [Portals](SP_70_10_00.md) \(SP701000\) form.
