# To Use Products in Wikis {#_ae15f234-f540-46a8-8aeb-3617bb02ba80 .task}

In wikis, you can group articles based on the *product* the articles apply to. When you create a new article, you can apply it to one product or multiple products, based on the article content. When you perform a search, you can select the product that the articles you're seeking apply to.

You can use the [Wiki Products](SM_20_05_24.md) \(SM200524\) form to define products for wiki articles and manage the articles that apply to a product.

## To Add a New Product { .section}

You can add product by using the [Wiki Products](SM_20_05_24.md) form as follows:

1.  On the **Configuration** tab, click **Document Management**. In the left pane, click **Configure** &gt; **Wiki Products**.
2.  In the **Product ID** box of the Summary area, type a short name for the new product.
3.  In the **Product Name** box, type a descriptive name for the new product.
4.  On the form toolbar, click **Save**.

## To Apply Products to Articles { .section}

You can assign articles to a particular product on the [Wiki Products](SM_20_05_24.md) form as follows:

1.  On the **Configuration** tab, click **Document Management**. In the left pane, click **Configure** &gt; **Wiki Articles By Product**.
2.  In the **Product ID** box of the Summary area, select the product.
3.  On the table toolbar, click **Add Row**.
4.  In the **Article ID** box, select an article that applies to the product.
5.  Repeat Steps 3 and 4 for every article that applies to the product.
6.  On the form toolbar, click **Save**.

You can also select products that a particular article that you're editing applies to. You do this by using the [Wiki Editor Form for Articles](DM__con_Wiki_Wiki_Editor_Form_for_Articles.md) as follows:

1.  Open the article.
2.  On the help toolbar, click **Edit Current Article** to open the article for editing.
3.  On the **Product** tab, click **Add Row** on the table toolbar.
4.  In the **Product ID** column, select the product.
5.  Repeat Steps 3 and 4 for every product the article applies to.
6.  On the form toolbar, click **Save**.
