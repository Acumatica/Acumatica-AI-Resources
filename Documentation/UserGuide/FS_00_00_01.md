# Service Management {#_cf87c184-096f-4688-99f5-ad15dfed99be .concept}

The service management functional area includes basic functionality that you can use to manage service-related activities, such as entering service orders of various types, creating the appointments of service orders, fulfilling appointments and service orders, and generating invoices for customers.

This part of the Field Services Guide contains all the information you need to configure and use the service management functionality.
