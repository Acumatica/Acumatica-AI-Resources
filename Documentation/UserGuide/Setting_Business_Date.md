# Your Working Environment: To Change the Business Date {#_6dfced5c-a970-4d22-880e-da44c463236f .task}

The business date is the date that appears by default on Acumatica ERP forms and on records that you create by using the forms.

By default, all users can change the business date in the system. If the *Secure Business Date* feature is enabled on the [Enable/Disable Features](CS_10_00_00.md) \(CS100000\) form, only users with the *BusinessDateOverride* role assigned to them can change the business date. You can change the business date, as described in this topic, if one of the following is true:

-   The *Secure Business Date* feature is disabled.
-   The feature is enabled and the *BusinessDateOverride* role is assigned to you.

## To Change the Business Date { .section}

1.  In the top pane, in the upper-right corner of the Acumatica ERP screen, click the Business Date menu button.
2.  In the **Business Date** box, type the date or click the arrow to select the date by using the calendar.
3.  Click **OK**.
