# Record Audit History

On this form, you can see the audit trail of a record you’ve selected on a specific form. The form title includes the following:

-   The name of the source form—that is, the form from which you opened the current form.
-   Key values of the record for which information is displayed. For example, it might show the specific record or document being audited, its type, and its ID or reference number.

This form is available if the source form has auditing enabled for it—that is, if the **Active** check box is selected for it on the [Audit](SM_20_55_10.md) \(SM205510\) form.

You open the current form by clicking **Settings** &gt; **Audit History** on the form title bar of the source form.

**Tip:** You assign access rights for this form on the [Access Rights by Screen](SM_20_10_20.md) \(SM201020\) form.
