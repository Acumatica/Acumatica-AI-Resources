# To Suspend Asset Depreciation {#_cb428601-8f59-43a6-850a-28b84f3e5902 .task}

You can suspend the depreciation of an asset by using the [Fixed Assets](FA_30_30_00.md) \(FA303000\) form.

**Attention:** You can suspend the depreciation of an active asset—that is, an asset with the *Active* status.

## To Suspend Asset Depreciation {#section_srf_ljv_vxb .section}

1.  Open the [Fixed Assets](FA_30_30_00.md) \(FA303000\) form.
2.  In the **Asset ID** box, select the fixed asset whose depreciation you want to suspend.
3.  On the More menu \(under **Processing**\), click **Suspend**.

    Alternatively, on the **Balance** tab, you can click **Suspend** on the table toolbar.


The depreciation of the selected asset is suspended starting on the current business date.
