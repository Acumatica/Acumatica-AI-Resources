# To Specify a Consolidation Account {#_0c99d830-e7ab-4486-84ac-e940784a71a1 .task}

You use the [Chart of Accounts](GL_20_25_00.md) \(GL202500\) form to specify a consolidation account—that is, an account of a parent company to which data will be imported during consolidation from a particular account of a consolidation unit.

**Attention:** To open any form, you can navigate to it or search for it \(by its name or by its form ID without periods\).

## To Specify a Consolidation Account {#section_ubf_mjv_vxb .section}

1.  Open the [Chart of Accounts](../Shared/../UserGuide/GL_20_25_00.md) \(GL202500\) form.
2.  For each account for which you want to specify a consolidation account, in the **Consolidation Account** column, select an account of the parent company to which data will be imported.

    **Note:** The *YTD Net Income* account cannot be specified as a consolidation account.

3.  Click **Save** on the form toolbar.
