# To Print an Article {#_feb4afb6-5783-46b3-9611-1fc4db5f4077 .task}

To print any wiki article, do the following:

1.  Open the article you want to print.
2.  On the wiki toolbar, click **Print**.
3.  Follow the necessary steps, depending on your browser, to print the article.
