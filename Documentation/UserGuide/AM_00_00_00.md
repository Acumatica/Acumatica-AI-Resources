# Manufacturing Edition {#_4e3d7ed6-5400-4c1f-bd8f-e3caa241b4b4 .concept}

The Manufacturing Edition of Acumatica ERP delivers powerful and comprehensive functionality for make-to-stock, make-to-order, engineer-to-order, project-centric, job shop, batch, and repetitive manufacturing companies. It is an easy-to-use and powerful solution designed to be used by a wide range of organizations with simple to advanced business flows and reporting needs.

In this guide, you will find information about the implementation and usage of Acumatica ERP Manufacturing Edition.
