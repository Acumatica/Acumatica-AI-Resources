# Weekly Crew Time Entry

Form ID: \(EP307100\)

On this form, you can create a time entry for a selected workgroup \(that is, crew\) and, if needed, associate this entry with a project, project task, and particular day of the week. Each time entry created on this form spans a particular week.

This form is available only if the *Time Management* feature is enabled on the [Enable/Disable Features](CS_10_00_00.md) \(CS100000\) form.

## Form Toolbar

The form toolbar includes the buttons described below.

|Command|Description|
|-------|-----------|
|**Complete Activities**|Completes activities—that is, changes their status to *Completed*.|

## Time Activities Tab

On this tab, you can click **Add Row** to create a time activity for a particular member of the workgroup or click **Mass Enter Time** to open the **Mass Enter Time** dialog box, where you can enter time for the crew \(that is, create multiple identical time activities for members of the selected workgroup\). You can also copy the selected time activities or load them from the previous week.

You can review the existing time activities created by the crew members during the selected week. The system shows time activities if their **Reported On** date—the date when the time activity was reported in the original time zone—falls within the specified week.

|Button|Description|
|------|-----------|
|**Mass Enter Time**|Opens the [Mass Enter Time](#_d6094135-c835-45c0-b878-4a651e2378e5) dialog box, where you can enter time for the crew.|
|**Copy Selected Line**|Copies the selected time activity and inserts it in the table.|
|**Load Activities from Previous Week**|Copies the time activities created by crew members for the previous week and inserts them in the table.|

## Crew Members Tab

On this tab, you can review the reported work hours of the crew members, including ad hoc members, broken down by the days of the week.

|Element|Description|
|-------|-----------|
|**Workgroup Members**|The total number of the crew members, including ad hoc members.|
|**Members with Activities**|The total number of the crew members who have time activities reported for the selected week.|

|Button|Description|
|------|-----------|
|**Load Workgroup from Previous Week**|Copies crew members from the previous week and inserts them in the table.|

|Column|Description|
|------|-----------|
|**Employee**|The ID of the employee who is a member of the crew.|
|**Employee Name**|The name of the employee who is a member of the crew.|
|**Status**|The status of the crew member, which can be one of the following: *Permanent*, *Temporary*, or *Ad Hoc*.|
|**Monday**|The work time reported for Monday, including overtime.|
|**Tuesday**|The work time reported for Tuesday, including overtime.|
|**Wednesday**|The work time reported for Wednesday, including overtime.|
|**Thursday**|The work time reported for Thursday, including overtime.|
|**Friday**|The work time reported for Friday, including overtime.|
|**Saturday**|The work time reported for Saturday, including overtime.|
|**Sunday**|The work time reported for Sunday, including overtime.|
|**Total Regular Time**|The total regular work hours spent by the employee on the time activities throughout the week.|
|**Total Billable Time**|The total billable work hours spent by the employee on the time activities throughout the week.|
|**Total Overtime**|The overtime spent by the employee on the time activities throughout the week.|
|**Total Billable Overtime**|The billable overtime spent by the employee on the time activities throughout the week.|

|Element|Description|
|-------|-----------|
|**Time Spent**|The total overtime spent by the members of the crew on the time activities listed on the **Time Activities** tab.|
