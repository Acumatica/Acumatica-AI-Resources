# Send and Receive Email {#_96cbe1ef-0e99-49b6-b5ae-863b560b3722 .reference}

Form ID: \(SM507010\)

You can use this form to control email the sending and receiving of emails. By using the **Schedules** menu on the form toolbar, you can also schedule the automatic execution of these operations for emails in queues.

## Form Toolbar { .section}

The form toolbar includes the buttons described below.

|Button|Description|
|------|-----------|
|**Process**|Executes the operation selected in the **Action** box for the email accounts you have selected.|
|**Process All**|Executes the selected operation for all email accounts.|

## Selection Area {#1 .section}

In this area, select the operation you want to execute.

|Element|Description|
|-------|-----------|
|**Action**|The operation you want to execute when you click **Process** or **Process All**.

 The following options are available:

 -   *Receive All*: Initiates the receipt of all incoming emails for the selected email accounts. Depending on the **Archive Emails** setting specified on the [Email Preferences](SM_20_40_01.md) \(SM204001\) form, this action may initiate archiving of incoming emails for the selected email accounts.

This option is not applicable to email accounts of the *Email Service Plug-In* type or those with no server specified in the **Incoming Mail Server** box on the **Service** tab of the [Email Accounts](SM_20_40_02.md) \(SM204002\) form.

-   *Send All*: Initiates the sending of all outgoing emails for the selected email accounts. Depending on the **Archive Emails** setting specified on the [Email Preferences](SM_20_40_01.md) \(SM204001\) form, this action may initiate archiving of sent emails for the selected email accounts.
-   *Send/Receive All*: Initiates the sending and receipt of all emails for the selected email accounts. Depending on the **Archive Emails** setting specified on the [Email Preferences](SM_20_40_01.md) \(SM204001\) form, this action may initiate archiving of both incoming and sent emails for the selected email accounts.

This option functions as *Send All* for email accounts of the *Email Service Plug-In* type or those with no server specified in the **Incoming Mail Server** box on the **Service** tab of the [Email Accounts](SM_20_40_02.md) \(SM204002\) form.


|

## Table {#2 .section}

This table lists the system email accounts. You can select the accounts you want to include in processing.

The table toolbar has only standard buttons.

|Column|Description|
|------|-----------|
|Included|A check box you select to include the email account in processing when you click **Process**.|
|**Email Account ID**|The name of the email account. Click the account name to open the selected account on the [Email Accounts](SM_20_40_02.md) \(SM204002\) form in a new browser window for editing.|
|**Email Address**|The email address used to receive and send emails.|
|**Inbox**|The number of incoming emails in the queue.|
|**Last Receive Date**|The date when the receiving operation was last performed.|
|**Last Send Date**|The date when the sending operation was last performed.|
|**Outbox**|The number of outgoing emails in the queue.|
