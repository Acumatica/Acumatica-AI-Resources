# Generate Payments

Form ID: \(AR511000\)

You can use this form to list AR documents, such as invoices and credit memos that are overdue or have due dates approaching, and then to generate payments for all listed documents or only those you have selected. This form displays documents whose payment method has the *Credit Card* means of payment. For details, see [Card Payments](AR__con_Card_Payments.md).

**Attention:** This form appears in the system if the *Integrated Card Processing* feature has been enabled on the [Enable/Disable Features](../Shared/../UserGuide/CS_10_00_00.md) \(CS100000\) form.

## Form Toolbar

The form toolbar includes the buttons described below.

|Button|Description|
|------|-----------|
|**Process**|Generates payments for the documents that you have selected in the table.|
|**Process All**|Generates payments for all documents listed in the table.|

## Table

This table contains the list of documents matching the criteria you have specified in the Selection area, along with the relevant details of each document. The system loads the documents of customers whose settings permit the use of the current branch, that is those for which the **Restrict Visibility To** box on the **Financial** tab of the [Customers](AR_30_30_00.md) \(AR303000\) form contains the current branch, company \(or a company within a company group\), or is empty.

If a credit memo appears in the table and is processed when you click **Process** or **Process All**, the credit memo is applied to a payment with a cash discount if the credit memo has a cash discount balance and the payment date is within the cash discount period.
