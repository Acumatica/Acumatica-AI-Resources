# Cash Entries: Adding Taxes {#_d4f0cd76-6a71-4b4c-8f00-ee37b4be5534 .concept}

On the [Cash Transactions](CA_30_40_00.md) \(CA304000\) form, taxes are applied to the cash entry based on the tax category of the items on the **Details** tab and the tax zone on the **Financial** tab. You can specify the default tax zone for a particular entry type on the [Cash Accounts](CA_20_20_00.md) \(CA202000\) form, and the default tax categories can be specified for non-stock items used for cash transactions; these default settings can be overridden for a particular cash entry. You can view and edit the applied taxes on the **Taxes** tab of the [Cash Transactions](CA_30_40_00.md) form.

For details on processing cash entries with a sales tax applied, see [Processing Cash Entries with Taxes](Taxes_Applying_SalesTax_to_CashEntry_Mapref.md). Also, see [Tax Zones and Categories: General Information](../ImplementationGuide/TaxZones_and_Categories_GeneralInfo.md) for more information on tax zones and tax categories.
