# Processing Customer Returns for Credit with Receipts {#_fb3532bb-b4f9-4e14-8ed8-7e770d27a228 .concept}

Acumatica ERP supports the basic types of customer returns of items your company has sold. This includes returns in which you receive the item or items to inventory and issue a credit memo to adjust the customer's balance in the system. The topics of this chapter explain how to process customer returns of items with returned items being received to inventory and a credit memo being prepared for the customer.
