# Company Contact

Form ID: \(SP201020\)

**Who uses this form:** An authorized portal users of the Modern Customer Portal or Vendor Portal.

On this form, you can create a contact—a record for an employee of your company who will use the portal—and grant this contact portal access. You open this form by clicking **Add New Contact** on the [Company Profile](SP_20_10_00.md) \(SP201000\) form.

Each of the Modern Customer Portal and Vendor Portal uses the same contact and company \(business account\) records as Acumatica ERP, but each portal is synchronized with Acumatica ERP independently. Any changes you make on the current form are reflected on the [Contacts](CR_30_20_00.md) \(CR302000\) form of Acumatica ERP, and the reverse is also true.

This form is available only if both of the following conditions are met for the portal owner:

-   The *Modern Customer Portal* or *Vendor Portal* feature is enabled on the [Enable/Disable Features](CS_10_00_00.md) \(CS100000\) form.
-   The **System** check box is selected on the **General** tab of the [Portals](SP_70_10_00.md) \(SP701000\) form.
