# Reclassifying Project-Related AP Bills

If a project-related accounts payable bill has been released with incorrect project, account, or commitment information, you can reclassify the bill to correct the GL account, subaccount, project, project task, cost code, or commitment specified in a bill line.

In this chapter, you will find general information about reclassifying project-related AP bills, an implementation checklist, reports and inquiries that can help you find and review the related transactions, and descriptions of the GL and project transactions generated during bill reclassification.
