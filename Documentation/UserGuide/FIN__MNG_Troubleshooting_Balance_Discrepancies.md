# Troubleshooting Balance Discrepancies {#_e51994ab-dcea-4220-9c1b-3938cfa4cb0c .concept}

Discrepancies in balances can occur because of errors and inconsistencies in transactions. Inconsistencies might occur if, for example, a GL entry has been posted directly to an AR account instead of a correct account, or the details of an AR invoice contain an AR account instead of an income account. These inconsistencies can result in different balances in AR, AP, and GL reports.

To quickly find these types of discrepancies in accounts, you use the following forms:

-   [Discrepancies by Account](AP_40_90_10.md) \(AP409010\)
-   [Discrepancies by Vendor](AP_40_90_20.md) \(AP409020\)
-   [Discrepancies by Document](AP_40_90_30.md) \(AP409030\)
-   [Discrepancies by Account](AR_40_90_10.md) \(AR409010\)
-   [Discrepancies by Customer](AR_40_90_20.md) \(AR409020\)
-   [Discrepancies by Document](AR_40_90_30.md) \(AR409030\)

You can run any of these inquiries separately. Alternatively, you can start by viewing discrepancies in accounts on the [Discrepancies by Account](AP_40_90_10.md) or [Discrepancies by Account](AR_40_90_10.md) form; you then click the needed links in the **Discrepancy** column to open the [Discrepancies by Vendor](AP_40_90_20.md) or [Discrepancies by Customer](AR_40_90_20.md) form and view the discrepancies listed by vendor or customer. From either of these forms, you click the needed links in the **Discrepancy** column to open the [Discrepancies by Document](AP_40_90_30.md) or [Discrepancies by Document](AR_40_90_30.md) form to view the specific documents causing the discrepancies.
