# Adding Contacts and Payment Methods {#_ca680438-fa1e-410e-b443-9bcc38ca9c6d .concept}

Before assigning roles in the Modern Customer Portal, you—if you’re an authorized portal user—may need to perform these setup steps:

-   Adding contact records for employees who will use the portal, if these don’t already exist. \(You can add contacts at any time.\)
-   Entering your company’s payment methods. Portal users can also add payment methods while paying sales orders and invoices.

In this chapter, you’ll learn how to perform both of these tasks.

**Important:**

This functionality is available if the following conditions are met in Acumatica ERP:

-   The *Modern Customer Portal* feature is enabled on the [Enable/Disable Features](CS_10_00_00.md) \(CS100000\) form
-   The **Admin** and **System** check boxes are selected for the portal on the [Portals](SP_70_10_00.md) \(SP701000\) form
-   The **Payments** check box is selected and the payment functionality has been configured on the [Portals](SP_70_10_00.md) \(SP701000\) form.

If expected functionality isn’t available, contact your company’s portal manager, who can work with the portal owner to review configuration settings, if needed.
