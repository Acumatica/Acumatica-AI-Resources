# Equipment Management {#_2f3d9516-b496-408c-8742-f265fe2ae9a4 .concept}

The equipment management functional area includes basic functionality you can use to manage the equipment of your company and the customer's equipment. You can process customers' contracts on recurring service delivery—that is, enter service contract information, create schedules for the service delivery, and generate service orders for the contracts.

This part of the Field Services Guide contains all the information you need to configure and use the equipment management functionality.
