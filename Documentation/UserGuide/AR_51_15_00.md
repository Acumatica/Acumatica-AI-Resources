# Capture Payments

Form ID: \(AR511500\)

On this form, you can initiate processing of inbound payments \(through processing center services\) by using the information provided by customers about their credit cards. For details on configuring this functionality, see [Configuring Payment Processing](CA__MNG_Payment_Processing.md) and [Card Payments](AR__con_Card_Payments.md).

The form does not display documents that have applications to sales orders or unreleased sales invoices.

If the *Acumatica Payments* feature is enabled on the [Enable/Disable Features](CS_10_00_00.md) \(CS100000\) form, when you create a payment for multiple invoices of the same customer, and then capture this payment on the current form, the tax amount and subtotal amounts are calculated based on the following amounts:

-   **Total Tax** amounts of the invoices on the [Invoices and Memos](AR_30_10_00.md) \(AR301000\) form
-   **Applied to Documents** amount of the payment on the [Payments and Applications](AR_30_20_00.md) \(AR302000\) form
-   Total amounts of the invoices

The system then sends the data to the payment gateway. For details, see [Level 2 Data Transmission During Card Payments](AR__con_Data_Transmission_Acumatica_Payments.md).

**Attention:** This form appears in the system if the *Integrated Card Processing* feature has been enabled on the [Enable/Disable Features](../Shared/../UserGuide/CS_10_00_00.md) \(CS100000\) form.

## Form Toolbar

The form toolbar includes the buttons described below.

|Button|Description|
|------|-----------|
|**Process**|Initiates the process of capturing payments for the documents you have selected in the table.|
|**Process All**|Initiates the process of capturing payments for all documents listed in the table, except for documents that are not ready for automated processing and require user attention. The system displays warning messages near the rows of these documents. You can review these documents on the [Card Payments Pending Review](CA_40_30_00.md) \(CA403000\) form and open them for manual processing.|
