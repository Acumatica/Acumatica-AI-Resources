# Credit Card Refunds: Related Report and Forms {#_986ab9a7-6d55-4cce-8d61-7d8067720cd8 .concept}

This topic describes reports and forms you may review to gather information about the balance of customers for whom you issue credit card refunds.

**Note:** If you do not see a report or inquiry, this could mean that you have signed in to the system with a user account that does not have access rights to a form. Sign in as the *admin* user, or contact your system administrator.

## Reviewing Customer Balance and Documents {#section_dbq_4jv_vxb .section}

You can review the balance and documents of a particular customer by clicking **Customer Details** \(under **Inquiries**\) on the More menu of the [Payments and Applications](AR_30_20_00.md) \(AR302000\) form with the refund selected on it. The system navigates to the [Customer Details](AR_40_20_00.md) \(AR402000\) form where the balance of documents with the *Refund* and *Voided Refund* types affect the customer's balance as a payment with a reverse sign.

## Reviewing a Refund {#section_fbq_4jv_vxb .section}

You can review a refund on the [AR Register Detailed](AR_62_20_00.md) \(AR622000\) form. To run the report, on the [Payments and Applications](AR_30_20_00.md) \(AR302000\) form, you select the refund and on the More menu \(under **Reports**\), you click **AR Register Detailed**.
