# Print/Email Sales Quotes

Form ID: \(CR504545\)

On this form, you can print or email multiple sales quotes. You can process all quotes listed in the table at once or only the ones you select. Before processing, you can narrow the range of listed quotes by selecting a specific owner, workgroup, or business account.

## Form Toolbar

The form toolbar includes the buttons described below.

|Button|Description|
|------|-----------|
|**Process**|Initiates the selected processing \(based on the **Action** that you select\) for the sales quotes that you have selected in the table.|
|**Process All**|Initiates the selected processing \(based on the **Action** that you select\) for all sales quotes that are listed in the table.|

## Table

This table lists all the sales quotes that match the selection criteria you’ve specified. You can process all listed quotes or only those you select.

|Column|Description|
|------|-----------|
|Included|An unlabeled check box that you select to include the sales quote in processing if you click **Process**.|
|**Quote Nbr.**|The reference number of the sales quote.|
|**Opportunity ID**|The identifier of the opportunity associated with the selected quote.|
|**Primary**|A check box that indicates \(if selected\) that the quote is defined as the primary quote for the associated opportunity.|
|**Business Account**|The business account of the prospect or customer of the associated opportunity.|
|**Status**|The status of the sales quote.|
|**Currency**|The currency of the sales quote.|
|**Total**|The total amount specified in the sales quote.|
|**Date**|The date when the sales quote was issued.|
|**Branch**|The branch of your company specified in the sales quote.|
