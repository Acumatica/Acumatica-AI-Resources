# Send Level 3 Data

Form ID: \(CA508000\)

You use this form to send level 3 data from payments, prepayments, and cash sales to the payment processing gateway.

This form shows a list of payments and prepayments captured with commercial or business cards and applied partially or in full to other documents and sales orders. A payment or prepayment is shown if all of the following conditions are met:

-   The payment method of the payment or prepayment has the *Credit Card* means of payment.
-   The processing center specified for the payment supports level 3 processing.
-   The level 3 status of the payment transaction is used for level 3 processing \(*Pending*, *Sent*, *Failed*, or *Resend Failed*\).
-   The sales order or sales invoice to which the payment is applied is not *On Hold* or the payment transaction is related to a cash sale.

Users with any of the following roles have access to this form and can add schedules for the processing: *Administrator*, *AcumaticaSupport*, *AR Admin*, *AR Clerk*, and *SO Admin*.

## Selection Area

You can use the elements in this area to specify the criteria of the documents you want to view. The elements of this area are summarized below.

<table id="_5b36108b-e9c1-483d-80a8-21fa680546ba"><thead><tr><th>

Element

</th><th>

Description

</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>

**Processing Center**

</td><td>

Required. The processing center that processed the payments, prepayments, and cash sales to be listed in the table. The lookup table that is opened in this box shows a list of active processing centers that support level 3 data.

</td></tr><tr><td>

**Processing Status**

</td><td>

Required. The processing status of documents for which level 3 data should be sent.

 The following options are available:

 -   *Pending* \(default\): The payment has been applied to a sales order, sales invoice, or cash sale and captured, but the level 3 data has not been sent to the payment gateway.
-   *Sent*: The level 3 data has been successfully sent and accepted by the payment gateway.
-   *Failed*: The level 3 data has been sent, but this action ended with an error.
-   *Resend Failed*: The level 3 data has been re-sent but this action ended with an error.

</td></tr></tbody>
</table>
