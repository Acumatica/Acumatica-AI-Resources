# Sales of Non-Stock Items with Shipping: Related Reports and Forms {#_86bef5ee-0248-4cb3-98ce-fbd320f5617d .concept}

In the following sections, you can find details about ways to gather information about sales of non-stock items that have been processed or are being processed in the system.

**Attention:** If you don’t see a particular report or form that is described, you may have signed in to the system with a user account that doesn’t have access rights to the report or form. Contact your system administrator to obtain access to any needed reports or forms.

## Tracking the Status of Order Fulfillment { .section}

To track how a sales order is being fulfilled, you can check its status in the Summary area of the [Sales Orders](SO_30_10_00.md) \(SO301000\) form. On the **Totals** tab of this form, you can also check the quantities and amounts that are not yet shipped and not yet billed, and the amount that is not yet paid.

## Printing Invoices { .section}

To prepare a printable form of the sales invoice that is being processed, you use the [Invoice &amp; Memo](SO_64_30_00.md) \(SO643000\) report. While you are viewing a sales invoice on the [Invoices](SO_30_30_00.md) \(SO303000\) form, you can navigate to this report by clicking **Print Invoice** on the More menu. The system opens the printable version of the sales invoice on the [Invoice &amp; Memo](SO_64_30_00.md) report; then you can review the sales invoice and print it by clicking **Print** on the report toolbar.
