# Payments for a Shared Vendor: Reports and Inquiries {#_38c88808-af30-4fe1-9471-07ef6ec89e51 .concept}

In this topic, you can find information about the reports and inquiries related to payments of companies that share a vendor.

## Viewing Bills Pending Payment {#section_ljn_njv_vxb .section}

You use the [Bills Pending Payment](../Shared/../UserGuide/AP_61_15_00.md) \(AP611500\) report to view the list of bills that have not been paid. You can limit the data in the report by any of the following criteria: company, branch, cash account, payment method, vendor, and report date.

## Viewing Payments Pending Processing {#section_mjn_njv_vxb .section}

To view the list of payments that need to be processed, you use the [Payments Pending Processing](AP_65_10_00.md) \(AP651000\) report. You can limit the data in the report by cash account, payment method, vendor, and date.

## Reviewing Checks that Are Pending Printing {#section_ojn_njv_vxb .section}

You can use the [Checks Pending Printing](../Shared/../UserGuide/AP_40_40_00.md) \(AP404000\) form to review which checks have not been printed yet as of a specified pay date. You can initiate check printing on this form by clicking **Print Checks** on the form toolbar. The system navigates to the [Process Payments / Print Checks](../Shared/../UserGuide/AP_50_50_00.md) \(AP505000\) form, where you can print checks.

## Running a Report of Checks that Are Pending Printing {#section_pjn_njv_vxb .section}

You can use the [Checks Pending Printing](../Shared/../UserGuide/AP_61_25_00.md) \(AP612500\) form to review a report on checks that have not yet been printed.

## Viewing Details of Released Payments {#section_qjn_njv_vxb .section}

To see the details of released AP payments for the type and financial period you select on the report form, you use the [AP Payment Register](AP_62_25_00.md) \(AP622500\) report. Payment documents are arranged by type, date, and vendor.
