# Space Usage

Form ID: \(SM203525\)

You can use this form to view statistics on the use of database space by your Acumatica ERP instance. You may need to view these statistics because your license for Acumatica ERP includes a limit on the database space that your instance can use.

**Note:** The statistics of using database space, which is displayed on this form, does not include data related to the System tenant.

Initially, the form displays the statistics calculated most recently and the date and time when they were calculated. To view the current statistics, you click **Calculate Used Space** on the form toolbar.

## Form Toolbar

The form toolbar includes the buttons described below.

|Button|Description|
|------|-----------|
|**Calculate Used Space**|Calculates the space used by the data of your instance in the Acumatica ERP database. The newly calculated data is displayed on the form.|
