# Advanced Planning and Scheduling \(Optimized\)

Advanced Planning and Scheduling \(Optimized\) calculates production dates with minute-level precision and lets you apply finite or infinite scheduling to individual work centers. A production order can include operations assigned to both finite and infinite work centers. The system preserves the routing sequence while it applies the scheduling method specified for each work center.

Optimized APS supports forward and backward scheduling. You can estimate production dates without reserving work center capacity or generate a capacity-constrained schedule that reserves the remaining capacity of finite work centers.

Optimized APS schedules work centers rather than individual machines, tools, or material availability.
