# Simple Budget: Related Report {#_8b6e8302-b03b-4dd9-860e-93cb5a392f49 .concept}

This topic describes a report that you may want to review to gather information about budget articles.

**Tip:** If you do not see the report form, this could mean that you have signed in to the system with a user account that does not have access rights to the report form.

## Viewing the List of Budget Articles { .section}

You can view the list of budget articles that have been created for a particular branch or company for a specific year on the [GL Budget Edit](../Shared/../UserGuide/GL_61_20_01.md) \(GL612001\) form. The report shows the current amount and the distributed amount of each budget article and their released amounts if an article has been released.
