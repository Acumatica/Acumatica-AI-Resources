# To Create the First Year of the Company Calendar {#_d187421a-630a-431c-bdd7-a9507c8c28ab .task}

In Acumatica ERP, with the *Multiple Calendar Support* feature enabled, you configure the company calendar separately for each company. To do this, you use the [Company Financial Calendar](GL_20_11_00.md) \(GL201100\) form.

## Before You Proceed {#section_hyf_mjv_vxb .section}

Make sure that the financial year and its periods have been configured on the [Financial Year](GL_10_10_00.md) \(GL101000\) form. For details, see [To Configure a Financial Year with Month-Based Periods](GL__Configuring_a_Fiscal_Year.md), [To Configure a Financial Year with Week-Based Periods](GL__Configuring_Week_Periods.md), or [To Configure a Financial Year with a Custom Number of Periods](GL__HOW_To_Configure_Fin_Year_with_Custom_Number_of_Periods.md).

## To Configure a Company Calendar {#section_jyf_mjv_vxb .section}

1.  Open the [Company Financial Calendar](GL_20_11_00.md) \(GL201100\) form.
2.  In the **Company** box, select the company for which you want to create the first year of the calendar.
3.  In the **Create First Year of Company Calendar** dialog box, which opens, in the **First Financial Year** box, enter the first financial year of the company's operation.
4.  In the **Master Period ID** box, select a period from the master calendar, which will be the first financial period in the year you have selected.

    This period can be the same as the first period for the year in the master calendar, such as *01-2021*, or it can be earlier or later, such as *11-2020* or *03-2020*.

5.  Click **OK** to close the dialog box.
