# Service Order Details

Form ID: \(FS401000\)

On this form, you can view all the items of the service orders defined in the system and their details.

## Form Toolbar

The form toolbar has only standard buttons.

## Selection Area

You can use the elements in this area to view in the table the service orders that are most relevant to the information you need. Most of these boxes can be left blank to view a broader range of information.
