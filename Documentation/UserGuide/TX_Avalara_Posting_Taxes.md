# Tax Reporting with Avalara AvaTax {#_979a580a-f18b-4dc2-9a92-8b1e51aa7cd6 .concept}

In Acumatica ERP, you can select documents to be included in your tax reports.

## Selecting Documents to Post to the AvaTax Account {#section_fjt_314_bdc .section}

The [Post Taxes](TX_50_15_00.md) \(TX501500\) form displays the list of released transactions for which AvaTax has calculated taxes. You can choose to post all the listed transactions to your AvaTax account or only the ones you select, based on the information you want AvaTax to include in your company's tax reports. Each posted document will have a copy in AvaTax.
