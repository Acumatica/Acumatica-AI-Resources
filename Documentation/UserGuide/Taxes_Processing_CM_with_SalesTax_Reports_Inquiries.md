# Credit Memos with Sales Taxes: Related Reports and Inquiries {#_b629323e-9ebb-4e98-8bbd-2213ed682ea3 .concept}

This topic describes reports, inquiries, and forms you may review to gather information about credit memos.

**Note:** If you do not see a report or inquiry, this could mean that you have signed in to the system with a user account that does not have access rights to a form. Sign in as the *admin* user, or contact your system administrator.

## Reviewing the Customer Information {#section_ssj_fjv_vxb .section}

You can review the balances of a specific customer on the [Customer Profiles](AR_65_10_00.md) \(AR402000\) form. When you open this inquiry from the [Invoices and Memos](AR_30_10_00.md) \(AR301000\) form by clicking **Customer Details** \(under **Inquiries**\) on the More menu the [Customer Profiles](AR_65_10_00.md) form, which is opened, shows the outstanding balances of the selected customer and a list of documents of this customer, which have the *Open* status. You can select the **Show All Documents** and **Include Unreleased Documents** check boxes in the Selection area of the form to include all documents and unreleased documents to the inquiry.

## Preparing a Printable Credit Memo Form {#section_usj_fjv_vxb .section}

You can use the [Print/Email AR Documents](AR_50_80_00.md) \(AR508000\) form to prepare the printable form of a credit memo, review it, and print. To quickly run this report when processing a particular credit memo, click **Print** \(under **Printing and Emailing**\) on the More menu of the [Invoices and Memos](AR_30_10_00.md) \(AR301000\) form.
