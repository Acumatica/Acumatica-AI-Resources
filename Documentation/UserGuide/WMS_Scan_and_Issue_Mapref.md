# Automated Processing of Inventory Issues {#_4989bc5d-9ddf-47cf-ae82-5a131a37265f .concept}

In Acumatica ERP, you can process inventory issues in an automated mode by using barcode scanners or mobile devices with a scanning option on the [Scan and Issue](IN_30_20_20.md) \(IN305020\) form \(IN304020\) form or the Scan and Issue screen in the Acumatica mobile app. This functionality becomes available when the *Inventory Operations* feature is enabled on the [Enable/Disable Features](CS_10_00_00.md) \(CS100000\) form.

The topics of this chapter describe the workflow that you can configure, depending on the business requirements of your company, to support the automated processing of inventory issues.
