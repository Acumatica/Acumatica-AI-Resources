# Automated Processing of Inventory Transfers {#_a860443e-106d-4bbf-b356-5891164dfba9 .concept}

In Acumatica ERP, you can process inventory transfers in automated modes by using barcode scanners or mobile devices with a scanning option on the [Scan and Transfer](IN_30_40_20.md) \(IN304020\) form or the Scan and Transfer screen in the Acumatica mobile app. This functionality becomes available when the *Inventory Operations* feature is enabled on the [Enable/Disable Features](CS_10_00_00.md) \(CS100000\) form.

The topics of this chapter describe the workflow that you can configure, depending on the business requirements of your company, to support the automated processing of inventory transfers.
