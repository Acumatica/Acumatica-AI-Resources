# Voucher Entry Codes

Form ID: \(GL106000\)

On this form, you can view transaction codes for General Ledger, Accounts Payable, Accounts Receivable, and Cash Management documents and transactions; you can also enter new codes. Transaction codes are used to generate documents or transactions of particular types in specific modules, based on batch of documents entered on the [Journal Vouchers](GL_30_40_00.md) \(GL304000\) form. For details, see [Voucher Processing Workflow](GL__CON_Processing_a_Batch_of_Documents.md).
