# History of Picking Location Changes {#_ef616622-f813-496c-a258-af0dc846c613 .reference}

Form ID: \(SO406020\)

You can use this form to view the changes of locations in processed pick lists and shipments on the [Pick, Pack, and Ship](SO_30_20_20.md) form.

## Form Toolbar { .section}

The form toolbar has only standard buttons.

## Selection Area {#1 .section}

You can use the settings in this area to narrow the range of pick lists and shipments listed in the table.

|Element|Description|
|-------|-----------|
|**Warehouse**|The warehouse for which pick lists or shipments will be displayed in the table. The value in this box is mandatory.|
|**Date Range**|The period during which the pick lists or shipments to display in the table were picked. The value in this box is mandatory.|
|**Inventory ID**|The identifier of a stock item whose location was changed in pick lists or shipments that will be displayed in the table.|
|**Picker**|The user who picked the pick lists or shipments that will be displayed in the table..|
|**Shipment Nbr.**|The number of the pick list or shipment that will be displayed in the table.|
|**Worksheet Nbr.**|The number of the worksheet with the shipments that will be displayed in the table.|

## Table Columns {#2 .section}

|Column|Description|
|------|-----------|
|**Date**|The date when a location was changed in the pick list or shipment.|
|**Document Type**|The type of the document in which the location was changed. The type can be one of the following ones:

 -   *Pick List*
-   *Shipment*

|
|**Document Nbr.**|The number of the pick list or shipment in which a location was changed.|
|**Inventory ID**|The identifier of the stock item that was partially or fully picked from a location other than the initial location.|
|**Initial Location**|The initial location of the stock item in the pick list or shipment.|
|**Current Location**|The current location from which a partial or full quantity of the stock item was picked.|
|**Quantity**|The quantity of the stock item picked from a location other than the initial location.|
|**UOM**|The unit of measure of the stock item that was partially or fully picked from a location other than the initial location.|
|**Picker**|The picker who changed the picking location for a partial or full quantity of the item in the pick list or shipment.|
