# To Move an Article {#_ea4d9506-17c0-421e-a020-048b683c934d .task}

You can move an article to a different folder or to a different position within the current folder. If you move an article that is a folder, it is moved along with its contents.

## To Move an Article Across Folders { .section}

To move an article between folders:

1.  Open the article.
2.  On the wiki toolbar, click **Edit Current Article** to open the Wiki Editor form.
3.  On the **Contents** tab, in the **Parent Folder** box, select the folder where you want to place the article.
4.  On the Wiki Editor form toolbar, click **Save**.

## To Move Articles Within a Folder { .section}

To rearrange articles within a folder, do the following:

1.  Open the folder that holds the article.
2.  On the wiki toolbar, click **Edit Current Article** to open the Wiki Editor form.
3.  On the **Subarticles** tab, select the article you want to move.
4.  To change the position of the article within the folder, do one of the following:
    -   On the table toolbar, click **Move Up** to move the article one row up in the list
    -   On the table toolbar, click **Move Down** to move the article one row down.
5.  Repeat Step 4 until you've moved the article where it should be.
6.  Repeat Steps 3 to 5 for each article you want to move.
7.  On the Wiki Editor form toolbar, click **Save**.

To view the new order of articles in the folder, reload or refresh the page.

**Note:** You can also change the order of articles in a folder by using the [Wiki Site Map](SM_20_20_10.md) \(SM202010\) form.
