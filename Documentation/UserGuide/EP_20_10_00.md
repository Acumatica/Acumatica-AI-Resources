# Positions

Form ID: \(EP201000\)

On this form, you can add, view, edit, and delete positions available in your company.

## Form Toolbar

The form toolbar has only standard buttons.

## Position Table

You use this table to add, view, edit, and delete positions and their descriptions.

The table toolbar has only standard buttons.
