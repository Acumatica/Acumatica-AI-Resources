# Release Expense Claims

Form ID: \(EP501000\)

This form lists all the expense claims that are ready for release. You can release either all claims or only selected ones.

On release of an expense claim, the system generates an AP bill that increases the balance of the employee account by the amount of the expense claim payable back to the employee. After successful completion of the operation, the status of the claim changes to *Released*. If the related financial period is closed in the accounts payable subledger and posting to closed periods is not allowed, you can specify in the **Post to Period** box on the **Financial** tab of the [Expense Claim](EP_30_10_00.md) \(EP301000\) form an open period to which the resulting bill should be posted. For more information about releasing expense claims, see [Expense Claims: Expense Claim Release](TimeExpenses_Process_Expense_Claims_Release.md).

This form is available if the *Expense Management* feature is enabled on the [Enable/Disable Features](CS_10_00_00.md) \(CS100000\) form.

## Form Toolbar

The form toolbar includes the buttons described below.

|Button|Description|
|------|-----------|
|**Release**|Releases the claims you have selected in the table and changes their status to *Released*.|
|**Release All**|Releases all the claims listed in the table and changes their status to *Released*.|
