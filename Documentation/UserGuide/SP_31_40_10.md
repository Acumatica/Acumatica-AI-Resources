# Balance Overview

Form ID: \(SP314010\)

**Who uses this form:** An authorized portal user of a company using the Modern Customer Portal.

On this form, you can view financial documents related to your company’s account, including open documents, invoices, payments, and prepayments. You can review document details, filter and sort the list of documents, and initiate payments for open documents individually or together.

The form also provides summary totals and balance information, including overdue amounts and unapplied payments, and supports handling documents in multiple currencies.

This form is available only if both of the following conditions are met for the portal owner:

-   The *Modern Customer Portal* feature is enabled on the [Enable/Disable Features](CS_10_00_00.md) \(CS100000\) form.
-   The portal has the **Financials** check box selected on the **General** tab of the [Portals](SP_70_10_00.md) \(SP701000\) form.

## Totals Section

In this section, you can view summary totals for all documents, including overdue amounts, unapplied payments, outstanding totals, and the overall balance. You can also initiate payment for all open documents in a particular currency.

This section is visible when you’re viewing any tab.

**Tip:** The form supports multiple currencies. If your company has documents in multiple currencies, the system displays a separate **Totals** section for each currency.

<table id="_2582134d-adc8-4694-af52-f414d64721b0"><thead><tr><th>

Element

</th><th>

Description

</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>

**Overdue Total**

</td><td>

The total amount that remains unpaid on accounts receivable documents whose due date is earlier than the current business date.

 The system excludes voided documents, as well as documents of the *Cash Sale* and *Cash Return* types.

</td></tr><tr><td>

**Unapplied Payments**

</td><td>

The total unapplied amount of payments and prepayments. For each document, if the document type is *Payment* or *Prepayment*, the system includes the document balance in the total. Otherwise, a value of *0* is included.

</td></tr><tr><td>

**Outstanding Total**

</td><td>

The total outstanding amount of invoices.

 For each document, if the document type is *Invoice*, the system includes the document balance in the total. Otherwise, a value of *0* is included.

</td></tr><tr><td>

**Balance**

</td><td>

The overall balance calculated from all non-voided documents.

 For each document, the system includes the document balance in the total with the plus or minus sign, depending on whether the document increases or decreases the overall balance.

</td></tr><tr><td>

**Proceed to Pay All**

</td><td>

A button you click to initiate payment for all open documents in a particular currency \(or all open documents if you use only one currency\).

 The button is visible only if the **Payments** check box is selected on the **General** tab of the [Portals](SP_70_10_00.md) \(SP701000\) form.

</td></tr></tbody>
</table>
