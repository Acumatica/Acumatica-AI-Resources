# Bill Prepayments: Mass Processing of Documents {#_3244bff6-820b-4a85-b3c4-23e1444ff725 .concept}

This chapter explains how to release multiple prepayments and the rules that the system uses to group the prepared documents.

## Mass Printing of Payments {#section_yrn_njv_vxb .section}

To print payments in bulk, you use the [Process Payments / Print Checks](AP_50_50_00.md) \(AP505000\) form. To print multiple payments, you select check boxes next to the needed payments in the table, and click **Process** on the form toolbar or click **Process All** to print all the payments listed in the table.

## Mass Release of Prepayments {#section_asn_njv_vxb .section}

You release multiple prepayments on the [Release AP Documents](AP_50_10_00.md) \(AP501000\) form. To release multiple prepayments, you select check boxes next to the needed payments in the table, and click **Process** on the form toolbar or click **Process All** to release all the payments listed in the table.
