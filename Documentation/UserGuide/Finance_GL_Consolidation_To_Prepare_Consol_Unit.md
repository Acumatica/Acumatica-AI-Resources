# GL Consolidation: To Prepare the Consolidation Unit {#_2e4f35d1-c565-4ccf-b5c4-421c1b3b38f1 .task}

The following activity will walk you through the process of preparing the consolidation unit \(a subsidiary company\) for consolidation data import.

## Story {#section_ahm_mjv_vxb .section}

Suppose that the management of the SweetLife AZ company decided to prepare the system for further GL consolidation. The trial balances for November and December 2025 have been transferred from the company's legacy system to Excel files and need to be uploaded to Acumatica ERP.

Acting as the system administrator of SweetLife AZ, you need to prepare the consolidation unit for GL consolidation by mapping its GL accounts with the GL accounts of the parent company and to import the trial balance of SweetLife AZ for the 11-2025 and 12-2025 periods.

## Configuration Overview {#section_tz4_djx_cnb .section}

In the parent company with the *U100* dataset, on the [Enable/Disable Features](CS_10_00_00.md) \(CS100000\) form, the *General Ledger Consolidation* feature has been enabled.

## Process Overview {#section_ghm_mjv_vxb .section}

In this activity, on the [Chart of Accounts](GL_20_25_00.md) \(GL202500\) form, you will map the accounts of the consolidation unit with the parent company accounts by uploading an Excel file.

On the [Trial Balance](GL_30_30_10.md) \(GL303000\) form, you will upload the Excel files with trial balances for the 11-2025 and 12-2025 periods. On the [Journal Transactions](GL_30_10_00.md) \(GL301000\) form, you will release the GL transactions generated by the system.

## System Preparation {#section_jhm_mjv_vxb .section}

Before you begin performing the steps of this activity, do the following:

1.  Launch the Acumatica ERP website, and sign in to the *AZ\_Subsidiary* tenant. You should sign in as the system administrator by using the *admin* username and the *123* password.
2.  Make sure the consolidation unit has been configured as described in [GL Consolidation Configuration: To Configure a Consolidation Unit](Finance_GL_Consolidation_Config_To_Configure_Consol_Unit.md).
3.  Make sure the *AZ\_Subsidiary* company \(the consolidation unit\) has been created and configured as described in [GL Consolidation Configuration: To Configure the Parent Company](Finance_GL_Consolidation_Config_To_Configure_Parent_Company.md).
4.  Make sure that in the parent company \(the company with the *U100* dataset preloaded\) the data synchronization has been performed as described in [GL Consolidation Configuration: To Configure the Parent Company](Finance_GL_Consolidation_Config_To_Configure_Parent_Company.md).
5.  Download the [AZ\_Subsidiary\_COA\_with\_Mapped\_Accounts.xlsx](Files/AZ_Subsidiary_COA_with_Mapped_Accounts.xlsx), [SWEETAZ\_Trial\_Balance\_11-2025.xlsx](Files/SWEETAZ_Trial_Balance_11-2025.xlsx), and [SWEETAZ\_Trial\_Balance\_12-2025.xlsx](Files/SWEETAZ_Trial_Balance_12-2025.xlsx) files.

## Step 1: Mapping Accounts {#section_lhm_mjv_vxb .section}

**Attention:** For the purposes of this step, the accounts have been mapped in an Excel file. To reduce the time for data entry, you will upload a chart of accounts with the accounts mapped, and then map four accounts manually.

To map the accounts of the consolidation unit with the parent company accounts, do the following:

1.  Open the [Chart of Accounts](GL_20_25_00.md) \(GL202500\) form.
2.  On the form toolbar, click **Load Records from File**.
3.  In Step 1 of the **Import Data** wizard, which opens, click Upload File and select the file path to the [AZ\_Subsidiary\_COA\_with\_Mapped\_Accounts.xlsx](Files/AZ_Subsidiary_COA_with_Mapped_Accounts.xlsx) file that you downloaded for this activity.
4.  In Step 2 of the wizard, select *Update Existing* in the **Mode** box, and click **Next**.
5.  In Step 3 of the wizard, leave the current mapping and click **Finish**.

    The system uploads a chart of accounts with accounts from the parent company specified in the **Consolidation Account** column. All the accounts have been mapped, except for four cash accounts. You need to map them manually.

6.  In the row for *10200 - Company Checking Account*, specify *10000* in the **Consolidation Account** column.
7.  In the row for *10300 - Company Savings Account*, specify *10000* in the **Consolidation Account** column.
8.  In the row for *10500 - Cash in Transit*, specify *10000* in the **Consolidation Account** column.
9.  In the row for *10600 - Credit Card Account*, specify *10000* in the **Consolidation Account** column.

    The amounts posted to these accounts will be consolidated in the *10000 - Cash Account* in the parent company.

10. On the form toolbar, click **Save**.

## Step 2: Importing Trial Balance for 11-2025 to the System {#section_qhm_mjv_vxb .section}

To import the trial balance for 11-2025 of SweetLife AZ and post the transaction, do the following:

1.  Open the Trial Balance \(GL3030PL\) list of records.
2.  On the form toolbar, click **New Record**.

    **Tip:** To open the form for creating a new record, type the form ID in the Search box, and on the Search form, point at the form title and click **New** right of the title.

3.  On the [Trial Balance](GL_30_30_10.md) \(GL303000\) form, which opens, specify the following settings in the Summary area:
    -   **Import Date**: *11/30/2025*
    -   **Period**: *11-2025* \(inserted automatically\)
    -   **Company/Branch**: *SWEETAZ* \(inserted by default\)
    -   **Ledger**: *ACTUALAZ*
    -   **Description**: `11-2025 TB import`
4.  On the table toolbar of the **Transaction Details** tab, click **Load Records from File**.
5.  In Step 1 of the **Import Data** wizard, click Upload File and select the path to the [SWEETAZ\_Trial\_Balance\_11-2025.xlsx](Files/SWEETAZ_Trial_Balance_11-2025.xlsx) file.
6.  In Step 2 of the wizard, leave the default settings and click **Next**.
7.  In Step 3 of the wizard, leave the default settings and click **Finish**.

    The system uploads the trial balance for the 11-2025 period on the **Transaction Details** tab.

8.  Select the unlabeled check box for all the rows of the table and on the table toolbar, click **Validate**.
9.  On the form toolbar, click **Remove Hold** and then click **Release** to release the trial balance.

    The system opens the [Journal Transactions](GL_30_10_00.md) \(GL301000\) form and displays a transaction with the uploaded balances.

10. On the form toolbar, click **Release** to release the transaction.

## Step 3: Importing Trial Balance for 12-2025 to the System {#section_vhm_mjv_vxb .section}

To import the trial balance for 12-2025 of SweetLife AZ and post the transaction, do the following:

1.  Open the Trial Balance \(GL3030PL\) list of records.
2.  On the form toolbar, click **New Record**.
3.  On the [Trial Balance](GL_30_30_10.md) \(GL303000\) form, which opens, specify the following settings in the Summary area:
    -   **Import Date**: *12/31/2025*
    -   **Period**: *12-2025* \(inserted automatically\)
    -   **Company/Branch**: *SWEETAZ* \(inserted by default\)
    -   **Ledger**: *ACTUALAZ*
    -   **Description**: `12-2025 TB import`
4.  On the table toolbar of the **Transaction Details** tab, click **Load Records from File**.
5.  In Step 1 of the **Import Data** wizard, which opens, click Upload File and select the path to the [SWEETAZ\_Trial\_Balance\_12-2025.xlsx](Files/SWEETAZ_Trial_Balance_12-2025.xlsx) file.
6.  In Step 2 of the wizard, leave the default settings and click **Next**.
7.  In Step 3 of the wizard, leave the default settings and click **Finish**.

    The system uploads the trial balance for the 12-2025 period on the **Transaction Details** tab.

8.  Select the unlabeled check box for all the rows in the table and on the table toolbar, click **Validate**.
9.  On the form toolbar, click **Remove Hold** and then click **Release** to release the trial balance.

    The system opens the [Journal Transactions](GL_30_10_00.md) \(GL301000\) form and displays a transaction with the uploaded balances.

10. On the form toolbar, click **Release** to release the transaction.
