# Construction Industry Scheme \(CIS\) for Subcontractor Payments

If your company pays subcontractors for construction work in the United Kingdom, HMRC's Construction Industry Scheme \(CIS\) shapes how you pay them. Under the scheme, you deduct an amount from each subcontractor's payment and pass it to HMRC as an advance toward that subcontractor's tax and National Insurance. How much you deduct—nothing at all, 20%, or 30%—depends on the subcontractor's status with HMRC.

Compliance doesn't end with the deduction. For every tax period—which runs from the 6th of one month to the 5th of the next—you give each subcontractor a statement of the amounts you deducted. You file a monthly return with HMRC, even in a month when you made no payments. Both are due by the 19th, so accuracy and timing matter.

Acumatica ERP supports this process from end to end. You set up CIS withholding taxes and tax categories, mark your vendors as CIS subcontractors, and let the system calculate deductions as you enter bills and release payments. When the tax month closes, you prepare and submit your CIS return \(original, amended, or nil\), generate deduction statements for your subcontractors, and review CIS payment activity for reconciliation and audit.
