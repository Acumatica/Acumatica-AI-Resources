# Customer Locations

Form ID: \(AR303020\)

On this form you can define, edit, or delete the locations of customers or prospective customers.

The form is available if the *Business Account Locations* feature is enabled on the [Enable/Disable Features](CS_10_00_00.md) \(CS100000\) form.

**Important:** You cannot delete a customer location if it is associated with any Accounts Receivable documents, sales orders, or customer contracts.

If you make changes to the customer's settings on the **Shipping** tab of the [Customers](AR_30_30_00.md) \(AR303000\) form, those changes are automatically copied to the first location.
