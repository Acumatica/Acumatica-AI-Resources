# Recalculate Inventory

Form ID: \(IN505000\)

**Attention:** This form is available only if the *Inventory* feature is enabled on the [Enable/Disable Features](../Shared/../UserGuide/CS_10_00_00.md) \(CS100000\) form.

You use this form to recalculate the quantities of stock items available at warehouses. The process recalculates availability data for the items based on all open inventory documents and open sales and purchase orders. You can view the list of stock and non-stock items registered in the system and perform recalculation for all stock items at a selected warehouse or for only the selected stock items at this warehouse. For details on availability calculation, see [Availability Calculation Rules: General Information](Availability_Calculation_Rules_GeneralInfo.md).

**Tip:** We recommend that you perform the recalculation after you change the item availability calculation options—the system will recalculate the item availability and allocation data in accordance with the options selected most recently. The recalculation process does not update the cost layers.

During the recalculation process, if the system recovers available quantities of specific stock items, it attempts to update plans for sales orders with the *Back Order* status created for these items. Starting from the earliest back order, the system checks the quantity on the back order. If the quantity is equal to or less than the recovered quantity, the system moves the respective quantity to a *Booked* plan and switches to the next back order until either all recovered quantities are applied or all back orders are checked.

## Form Toolbar

The form toolbar includes the buttons described below.

|Button|Description|
|------|-----------|
|**Process**|Starts the recalculation process for the items that you have selected in the table.|
|**Process All**|Starts the recalculation process for all items listed in the table.|

## Table

You use this area to view all the items that meet the criteria you have selected. You can select the items for which the recalculation will be performed.

The table toolbar has only standard buttons.

|Column|Description|
|------|-----------|
|Included|An unlabeled check box that you select to include the item in recalculation if you click **Process**.|
|**Inventory ID**|The inventory item available at the warehouse.|
|**Description**|The description of the inventory item.|
|**Qty. on Hand**|The quantity of the base units of the inventory item on hand at the selected warehouse.|
|**Qty. Available**|The total quantity of base units of the inventory item available at the selected warehouse.|
|**Qty. Not Available**|The quantity \(in base units\) of inventory items on locations with the **Include in Qty. Available** check box cleared. The option can be found on the [Warehouses](IN_20_40_00.md) \(IN204000\) form.|
