# Staff {#_bb4ef1ac-c43b-42ba-8218-5a3928953af9 .reference}

Form ID: \(FS205500\)

You can use this form to view information about the staff members available for performing field services and to navigate to the [Employees](EP_20_30_00.md) \(EP203000\) or [Vendors](AP_30_30_00.md) \(AP303000\) form to create new staff members or view the settings of existing ones.

## Table Toolbar { .section}

The table toolbar includes standard buttons and buttons specific to this table. The table-specific buttons are listed below.

|Button|Description|
|------|-----------|
|**Add Employee**|Opens the [Employees](EP_20_30_00.md) \(EP203000\) form, where you can create a new employee that can perform field services.|
|**Add Vendor**|Opens the [Vendors](AP_30_30_00.md) \(AP303000\) form, where you can create a new vendor that is defined to perform field services for your company.|

## Table { .section}

|Column|Description|
|------|-----------|
|**Staff Member ID**|The unique identifier of the staff member.

 If the type of the staff member is *Vendor*, the list of vendors available for selection may be limited based on the role or roles assigned to the user account to which you are signed in if the *Customer and Vendor Visibility Restriction* feature is enabled on the [Enable/Disable Features](../Shared/../UserGuide/CS_10_00_00.md) \(CS100000\) form. In this case, the **Restrict Visibility To** box appears on the **Financial** tab of the [Vendors](../Shared/../UserGuide/AP_30_30_00.md) \(AP303000\) form. With the feature enabled, a vendor is available for selection if one of the following is true:

-   The **Restrict Visibility To** box is empty for the vendor—that is, the vendor’s visibility is not restricted.
-   A branch or company is specified in the **Restrict Visibility To** box for the vendor, and your user account is assigned to the role specified in the **Access Role** box of the **Configuration Settings** section of one of the following: the **Branch Details** tab of the [Branches](../Shared/../UserGuide/CS_10_20_00.md) \(CS102000\) form for the branch, or the **Company Details** tab of the [Companies](../Shared/../UserGuide/CS_10_15_00.md) \(CS101500\) form for the company.

|
|**Staff Member Name**|The name of the staff member.|
|**Type**|The type of staff member, which can be *Employee* or *Vendor*.|
|**Branch**|The branch or location of your organization where the staff member of the *Employee* type works. \(This column is blank for a staff member of the *Vendor* type.\)|
|**Email**|The email address of the staff member.|
|**Phone 1**|The phone number of the staff member.|
