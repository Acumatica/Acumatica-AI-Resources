# Integrating Acumatica ERP with Gmail {#_c9c056dd-d2d2-4d99-935f-0a9bb6f860bf .concept}

You can enhance your customer relationship management by integrating Acumatica ERP with Gmail. This integration allows you to create and view contacts, leads, cases, and opportunities right from your Gmail mailbox. You can also link emails to related records in Acumatica ERP to keep your work organized and transparent. In addition, you can streamline accounts payable processing by creating vendor bills from incoming PDF emails.
