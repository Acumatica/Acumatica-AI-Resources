# My Payroll Documents

Form ID: \(PR405000\)

By using this form, you can view a list of your own pay stubs. \(You cannot view pay stubs of any other employee.\)

If the *Canadian Payroll* feature is enabled on the [Enable/Disable Features](CS_10_00_00.md) \(CS100000\) form, you can also review your own annual tax forms submitted to the Canadian Government authorities.

## Pay Stubs Tab

In the table on this tab, your pay stubs are listed. If you click a link in the **Reference Nbr.** column, you can review payment details of the particular pay stub on the printed form.

The system shows only pay stubs linked with the paychecks that have the *Printed* status.

## Annual Forms Tab

On this tab, you can view a list of your submitted annual tax forms, such as *T4* or *Releve1*. If you click a link in the **Year** column, you can review the particular tax form on the printed form.

This tab appears only if the *Canadian Payroll* feature is enabled on the [Enable/Disable Features](CS_10_00_00.md) \(CS100000\) form.

The table toolbar has only standard buttons.

|Column|Description|
|------|-----------|
|**Form Name**|The name of the annual tax form.|
|**Year**|The reporting year, which is also a link that you click to download the document in PDF format.|
|**Company/Branch**|The company or branch that has been reported on, if the *Multicompany Support*, *Multibranch Support*, or both features are enabled on the [Enable/Disable Features](CS_10_00_00.md) \(CS100000\) form.|
|**Document Type**|The type of the document, which can be *Original*, *Amendment*, or *Cancellation*.|
|**Province of Employment**|The Canadian province of employment for which the tax form was prepared.|
