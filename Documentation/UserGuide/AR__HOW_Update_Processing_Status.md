# To Update the Processing Status of Payments

You use the [Update Processing Status](AR_51_40_00.md) \(AR514000\) form to update the processing status for existing credit card and ACH payments.

## Before You Proceed

Make sure that the *Third-Party Integrations* and *Integrated Card Processing* features are enabled on the [Enable/Disable Features](CS_10_00_00.md) \(CS100000\) form.

## To Update the Processing Status

1.  Open the [Update Processing Status](AR_51_40_00.md) \(AR514000\) form.
2.  In the **Payment Date After** box, specify the earliest payment date whose payments should be shown.
3.  Review the list of payment dates that have unprocessed payments.

    By default, the form shows payments from the most recent date range.

4.  Either click **Process All** or select the dates you want to include and click **Process**.

    If more than 30 payment dates are selected, the system asks you to confirm that you want to continue.
