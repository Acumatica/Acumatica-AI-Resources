# Correcting Appointments {#_3c3c9850-5778-4415-869a-1a325162ed99 .concept}

Afteran appointment is completed, you may need to adjust time logs or other settings, particularly when multiple staff members are involved. The appointment editing functionality available in Acumatica ERP simplifies this process and ensures accurate records. Additionally, in some cases, you may need to reopen a canceled appointment and make changes to it.

In this topic, you will learn how to edit completed appointments and reopen canceled appointments in Acumatica ERP.
