# Grouping of Invoices: Related Reports and Inquiry Forms {#_c17cd234-2ac9-4e6e-a687-fa3abcda1e16 .concept}

In the following sections, you can find details about the reports and forms you may want to review to gather information about project billing and grouped invoices.

**Attention:** If you don’t see a particular report or form that is described, you may have signed in to the system with a user account that doesn’t have access rights to the report or form. Contact your system administrator to obtain access to any needed reports or forms.

## Reviewing Project Transactions { .section}

You can review the list of corresponding project transactions of a pro forma invoice line on the **Time and Material** tab of the [Pro Forma Invoices](PM_30_70_00.md) \(PM307000\) form. To review the corresponding project transactions of a line, you click the line, on the table toolbar, click **View Transaction Details**, the system opens the [Project Transaction Details](PM_40_10_00.md) \(PM401000\) form.

## Printing Invoices {#section_rxl_kjw_1bb .section}

You can print a pro forma invoice by clicking **Print** on the More menu of the [Pro Forma Invoices](PM_30_70_00.md) \(PM307000\) form. The system opens the printable form of the invoice that is project-specific and specified for the *PROFORMA* mailing of the project on the **Mailing &amp; Printing** tab of the [Projects](PM_30_10_00.md) \(PM301000\) form. By default, this is the [Pro Forma Invoice](PM_64_20_00.md) \(PM642000\) form.
