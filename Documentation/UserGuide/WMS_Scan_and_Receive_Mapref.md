# Automated Processing of Inventory Receipts {#_77dba98c-a4d6-4480-9a06-a1ba13aa7b5e .concept}

In Acumatica ERP, you can process inventory receipts in an automated mode by using barcode scanners or mobile devices with a scanning option on the [Scan and Receive](IN_30_10_20.md) \(IN301020\) form or the Scan and Receive screen in the Acumatica mobile app. This functionality becomes available when the *Inventory Operations* feature is enabled on the [Enable/Disable Features](CS_10_00_00.md) \(CS100000\) form.

The topics of this chapter describe the workflow that you can configure, depending on the business requirements of your company, to support the automated processing of inventory receipts.
