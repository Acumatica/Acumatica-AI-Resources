# Customer Returns with Refunds: Mass-Processing of Documents {#_30683f8a-69b6-4e71-b9b6-d0d7e93bc1a2 .concept}

The following sections explain how to mass-process documents related to the processing of customer returns with refunds, and how the system generates, changes, or works with documents as a result of the mass processing.

## Mass-Confirming Shipments { .section}

You can confirm multiple incoming and outgoing shipments at a time. To do this, you open the [Process Shipments](SO_50_30_00.md) \(SO503000\) form and select the *Confirm Shipment* action. Then you select the unlabeled check boxes in the rows of the shipments to be processed and click **Process** on the form toolbar. The system confirms the selected shipment documents.

## Mass-Creating Credit Memos { .section}

You can create multiple credit memos \(which are invoices of the *Credit Memo* type\) on the [Process Orders](SO_50_10_00.md) \(SO501000\) or [Process Shipments](SO_50_30_00.md) \(SO503000\) form. On these forms, you select the *Prepare Invoice* action. Then you select the unlabeled check boxes in the return order lines to be processed, which could include lines of the return order or orders you were working with, and lines of any other order. Finally, you click **Process** on the form toolbar.
