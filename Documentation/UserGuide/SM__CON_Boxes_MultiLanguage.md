# Boxes That Have Multilanguage Support {#_837d92c1-eeba-4023-8f83-23a7023a48c7 .concept}

Once you have enabled multilingual user input, the system displays a link with a language ISO code next to the supported text boxes in the system. When you click the link, the **Translations** dialog box appears, where you can enter translations.

By default, the system supports multilingual user input for the boxes listed in this topic. You can expand this list by means of customization. For details, see [Customization of Field Attributes in DAC Extensions](../CustomizationPlatform/CG_Platform_Framework_CS_DACExt_FieldAttributes.md).

In this topic, you will find lists, broken down by module, of the boxes that have multilanguage support.

## Projects { .section}

You can localize values for the following text boxes on the project-related forms:

-   The **Invoice Description** column on the [Billing Rules](PM_20_70_00.md) \(PM207000\) form
-   The **Description** box on the [Projects](PM_30_10_00.md) \(PM301000\) form
-   The **Description** box in the Summary area of the [Project Tasks](PM_30_20_00.md) \(PM302000\) form
-   The **Description** box in the Summary area of the [Account Groups](PM_20_10_00.md) \(PM201000\) form
-   The **Description** column on the [Cost Codes](PM_20_95_00.md) \(PM209500\) form

## General Ledger { .section}

You can localize values for the following text boxes on the general ledger forms:

-   The **Description** column on the [Financial Year](GL_10_10_00.md) \(GL101000\) form
-   The **Description** column on the [Master Financial Calendar](GL_20_10_00.md) \(GL201000\) form

    **Tip:** If you have provided translations for financial periods on the [Financial Year](GL_10_10_00.md) form, the system copies these translations to the **Description** column on the [Master Financial Calendar](GL_20_10_00.md) form, when you generate periods for a new year. For an existing year you need to add translations manually.

-   The **Description** column on the [Ledgers](GL_20_15_00.md) \(GL201500\) form
-   The **Description** column on the [Account Classes](GL_20_20_00.md) \(GL202000\) form
-   The **Description** column on the [Chart of Accounts](GL_20_25_00.md) \(GL202500\) form
-   The **Description** column on the [Subaccounts](GL_20_30_00.md) \(GL203000\) form
-   The following text boxes on the analytical reports \(ARM\) forms:
    -   The **Description** and **Value** columns on the [Row Sets](CS_20_60_10.md) \(CS206010\) form
    -   The Formula columns in the upper table and the **Description** and **Value** lines on the [Column Sets](CS_20_60_20.md) \(CS206020\) form
    -   The **Description** and **Value** columns on the [Unit Sets](CS_20_60_30.md) \(CS206030\) form

## Cash Management { .section}

You can localize values for the following text boxes on the cash management forms:

-   The **Description** box in the Summary area of the [Cash Accounts](CA_20_20_00.md) \(CA202000\) form
-   The **Description** box in the Summary area of the [Payment Methods](CA_20_40_00.md) \(CA204000\) form

-   The **Fee Description** box on the [Overdue Charges](AR_20_45_00.md) \(AR204500\) form
-   The **Description** box on the [Customer Classes](AR_20_10_00.md) \(AR201000\) form
-   The **Description** column on the [Customer Price Classes](AR_20_80_00.md) \(AR208000\) form
-   The **Description** box on the [Reason Codes](CS_21_10_00.md) \(CS211000\) form
-   The **Description** box on the [Contract Templates](CT_20_20_00.md) \(CT202000\) form
-   The **Invoice Description** column on the [Contract Templates](CT_20_20_00.md) \(CT202000\) form
-   The **Line Description** column on the [Contract Templates](CT_20_20_00.md) \(CT202000\) form
-   The **Description** box on the [Customer Contracts](CT_30_10_00.md) \(CT301000\) form
-   The **Invoice Description** column on the [Customer Contracts](CT_30_10_00.md) \(CT301000\) form
-   The **Line Description** column on the [Customer Contracts](CT_30_10_00.md) \(CT301000\) form
-   The **Credit Memo Description** box on the [Accounts Receivable Preferences](AR_10_10_00.md) \(AR101000\) form

-   The **Description** box on the [Order Types](SO_20_10_00.md) \(SO201000\) form
-   The **Description** column on the [FOB Points](CS_20_85_00.md) \(CS208500\) form
-   The **Description** box on the [Shipping Terms](CS_20_80_00.md) \(CS208000\) form

## Common Settings { .section}

You can localize values for the following text boxes in the common settings:

-   The **Country** box on the [Countries/States](CS_20_40_00.md) \(CS204000\) form
-   The **Description** box and the **Description** column on the [Attributes](CS_20_50_00.md) \(CS205000\) form
-   The **New Number Symbol** box on the [Numbering Sequences](CS_20_10_10.md) \(CS201010\) form

## Customization { .section}

You can localize values for the following text boxes on the customization forms:

-   The **Name** box on the [Filters](CS_20_90_10.md) \(CS209010\) form
-   The **Display** column on the **Parameters** tab of the [Generic Inquiry](SM_20_80_00.md) \(SM208000\) form
-   The **Caption** column on the **Results Grid** tab of the [Generic Inquiry](SM_20_80_00.md) \(SM208000\) form

## Dashboards { .section}

If you have rights to edit a particular dashboard, you can localize values for the following text boxes in the **Widget Properties** dialog box:

-   The **Caption** box for all widget types
-   The **Scorecard Title** box for the scorecard widget type
-   The **Trend Card Title** box for the trend card widget type
