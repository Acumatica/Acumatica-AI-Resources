# Committed Costs: Related Reports and Forms {#_c17cd444-2ac9-4e6e-a687-fa3abcda1e16 .concept}

In the following sections, you can find details about the reports and forms you may want to review to gather information about commitments.

**Attention:** If you don’t see a particular report or form that is described, you may have signed in to the system with a user account that doesn’t have access rights to the report or form. Contact your system administrator to obtain access to any needed reports or forms.

## Reviewing the Commitments of a Project { .section}

You can review the list of a project’s commitments on the [Commitments](../Shared/../UserGuide/PM_30_60_00.md) \(PM306000\) form. In the Selection area of this form, you select the project; you can also filter the selected transactions by selecting a project task, an account group, and an inventory item.

**Important:** The form displays the commitments associated with the branches to which your user has sufficient access rights.

## Reviewing Commitments by Project Budget Line { .section}

You can review the commitments corresponding to a project budget line with a nonzero committed value on the **Cost Budget** tab of the [Projects](../Shared/../UserGuide/PM_30_10_00.md) \(PM301000\) form. To review the corresponding commitments of a line, you click the line; then on the table toolbar, you click **View Commitments**, and the system opens the [Commitments](../Shared/../UserGuide/PM_30_60_00.md) \(PM306000\) form.

## Reviewing Commitments by Account Group { .section}

You can review the project budget broken down by account groups on the **Balances** tab of the [Projects](../Shared/../UserGuide/PM_30_10_00.md) \(PM301000\) form. To review the commitments corresponding to an account group, you click a line; then on the table toolbar, you click **View Commitments**, and the system opens the [Commitments](../Shared/../UserGuide/PM_30_60_00.md) \(PM306000\) form.

## Reviewing Purchase Orders Related to a Project { .section}

You can review the list of purchase orders from which the project commitments originate on the **Commitments** tab of the [Projects](PM_30_10_00.md) \(PM301000\) form.

## Retrieving Committed Data for Analytical Reports { .section}

The commitment data is available in the analytical reports and aggregated by the following key fields of a project budget line:

-   `Project ID`: The project
-   `Project Task ID`: The project task
-   `Account Group ID`: The account group
-   `Inventory ID`: The inventory item
