# Staff Schedule Rules

Form ID: \(FS4010PL\)

On this form, you can view all the information about the schedule rules of every staff member available to perform field services. This information includes the name of the staff member, the branch location \(to which the staff member is assigned\), and the staff member's schedule \(that is, the times when the person is available to perform services\).

To add a new schedule rule, you click **New Record** on the form toolbar. The system opens the [Staff Schedule Rules](FS_20_20_01.md) \(FS202001\) form, where you can enter the data for a new schedule rule.

## Form Toolbar

The form toolbar includes only standard buttons.

## Selection Area

By selecting a staff member in this area, you can display the records of only this staff member.

|Element|Description|
|-------|-----------|
|**Staff Member**|The name of the staff member for whom you want to view schedule rules.|
