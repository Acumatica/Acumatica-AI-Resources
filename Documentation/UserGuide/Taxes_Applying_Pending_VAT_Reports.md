# Applying a Pending VAT: Related Report and Inquiry Forms {#_e7f35d8b-12aa-43e6-804e-505406a26a3a .concept}

In the following sections, you can find details about the report you may want to review to gather information about pending VAT.

**Attention:** If you don’t see a particular report or form that is described, you may have signed in to the system with a user account that doesn’t have access rights to the report or form. Contact your system administrator to obtain access to any needed reports or forms.

## Reviewing VAT Pending Recognition {#section_zwp_fjv_vxb .section}

You can review the amounts of pending output VAT and pending input VAT for a specific period by running a report on the [VAT Pending Recognition](TX_63_10_00.md) \(TX631000\) form. By using this report, you can reconcile the documents that have unrecognized VAT amounts with the balances of the Pending Tax Payable and Pending Tax Claimable accounts.
