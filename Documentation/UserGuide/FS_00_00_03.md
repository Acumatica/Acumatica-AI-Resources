# Route Management

With the integration between the route management and service management functionality of Acumatica ERP, you can handle appointments associated with routes. The routes can be generated through contracts or can be created manually for a specific day. 

You can assign a driver and a vehicle to a route, according to the required skills of the route. For a route, the module also lets you reorganize the scheduled appointments by adding, deleting or moving them, as well as changing their order. A generated route will give you useful statistics, such as driving time and distance, that will help you make better decisions to optimize your business management.

In the scenarios in which a route also handles pickups and delivery items, you can also generate inventory documents.

This part of the Field Services Guide contains all the information you need to use the route management functionality.
