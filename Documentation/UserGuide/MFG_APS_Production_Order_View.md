# Advanced Planning and Scheduling: Production Order View {#_916bed49-8dca-499f-aad5-1add91d23a81 .concept}

The [Production Order View](AM_21_55_80.md) \(AM215580\) form gives you a consolidated, real-time look at every level of a product’s manufacturing process, so you can stay ahead of issues and keep operations on track.

With this view, you can:

-   Quickly review unissued material and operation statuses across all child and parent production orders right from a single form
-   Take immediate action, such as firming or rescheduling child orders, based on the latest supply or timing changes
-   Spend less time fielding status requests because customer service representatives can access the same view and handle most inquiries themselves

The icons and color coding used on the [Production Order View](AM_21_55_80.md) form follow the patterns established on the [Production Schedule Board](AM_21_55_55.md) \(AM215555\) form. For details, see [Advanced Planning and Scheduling: Production Schedule Board](MFG_APS_Production_Schedule_Board.md).
