# Inventory Lot/Serial History

Form ID: \(IN407000\)

**Attention:** This form is available only if the *Lot and Serial Tracking* feature is enabled on the [Enable/Disable Features](CS_10_00_00.md) \(CS100000\) form.

On this inquiry form, you can view detailed information about inventory stocked at the warehouses. You can locate a particular unit of the inventory item by its lot or serial number.

## Form Toolbar

The form toolbar includes the buttons described below.

|Button|Description|
|------|-----------|
|**Inventory Summary**|Opens the [Inventory Summary](IN_40_10_00.md) \(IN401000\) form as a pop-up to display the availability data of the inventory item.|
|**Allocation Details**|Opens the [Inventory Allocation Details](IN_40_20_00.md) \(IN402000\) form as a pop-up to display information about how the selected inventory item is allocated at the specified warehouse.|
