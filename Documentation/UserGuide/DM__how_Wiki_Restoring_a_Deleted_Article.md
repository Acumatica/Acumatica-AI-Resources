# To Restore a Deleted Article {#_3da9ec52-d545-48e8-9b07-da574c9478d6 .task}

Once authorized users have deleted an article, the system places it in the **Deleted Items** folder in the navigation pane. Deleted articles remain in this folder until they are permanently deleted.

To restore an article from the **Deleted Items** folder, do the following:

1.  Open the **Deleted Items** folder.
2.  Open the article you want to restore.
3.  On the wiki toolbar, click **Edit Current Article** to open the Wiki Editor form.
4.  In the **Parent Folder** box, select the folder to hold the restored article.
5.  On the Wiki Editor form toolbar, click **Save**.
