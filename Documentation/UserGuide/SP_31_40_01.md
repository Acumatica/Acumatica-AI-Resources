# Invoice Payments

Form ID: \(SP314001\)

**Who uses this form:** An authorized portal user of a company using the Modern Customer Portal.

On this form, you can view the payments and prepayments related to an invoice or memo, as well as the settings of these payments and prepayments. You can open this form from the [Payments](SP_33_40_00.md) \(SP334000\) form by clicking the invoice’s reference number, which is displayed as a link.

This form is available only if both of the following conditions are met in Acumatica ERP for the portal owner:

-   The *Modern Customer Portal* feature is enabled on the [Enable/Disable Features](CS_10_00_00.md) \(CS100000\) form.
-   The portal has the **Payments** check box selected on the **General** tab of the [Portals](SP_70_10_00.md) \(SP701000\) form.
