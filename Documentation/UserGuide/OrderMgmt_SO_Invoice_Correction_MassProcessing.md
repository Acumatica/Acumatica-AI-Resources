# Sales Invoice Correction: Mass-Processing Documents {#_a7e8136d-938a-4d64-9260-bbc5d0d5ab4f .concept}

The following sections explain how to mass-process sales invoices, and how the system generates, changes, or works with documents as a result of the mass processing.

## Mass-Creating Sales Invoices { .section}

Invoices can be mass-generated for sales orders or for the corresponding shipments. To prepare multiple invoices for sales orders, you open the [Process Orders](SO_50_10_00.md) \(SO501000\) form; to prepare multiple invoices for shipments, you open the [Process Shipments](SO_50_30_00.md) \(SO503000\) form. Then you select the *Prepare Invoice* action, select the unlabeled check boxes in the rows of the sales orders or shipments to be processed, and click **Process** on the form toolbar. The system prepares invoices for the selected documents.

## Mass-Printing Sales Invoices { .section}

You can print multiple sales invoices at a time by using the [Process Invoices and Memos](SO_50_50_00.md) \(SO505000\) form. On this form, you select the *Print Invoice* action, select the unlabeled check boxes in the rows of the invoices to be printed, and click **Process** on the form toolbar.

## Mass-Releasing Sales Invoices { .section}

You can release multiple sales invoices at a time by using the [Process Invoices and Memos](SO_50_50_00.md) \(SO505000\) form. On this form, you select the *Release* action, select the unlabeled check boxes in the rows of the sales invoices to be printed, and click **Process** on the form toolbar.
