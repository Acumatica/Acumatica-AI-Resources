# Employee PTO Balances {#_2cd2b37a-94be-44cc-add6-e84f541a31d6 .reference}

Form ID: \(PR406000\)

By using this form, you can view PTO balances over a selected period for all employees or for only a selected employee.

**Attention:** Regular employees with the *Internal User* role cannot access the from. They can review their PTO balances on the *Payroll Personal View* dashboard and in the PTO section of their pay stubs.

## Form Toolbar { .section}

The form toolbar has only standard buttons.

## Selection Area {#1 .section}

You can use the settings in this area to narrow the range of records listed in the table. The dates in the **Start Date** and **End Date** boxes are selected from the transaction dates specified for paychecks on the [Paychecks and Adjustments](PR_30_20_00.md) \(PR302000\) form and PTO adjustment dates specified on the [PTO Adjustments](PR_30_60_00.md) \(PR306000\) form.

|Element|Description|
|-------|-----------|
|**Start Date**|The starting date of the period for which records are to be displayed in the table.|
|**End Date**|The ending date of the period for which records are to be displayed in the table.|
|**Employee**|The employee whose information is to be displayed in the table. If none is selected, the table lists records associated with all employees.|
|**PTO Bank**|The PTO bank whose information is to be displayed in the table. If none is selected, the table lists records associated with all PTO banks.|

## Table {#2 .section}

The table toolbar has only standard buttons.

|Column|Description|
|------|-----------|
|**Employee**|The identifier of the employee.|
|**Employee Name**|The name of the employee, as defined on the [Employees](EP_20_30_00.md) \(EP203000\) form.|
|**PTO Bank**|The unique identifier of the PTO bank as defined on the [PTO Banks](PR_20_40_00.md) \(PR204000\) form.|
|**PTO Bank Description**|The description of the PTO bank as defined on the [PTO Banks](PR_20_40_00.md) \(PR204000\) form.|
|**Effective From**|The date starting from which the settings of the PTO bank are applied to the employee.|
|**Total Hours Accumulated**|The total number of hours accumulated in the PTO bank over the specified period for the selected employee.|
|**Total Hours Used**|The total number of hours the selected employee used from the PTO bank over the specified period.|
|**Total Hours Available**|The total number of hours available in the PTO bank by the end of the specified period for the selected employee.|
