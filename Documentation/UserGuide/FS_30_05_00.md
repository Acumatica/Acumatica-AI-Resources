# Staff Working Schedule Board

Form ID: \(FS300500\)

On this form, you can view and modify the generated schedules for a particular staff member on the calendar board for a specific day, week, or month.

## Dashboard

The dashboard displays the work schedule for the selected staff member in the specific day, week, or month. The work schedules that are displayed in blue have the *Availability* type, and the work schedules that are displayed in red have the *Unavailability* type.
