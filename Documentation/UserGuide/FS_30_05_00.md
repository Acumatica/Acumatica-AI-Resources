# Staff Working Schedule Board {#_12d4ce83-6520-46dc-afad-46a36627a82a .reference}

Form ID: \(FS300500\)

On this form, you can view and modify the generated schedules for a particular staff member on the calendar board for a specific day, week, or month.

## Dashboard { .section}

The dashboard displays the work schedule for the selected staff member in the specific day, week, or month. The work schedules that are displayed in blue have the *Availability* type, and the work schedules that are displayed in red have the *Unavailability* type.

You can change the work start and end time for each day of the work schedule on the dashboard by dragging the top or bottom edge of the work schedule to the needed time. You can also delete the work schedule for a particular day by clicking the **Delete** icon \(![](Images/Icon_Trash.PNG)\) in the upper right corner of the work schedule.

|Element|Description|
|-------|-----------|
|**Branch**|The branch of the staff member for whom you want to view data.|
|**Branch Location**|The branch location of the staff member for whom you want to view data.|
|**Staff**|The staff member for whom you want to view data.|
|**Day**|A button that you click to display on the dashboard the data for the date selected in the Date or Date Range box.|
|**Week**|A button that you click to display on the dashboard the data for the week of the date selected in the Date or Date Range box.|
|**Month**|A button that you click to display on the dashboard the data for the month of the date selected in the Date or Date Range box.|
|**Merge Rules**|A button that you click to display on the dashboard the schedules associated with the selected staff member that take place at the same time, merged into a single schedule.

 If this button is selected, you cannot change the times of the schedules.
