# Debit and Credit Adjustments: Related Reports and Inquiries {#_36a6c54d-17d3-4fa2-8d56-10ba011345ba .concept}

This topic describes the reports, inquiries, and forms you may review to gather information about debit and credit adjustments and how they affect vendor balances.

**Attention:** If you do not see a report or inquiry, this could mean that you have signed in to the system with a user account that does not have access rights to the form. Sign in as the *admin* user, or contact your system administrator.

## Reviewing the Vendor's Outstanding Balance {#section_kp3_njv_vxb .section}

You use the [Vendor Details](AP_40_20_00.md) \(AP402000\) form to review the outstanding balance of a particular vendor. The debit adjustments you create in the system decrease the outstanding balance of the vendor, and the credit adjustments increase the outstanding balance.

## Reviewing the Vendor's Balance and Documents {#section_mp3_njv_vxb .section}

You run the [AP Balance by Vendor](AP_63_25_00.md) \(AP632500\) report to review the balance and documents of a particular vendor for a particular period.

## Reviewing Debit Adjustments with Cash Discounts on AP Aging Reports { .section}

You can review debit adjustments with credit terms specified in the following AP aging reports:

-   [AP Aging](AP_63_10_00.md) \(AP631000\) and [AP Aging MC](AP_63_11_00.md) \(AP631100\): Based on the **Due Date** of the debit adjustment, the system shows the document amount either in the **Current** column or in one of the **Past Due** columns.
-   [AP Coming Due](AP_63_15_00.md) \(AP631500\) and [AP Coming Due MC](AP_63_16_00.md) \(AP631600\): Based on the **Due Date** of the debit adjustment, the system shows the document amount either in the **Past Due** column or in one of the **Coming Due** columns.
-   [AP Aged Period-Sensitive](AP_63_05_00.md) \(AP630500\): Based on the **Due Date** of the debit adjustment, the system shows the document amount either in the **Current** column or in one of the **Past Due** columns.
