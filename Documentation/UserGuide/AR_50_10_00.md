# Release AR Documents {#_78ad2d21-b6a8-46d0-8f2d-98dd0a23f75e .reference}

Form ID: \(AR501000\)

This form can be used to release all or selected ready-to-be-released \(*Balanced*\) documents and unreleased applications of open payments and credit memos. Whether documents will be posted automatically when they are released is defined by the **Automatically Post on Release** check box on the [Accounts Receivable Preferences](AR_10_10_00.md) \(AR101000\) form.

## Form Toolbar { .section}

The form toolbar includes the buttons described below.

|Button|Description|
|------|-----------|
|**Release**|Releases the Accounts Receivable documents you have selected in the table, changes their status to *Open*, and creates unposted batches or posts batches directly to the General Ledger, depending on whether the **Automatically Post on Release** check box is selected on the [Accounts Receivable Preferences](AR_10_10_00.md) form.|
|**Release All**|Releases all the Accounts Receivable documents on the list at once and changes their status to *Open*. Whether transactions are directly posted on release is defined by the **Automatically Post on Release** check box on the [Accounts Receivable Preferences](AR_10_10_00.md) form.|

## Table { .section}

The table contains all ready-to-be-released Accounts Receivable documents and payments \(those with a status of *Balanced*\). To include a document, select the check box that precedes it. To select all the documents, select the check box in the column header.

|Column|Description|
|------|-----------|
|Included|An unlabeled check box that you select to include the document when you click **Release**.|
|**Type**|The type of Accounts Receivable document. The following types can be released: -   *Invoice*: An invoice sent to the customer for goods sold or services provided
-   *Credit Memo*: A document that adjusts the credit amount in one previous invoice or multiple previous invoices
-   *Debit Memo*: A document that adjusts the debit amount in one previous invoice or multiple previous invoices
-   *Overdue Charge*: A document for overdue charges
-   *Cash Sale*: A document created to record a sale paid immediately by either cash or credit card
-   *Payment*: A customer payment
-   *Refund*: A document created for a refund
-   *Deferred Revenue*: A document created for unearned revenues that initially are recorded as liabilities but are expected to become assets over time

|
|**Reference Nbr.**|The identifier of the document. Clicking the link navigates to the [Invoices and Memos](AR_30_10_00.md) \(AR301000\), [Payments and Applications](AR_30_20_00.md) \(AR302000\), or [Cash Sales](AR_30_40_00.md) \(AR304000\) form, depending on the type of the selected document, and displays the details of the selected Accounts Receivable document.|
|**Customer**|The customer \(by ID\) referred to on this document.|
|**Customer Name**|The customer's complete name as it should appear on the documents.|
|**Customer Order Nbr.**|The document reference number used by the customer.|
|**Status**|The status of the document to be released \(*Balanced*\) or of the payment or credit memo to be applied \(*Open*\).|
|**Date**|The date when the document was created.|
|**Post Period**|The period to which the transactions of the document should be posted.|
|**Currency Amount**|The amount of the document in the document currency.

 This column appears on the form if the *Multicurrency Accounting* feature has been enabled on the [Enable/Disable Features](CS_10_00_00.md) \(CS100000\) form.

|
|**Currency**|The currency used in the document.

 This column appears on the form if the *Multicurrency Accounting* feature has been enabled on the [Enable/Disable Features](CS_10_00_00.md) \(CS100000\) form.

|
|**Amount**|The amount of the document in the base currency.|
|**Description**|The user-provided description for the document.|
