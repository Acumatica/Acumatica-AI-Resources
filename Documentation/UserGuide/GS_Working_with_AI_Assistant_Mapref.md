# Working with AI Assistant {#_44dc5626-383c-4881-bbc4-deaf1bcfe426 .concept}

*AI Assistant* is a chat window inside Acumatica ERP where you can ask questions in plain language and quickly explore your data. Instead of checking multiple forms and reports to find answers, you can get answers immediately and act on them with just a click.

AI Assistant can return answers as text summaries, tables, lists, charts, and pivot tables. You can jump directly to the relevant forms and records from the response.

In this chapter, you will learn how to work with AI Assistant.
