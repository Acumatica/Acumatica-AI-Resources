# Purchases of Services Without Receipts: Mass Processing of Documents {#_60b1d0d3-2bcb-4a4e-8393-19a9d2b72dfb .concept}

The following sections explain how to mass-process purchase orders, and how the system generates, changes, or works with documents as a result of the mass processing.

## Mass-Printing Purchase Orders { .section}

You can print multiple purchase orders at the same time by using the [Print/Email Purchase Orders](PO_50_30_00.md) \(PO503000\) form. To do this for multiple purchase orders simultaneously, you select the *Print Purchase Order* action, as well as any other selection criteria to narrow the orders to be listed.

Then you select the unlabeled check boxes for the purchase orders to be printed, and click **Process** on the form toolbar. As an alternative to printing only selected orders, you can click **Process All** to print all the listed purchase orders.

## Mass-Emailing Purchase Orders { .section}

If needed, you can email multiple purchase orders at the same time by using the [Print/Email Purchase Orders](PO_50_30_00.md) \(PO503000\) form. To do this for multiple purchase orders simultaneously, you first select the *Email Purchase Order* action, as well as any other selection criteria to narrow the orders to be listed.

Then you select the unlabeled check boxes for the purchase orders to be emailed, and click **Process** on the form toolbar. As an alternative to emailing only selected orders, you can click **Process All** to email all the listed purchase orders.
