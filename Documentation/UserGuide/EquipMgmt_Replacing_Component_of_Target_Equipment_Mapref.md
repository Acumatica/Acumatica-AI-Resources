# Replacing a Component of Target Equipment {#_abcb7231-52a2-4e9e-a6b4-6f7c28c3ef45 .concept}

In Acumatica ERP, you can process the replacement of a component in existing equipment and coordinate the associated replacement services. To do this, you will specify the *Replacing Component* equipment-related action for the selected stock item in an appointment created on the [Appointments](FS_30_02_00.md) \(FS300200\) form.
