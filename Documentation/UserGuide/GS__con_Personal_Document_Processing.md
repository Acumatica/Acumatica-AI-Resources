# Personal Settings for Record Processing {#_2c6bcbb9-f9ce-4561-90db-1b436da8d9b5 .concept}

When you process records, you may want to apply your personal settings. In Acumatica ERP, you can specify personal settings for records exported to Microsoft Excel and for emails that you send from the system.

In this topic, you will read about personalizing settings for record processing.

## Email Settings { .section}

Your personal email account is specified on the [Users](SM_20_10_10.md) \(SM201010\) form by a system administrator who creates user accounts. You can view and change it on the **General Info** tab of the [User Profile](SM_20_30_10.md) \(SM203010\) form.

If you need to send emails from Acumatica ERP, you can configure your signature that will be automatically added to each email. You can create your signature on the **Email Settings** tab of the [User Profile](SM_20_30_10.md) form.

On the same tab, you can select the system email account that will be used as the default email account when you send emails through Acumatica ERP. Incoming emails and replies to mass emails will be automatically sorted by the system and routed to your personal email account.

For details about how to configure your email account, see [To Configure Your Email Account](User_Profile_Email.md).

For more information about using the email functionality in Acumatica ERP, see [Managing Emails](EP__con_Email_Management.md).
