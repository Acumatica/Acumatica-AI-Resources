# Subcontracts: Mass Processing {#_836181b8-c7c9-4ae3-a890-1298ec798f34 .concept}

The following sections explain how to mass-process subcontracts.

## Mass Printing of Subcontracts {#section_lc2_xhs_tnb .section}

To print subcontracts in bulk, you use the [Print/Email Subcontracts](SC_50_30_00.md#) \(SC503000\) form. You select *Print* in the **Action** box, select the check boxes in the rows of the needed subcontracts in the table, and click **Process** on the form toolbar; you can click **Process All** on the form toolbar to print all the subcontracts listed in the table.

## Mass Emailing of Subcontracts {#section_mc2_xhs_tnb .section}

To send emails with multiple subcontracts, you use the [Print/Email Subcontracts](SC_50_30_00.md#) \(SC503000\) form. You select *Email* in the **Action** box, select check boxes in the rows of the needed subcontracts in the table, and click **Process** on the form toolbar; you can click **Process All** to send emails with all the subcontracts listed in the table.
