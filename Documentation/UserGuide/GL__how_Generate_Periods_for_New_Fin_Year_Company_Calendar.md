# To Generate Periods for New Financial Years in a Company Calendar {#_fac07e31-43f9-4f7f-955f-f7deb9ecc057 .task}

Once you have configured a company calendar, you need to generate periods for new financial years in the company calendar. To do this, you use the [Company Financial Calendar](GL_20_11_00.md) \(GL201100\) form.

## To Generate Periods for New Financial Years in Each Company Calendar {#section_rzf_mjv_vxb .section}

1.  Open the [Company Financial Calendar](GL_20_11_00.md) \(GL201100\) form.
2.  In the **Company** box of the Selection area, select a company for which you want to generate periods.
3.  On the form toolbar, click **Generate Calendar**.
4.  In the **Generate GL Calendars** dialog box, which opens, in the **From Year** and **To Year** boxes, enter the financial years for which you want to generate periods.

    You use these boxes to specify the range of financial years for which periods are generated.

5.  Click **OK** to close the dialog box.

    A financial calendar has been generated with the periods with the *Inactive* status.
