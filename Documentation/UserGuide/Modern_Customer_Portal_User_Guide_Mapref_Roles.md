# Assigning Roles to Users in the Portal {#_6c9cce5b-e789-4eb6-88c3-a7f53937833b .concept}

Roles determine which forms and actions are available to portal users in the Modern Customer Portal. Each role includes a defined set of access rights, and every portal user must have at least one role. Roles can be assigned to portal users by:

-   An administrator in Acumatica ERP
-   An authorized portal user directly in the Modern Customer Portal

When you create a contact—an employee of your company who will use the portal—it becomes visible in both Acumatica ERP and the Modern Customer Portal. You assign one or more roles to the contact, causing a record for the corresponding portal user to be created on the [Users](SM_20_10_10.md) \(SM201010\) form of Acumatica ERP. The user can then sign in to the portal and access forms based on the assigned roles.

In this chapter, you’ll learn:

-   Which predefined roles are available in the Modern Customer Portal and what access rights they include
-   How to create a case to ask an Acumatica ERP administrator to assign roles
-   How to assign roles to contacts directly in the Modern Customer Portal

**Important:**

If the expected functionality isn’t available, contact your company’s portal manager. The portal manager can work with the portal owner to review configuration settings, if needed.
