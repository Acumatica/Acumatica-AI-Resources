# Managing Project Materials {#_791c91e8-db54-473e-9159-eaffc31354d2 .concept}

The topics of this chapter describe how you can track the stock and non-stock items that are used for construction projects.

Project material management provides a centralized way to track the stock and non-stock items used in construction projects, from planning through fulfillment. You can manage material lists, monitor item availability, and keep material costs aligned with project budgets.
