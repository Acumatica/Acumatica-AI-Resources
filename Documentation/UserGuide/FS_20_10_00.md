# Licenses

Form ID: \(FS201000\)

You can use this form to enter a new license or view and modify an existing one. A license is a document proving that a particular person has permission to perform work of a certain kind or has a knowledge in a certain area.

## Form Toolbar

The form toolbar has only standard buttons.

## Summary Area

You use the elements in this area to define a new license and its settings, or to select an existing license by its number and view its settings.
