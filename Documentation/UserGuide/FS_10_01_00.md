# Service Management Preferences {#_1c1b94d7-ac39-4f84-a966-588290b5ba80 .reference}

Form ID: \(FS100100\)

On this form, you specify the general settings for the service management functionality, such as calendar settings, validation requirements for service orders and appointments, approval and mailing settings, and default values.

## Form Toolbar { .section}

The form toolbar has only standard buttons.

## General Tab { .section}

By using this tab, you can define the general settings of the service management functionality.

|Element|Description|
|-------|-----------|
|**Batch Numbering Sequence**|The numbering sequence the system uses during the generation of billing documents to assign identifiers to batches of documents.|
|**Staff Schedule Numbering Sequence**|The numbering sequence the system uses to assign identifiers to the staff schedule rules created on the [Staff Schedule Rules](FS_20_20_01.md) \(FS202001\) form.|
|**Equipment Numbering Sequence**|The numbering sequence the system uses to assign identifiers to equipment created on the [Equipment](FS_20_50_00.md) \(FS205000\) form, as well as to vehicles created on the [Vehicles](FS_20_36_00.md) \(FS203600\) form if the *Route Management* feature is enabled on the [Enable/Disable Features](CS_10_00_00.md#) \(CS100000\) form.|
|**License Numbering Sequence**|The numbering sequence the system uses to assign identifiers to licenses created on the [Licenses](FS_20_10_00.md) \(FS201000\) form.|

|Element|Description|

The settings that control these capabilities are specified in the **Integrating with Time &amp; Expenses** section on the [Service Order Types](FS_20_23_00.md) \(FS202300\) form.|
|**Enable Rooms**|A check box that indicates \(if selected\) that the rooms of branch locations where appointments can be attended can be specified and managed.|

|**Enable Workflow Stages**|A check box that indicates \(if selected\) that the **Workflow Stage** box is available in the summary of the [Service Orders](FS_30_01_00.md) \(FS300100\) and [Appointments](FS_30_02_00.md) \(FS300200\) forms.|
|**Manage Multiple Billing Options per Customer**|A check box that indicates \(if selected\) that multiple billing options can be assigned to a customer depending on the service order type.

 If the check box is selected, when the billing process is run, the system uses the billing cycle associated with the applicable service order type on the **Service Billing** tab of the [Customers](AR_30_30_00.md) \(AR303000\) form.

 If the check box is cleared, the system uses the billing cycle specified for the customer in the **Billing Cycle** box on the **Billing** tab of the [Customers](AR_30_30_00.md) \(AR303000\) form.

 When you change the state of the check box \(that is, when you select or clear it\), the system displays a warning message indicating that after you modify this setting, you should ensure that billing cycles are specified for all customers.

|**Require Manual Filtering on Billing Forms**|A check box that indicates \(if selected\) that the **Apply Filter** button on the [Run Appointment Billing](FS_50_01_00.md) \(FS500100\) and [Run Service Order Billing](FS_50_06_00.md) \(FS500600\) forms is available, and users have to filter the appointments or service orders for which they want to view data.|

 **Attention:** If this check box is selected, location tracking has to be turned on for a user starting and completing an appointment, that is the **Track Location** check box should be selected for the user on the **Location Tracking** tab on the [Users](SM_20_10_10.md) \(SM201010\) form.

-   *Prevent*: The system prevents you from assigning the staff member to an appointment if the staff member does not meet the skill requirements to perform the applicable service or services.

|

-   *Prevent*: The system prevents you from assigning the staff member to an appointment if the staff member does not perform services in the service area of the appointment.

|

Select one of the following:-   *Do Not Validate*: The system does not validate whether the staff member assigned to an appointment meets the license requirement to perform the applicable service or services.

-   *Prevent*: The system prevents you from assigning the staff member to an appointment if the staff member does not meet the license requirement to perform the applicable service or services.

|

Select one of the following:-   *Do Not Validate*: The system does not validate whether the time overlaps for two or more appointments to which the same staff member is assigned.
-   *Warn*: The system warns you if the time overlaps for two or more appointments to which the same staff member is assigned.
-   *Prevent*: The system prevents you from assigning the staff member to an appointment if the time of the appointment overlaps with the time of another appointment to which this staff member is assigned.

## Calendars &amp; Maps Tab { .section}

On this tab, you can specify, view, and edit the settings of the calendar boards.

|Element|Description|
|-------|-----------|
|**Work Calendar**|The identifier of the calendar that is used in your organization to determine when staff members are available to provide services. you configure the calendar on the [Work Calendar](CS_20_90_00.md) \(CS209000\) form.|
|**Appointment Resize Precision**|The accuracy in minutes for which you can schedule appointments on the calendar boards. Select one of the following options:-   *10 minutes*: An appointment can be scheduled on every 10 minutes of the hour.
-   *15 minutes*: An appointment can be scheduled on every 15 minutes of the hour.
-   *30 minutes*: An appointment can be scheduled on every 30 minutes of the hour.
-   *60 minutes*: An appointment can be scheduled on every hour.

|
|**Appointment Auto-Confirm Time**|The range, in minutes and hours, that determines whether an appointment is automatically confirmed when a user creates it. An appointment is automatically confirmed if the time range between the creation of the appointment and the scheduled start time of the appointment is less than or equal to the range in this box. Auto-confirmation applies only when the appointment has not been edited after it has been created. By default, this time range is set to 12 hours.|
|**Number of Staff Members**|The maximum number of staff members to be shown simultaneously on the [Calendar Board](FS_30_03_00.md) \(FS300300\) form. By default, the box contains *10*. If you clear the box and save it, all of the field service staff members will be shown on the calendar board.|
|**Show Service Orders in a Period Of**|An integer that identifies the range for which the service orders are shown on the **Unscheduled** tab of the [Calendar Board](FS_30_03_00.md) \(FS300300\) form. The system determines the dates of the date range as follows:-   If a deadline date is specified in the **SLA** box of the [Service Orders](FS_30_01_00.md) \(FS300100\) form, the service order is shown for a range of *x* days before the date when the service order document was created in the system and *x* days after the deadline date.
-   If no deadline date is specified in the **SLA** box and the planned date of service order fulfillment is specified in the **Promised Date** box of the [Service Orders](FS_30_01_00.md) \(FS300100\) form, the service order is shown for a range of *x* days before the date when the service order document was created in the system and *x* days after the promised date.
-   If the dates are specified in neither the **SLA** box nor the **Promised Date** box of the [Service Orders](FS_30_01_00.md) \(FS300100\) form, the service order is shown for a range of *x* days before and after the date when the service order document was created in the system.

|

|**Refresh GPS Locations Every**|The frequency at which the latest staff locations of applicable staff members are refreshed \(in seconds\) on the [Appointment History on Map](FS_30_12_00.md) \(FS301200\), [Staff Appointments on Map](FS_30_11_00.md) \(FS301100\), and [Staff Routes on Map](FS_30_10_00.md) \(FS301000\) forms if the **Show Location Tracking** check box is selected in the**Calendar Settings** section of the current form.

-   *Booked Days*: The dashboard shows the days to which at least one appointment is assigned for the displayed staff members during the selected time range selected; for each day, it shows only the time interval to which at least one appointment is assigned.

 By default, the *Cleared Filter* option is selected.

## Mailing &amp; Printing Tab { .section}

The **Mailing &amp; Printing** tab contains the list of the predefined mailings for the appointments. Mailings are used to send notifications about an appointment to the scheduled staff members, the customer contact, or the staff members of the appointment's geographical zone. The mailing settings specified here are used as default values for the mailing settings of service order types. Emails for active mailings are sent when you invoke the action specific to the mailing. These emails can contain details on service orders, appointments, the equipment of the service, and other information.

You can modify predefined mailings or add new custom mailings. To add a new custom mailing to be used for customers, you need to configure actions in a mailing workflow for the forms on which specific documents are created or processed. For details, see [Mailings for Customers: General Information](Finance_PredefinedMailings_Customers_GeneralInfo.md) and [Mailings for Vendors: General Information](Finance_PredefinedMailings_Vendors_GeneralInfo.md). For details on workflows, see [Getting Started with Workflow API: General Information](../DeveloperGuide/WorkflowAPI_Overview.md) and [Workflow UI Guide](../DeveloperGuide/WorkflowUI_Guide.md).

The tab includes the **Default Sources** table, with the list of mailings, and the **Default Recipients** table, with the list of recipient types.

|Column|Description|
|------|-----------|
|**Active**|A check box that \(if selected\) indicates that this mailing is active. Only active mailings can be used.|
|**Mailing ID**|The unique identifier of the mailing. An alphanumeric string of up to 10 characters can be used. The following mailings are predefined to inform users and external contacts about the noted events: -   *NOTIFY CUSTOMER*: To notify the customer contacts about the settings of an appointment
-   *NOTIFY SERVICE AREA STAFF:* To notify all the staff members of an of the appointment's service area, so you can check for availability
-   *NOTIFY SIGNED APPOINTMENT:* To notify the customer that the appointment has been signed
-   *NOTIFY STAFF:* To notify the staff members scheduled for the appointment about its settings

|
|**Branch**|The branch for which this mailing is defined.|
|**Default Email Account**|The system email account to be used by default for the mailing. If no account is specified in this box, the default system account is used.|
|**Report**|The identifier of the report, such as [Service Order](FS_64_10_00.md) \(FS641000\) or [Appointment](FS_64_20_00.md) \(FS642000\), to be used as the email's body \(if a report is used for this mailing\). Select the report from the list.|
|**Email Template**|The email template to be used to generate the body of the email \(if an email template is used for this mailing\). **Attention:** For each mailing, you can use only one of the options as the body of the email: a report or a notification template \(email template\).

|
|**Format**|The format in which the report \(if applicable\) is sent by default if a recipient has no other preferences. Select one of the following options: *Text*, *HTML*, *Excel*, or *PDF*.|
|**Recipients**|The way the system specifies recipients in the generated email. The following options are available:-   *Add Recipients*: On the generation of an email to which these settings apply, the system will copy the recipients specified for the selected notification template, if one has been specified for the mailing, and from the **Default Recipients** table on this tab.
-   *Override Recipients*: On the generation of an email to which these settings apply, the recipients will be copied only from the **Default Recipients** table on this tab. The recipients specified in the notification template or report associated with the mailing will not be included in the generated email.

|

|Column|Description|
|------|-----------|
|**Active**|A check box that \(if selected\) indicates that this contact or contact type is active. Only active recipients receive the emails.|
|**Contact Type**|The type of contact to receive the emails generated for the mailing. Select one of the following: -   *Billing*: The contact of the customer listed on the **Financial Settings** tab of the [Service Orders](FS_30_01_00.md) \(FS300100\) or [Appointments](FS_30_02_00.md) \(FS300200\) form.
-   *Employee*: An employee of your company who handles service management, which you specify in the **Contact ID** column of this table.
-   *Customer*: The contact of the customer listed on the **Settings** tab of the [Service Orders](FS_30_01_00.md) \(FS300100\) or [Appointments](FS_30_02_00.md) \(FS300200\) form.
-   *Staff Members*: A staff member who handles service management, which you specify on the **Staff** tab of the [Service Orders](FS_30_01_00.md) \(FS300100\) or [Appointments](FS_30_02_00.md) \(FS300200\) form.
-   *Service Area Staff*: A staff member of the appointment's service area.
-   *Salesperson*: The salesperson assigned to the appointment in the **Salesperson** box, which is on the **Financial** tab of the [Appointments](../Shared/../UserGuide/FS_30_02_00.md) form.

**Tip:** You have to associate a salesperson account with an employee account in the **Salesperson** box, which is in the **Employee Settings** section on the **General Info** tab of the [Employees](../Shared/../UserGuide/EP_20_30_00.md) \(EP203000\) form. Emails will be sent to the email address of the employee. The email address has to be defined in the **Email** box, which is in the **Contact Info** section of the **General Info** tab.


|
|**Contact ID**|The identifier of the recipient. A particular recipient can be selected only if *Employee* was specified as the **Contact Type**. This employee receives all the emails generated for the selected mailing.|
|**Format**|The format in which the report \(if applicable\) is sent. Select one of the following options: *Text*, *HTML*, *Excel*, or *PDF*.|
|**Add To**|The *To*, *CC*, and *BCC* options, which are available in this column, determine the element in which the recipient will be included in the generated email.|
