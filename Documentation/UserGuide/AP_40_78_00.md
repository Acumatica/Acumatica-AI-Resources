# CIS Payment Details

Form ID: \(AP407800\)

You use this form to review the details of payments made to subcontractors under the Construction Industry Scheme \(CIS\) within a specified date range. To display the data, you specify a contractor and a date range in the Selection area; you can further narrow the results by subcontractor and project.

For each payment, the table lists the total payment amount, the cost of materials, the amount liable to deduction, the deduction rate, and the amount deducted.

This form is available only if the *UK Localization* feature is enabled on the [Enable/Disable Features](CS_10_00_00.md) \(CS100000\) form.

## Form Toolbar

The form toolbar has only standard buttons.

## Selection Area

You can use the elements in this area to specify the criteria of the CIS payments you want to view.

</td><td>
