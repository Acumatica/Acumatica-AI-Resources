# Applying Payments to Particular Lines: Related Reports {#_3b02e5bd-ad83-4133-8fb0-e1bf0f16dc1e .concept}

In the following sections, you can find details about the reports you may want to review to gather information about the application of payments to particular lines of AP documents.

**Attention:** If you don’t see a particular report or form that is described, you may have signed in to the system with a user account that doesn’t have access rights to the report or form. Contact your system administrator to obtain access to any needed reports or forms.

## Printing Reports and Documents { .section}

To prepare a printable form of the payment that is being processed, you use the [Check Form](AP_64_05_00.md) \(AP641000\), [Check Form with Remittance](AP_64_10_00.md) \(AP640500\), and [Additional Remittance Form](AP_64_20_00.md) \(AP642000\) reports. In all these reports, a payment of multiple document lines is presented in one line.
