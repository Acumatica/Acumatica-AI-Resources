# Selling a Piece of Equipment and an Optional Component

In Acumatica ERP, you can sell equipment and its components. To do this, you will specify the *Selling Model Equipment* and *Selling Optional Component* equipment-related actions for the selected stock item in an appointment created on the [Appointments](FS_30_02_00.md) \(FS300200\) form.
