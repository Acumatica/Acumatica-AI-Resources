# Master Calendar Generation {#_cfa82ffa-184e-442f-a82c-9c5a665f6041 .concept}

In Acumatica ERP, you can post transactions to periods that are open or to both open and closed periods \(depending on the conditions described in [Closing Financial Periods: General Information](Finance_ClosingPeriods_GeneralInfo.md)\). You generate financial periods on the [Master Financial Calendar](GL_20_10_00.md) \(GL201000\) form and invoke actions that open [Manage Financial Periods](GL_50_30_00.md) \(GL503000\) form with the appropriate action selected in the **Action** box. On this form, you can process periods with the selected action to change their statuses.

## Generating the Master Calendar {#section_grf_mjv_vxb .section}

After you have performed the initial setup, you generate a master financial calendar on the [Master Financial Calendar](GL_20_10_00.md) \(GL201000\) form for any number of financial years by clicking **Generate Calendar** on the form toolbar. For details on how to generate periods, see [To Generate Periods for New Financial Years in Master Calendar](GL__Generating_Periods_for_a_New_Financial_Year.md).

When periods have been generated, you can modify them before any transactions have been made. To do so, you select the **User-Defined Periods** check box. For details about modifying periods, see [Modification of Periods for a Year](GL__CON_Period_Modification.md).

After you have generated the master calendar, you open any number of periods for posting.
