# Integrating Acumatica ERP with SendGrid

In Acumatica ERP, you can configure integration with the [SendGrid](https://sendgrid.com/) email platform. With this integration configured, users can send emails through the SendGrid and track the following:

-   The status of email delivery
-   Emails that bounced or were marked as spam
-   The opening of emails and the clicking of links within them

Users can review the tracking details in the SendGrid portal or in Acumatica ERP.

For marketing communications, you can also synchronize SendGrid unsubscribe groups with marketing categories in Acumatica ERP. This synchronization helps you apply recipients’ category-level unsubscribe preferences when sending mass emails.
