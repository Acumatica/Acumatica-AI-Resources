# Company Profile

Form ID: \(SP201000\)

**Who uses this form:** An authorized portal user of a company using the Modern Customer Portal or Vendor Portal. The ability to enter or modify information in sections of the form depends on your user role or roles.

This form shows basic information about your company—its name, address, contact information, payment methods and instructions, and contacts. On this form, you can review the data, make any needed changes, and enter additional company settings. Each of the Modern Customer Portal and Vendor Portal uses the same contact and company \(business account\) records as Acumatica ERP, but each portal is synchronized with Acumatica ERP independently. Changes made in one portal are immediately reflected in Acumatica ERP.

## Form Toolbar

The form toolbar has only standard buttons.

## Summary Area

This area contains your company’s basic information, which is copied from Acumatica ERP when the portal is created. The portal and Acumatica ERP use the same contact and company records, so updates made in one location are immediately reflected in the other.

The **Company Information**, **Contacts**, and **Company Address** sections are visible on both the Modern Customer Portal and Vendor Portal.
