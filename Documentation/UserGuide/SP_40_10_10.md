# Cases

Form ID: \(SP401010\)

**Who uses this form:** An authorized portal user of a company using the Modern Customer Portal.

In the Modern Customer Portal, this form is used to view and manage support cases, which are integrated with Acumatica ERP’s case management capabilities.

You can use this form as a starting point to create a case for the portal owner’s support team, use filtering and sorting capabilities to find the cases you need, or track the status of any case. Members of the support team view and manage these cases in Acumatica ERP.

This form is available only if both of the following conditions are met in Acumatica ERP for the portal owner:

-   The *Modern Customer Portal* and *Case Management* features are enabled on the [Enable/Disable Features](CS_10_00_00.md) \(CS100000\) form.
-   The portal has the **Case Management** check box selected on the **General** tab of the [Portals](SP_70_10_00.md) \(SP701000\) form.

## Form Toolbar

The form toolbar has only standard buttons.
