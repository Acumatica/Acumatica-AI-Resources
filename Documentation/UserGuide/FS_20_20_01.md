# Staff Schedule Rules {#_700e168c-2cb4-4e9b-bee1-8fda87efd071 .reference}

Form ID: \(FS202001\)

You can use this form to enter a new schedule rule for a particular staff member, or to view and modify an existing schedule rule.

**Tip:** This form opens from the [Staff Schedule Rules](FS_40_10_PL.md) \(FS4010PL\) form when you click **Add New Record** on the form toolbar or click a link in the **Staff Schedule Nbr.** column.

## Form Toolbar { .section}

The form toolbar includes the buttons described below.

|Button|Description|
|------|-----------|
|**Generate Staff Schedules**|Opens the [Generate Staff Schedules](FS_50_04_00.md) \(FS500400\) form, where you can generate the schedule.|
|**Roll Back Schedule Generation**|Cancels the generated staff schedule. This command is available only if the staff schedule has been generated for a staff member. When you click this button, the system cancels the previously generated staff schedule, so that you can update schedule settings, and generate the schedule once again.|

## Summary Area { .section}

In this area, you can define a new schedule and its settings, or select an existing schedule and view its settings.

|Element|Description|
|-------|-----------|
|**Staff Schedule Nbr.**|The unique identifier assigned to the staff schedule in accordance with the numbering sequence specified in the **Staff Schedule Numbering Sequence** box on the [Service Management Preferences](FS_10_01_00.md) \(FS100100\) form.|
|**Staff Member**|The staff member the schedule rule applies to.|
|**Branch**|The unique identifier of the branch where the staff member works.|
|**Branch Location**|The identifier of the branch location \(physical location from which the staff member might depart to attend the appointments\).|
|**Description**|A description of the schedule rule.|

## Scheduling Time Section {#section_ewk_hfn_rfc .section}

In this section, you define the work time of the staff member.

|Element|Description|
|-------|-----------|
|**Start Date**|The date from which the schedule is generated; this is also the date the staff member can start perform the services \(at the times specified in the schedule rule\).|
|**End Date**|The end date of the range when the schedule is generated; this is also the last date the staff member can perform the services. This box is available only if the **Enable Expiration Date** check box is selected on this form.|
|**Enable Expiration Date**|A check box that \(if selected\) makes the **End Date** box available so you can specify the end date of the schedule rule.|
|**Start Time**|The exact time when the staff member can start performing the services.|
|**End Time**|The exact time when the staff member should stop performing the services.|
|**Schedule Type Section**|The type of the schedule rule. One of the following option buttons should be selected:-   **Availability**: Indicates \(if selected\) that the schedule rule defines the times when a staff member is available to perform services.
-   **Unavailability**: Indicates \(if selected\) that the schedule rule defines the times when a staff member is not available to perform services \(for instance, if he or she is on vacation or sick leave\).

|

## Recurrence Section { .section}

In this section, you can define the recurrence settings for the schedule rule.

|Element|Description|
|-------|-----------|
|**Description**|A brief description of the schedule.|
|**Frequency**|The frequency which defines how often the schedule recurs. The following options are available-   *Daily*: The schedule repeats every *x* day\(s\).
-   *Weekly*: The schedule repeats every *x* week\(s\) on the specified day\(s\) of the week.
-   *Monthly*: The schedule repeats every *x* month on the specified day of the month.
-   *Yearly*: The schedule repeats every *x* year\(s\) on the specified day of the selected month\(s\).

|

|Element|Description|
|-------|-----------|
|**Every _x_ days**|An integer that defines the time interval, in days, at which the schedule repeats.|

|Element|Description|
|-------|-----------|
|**Every _x_ weeks**|An integer that defines the number of weeks between each occurrence of the schedule.|
|**Day of the Week**|Check boxes used to select the day\(s\) of the week on which the schedule recurs: **Sunday**, **Monday**, **Tuesday**, **Wednesday**,**Thursday**, **Friday**, and **Saturday**.|

|Element|Description|
|-------|-----------|
|**Every _x_ months**|An integer that defines the time interval in months when the schedule repeats.|
|**First Recurrence**|Defines the specific conditions for the initial occurrence of the schedule. The following options are available: -   *Fixed Day of Month*: Specify the day of the month on which the schedule will recur.
-   *Fixed Day of Week*: Specify the weekday and its order in the month for the schedule to recur.

|
|**Second Recurrence**|A check box that indicates \(if selected\) that the second recurrence is applied to the monthly schedule.Specify one of the following options:

-   *Fixed Day of Month*: Specify the day of the month on which the schedule will recur.
-   *Fixed Day of Week*: Specify the weekday and its order in the month for the schedule to recur.

|
|**Third Recurrence**|A check box that indicates \(if selected\) that the third recurrence is applied.Specify one of the following options:

-   *Fixed Day of Month*: Specify the day of the month on which the schedule will recur.
-   *Fixed Day of Week*: Specify the weekday and its order in the month for the schedule to recur.

|
|**Fourth Recurrence**|A check box that indicates \(if selected\) that the third recurrence is applied.Specify one of the following options:

-   *Fixed Day of Month*: Specify the day of the month on which the schedule will recur.
-   *Fixed Day of Week*: Specify the weekday and its order in the month for the schedule to recur.

|

|Element|Description|
|-------|-----------|
|**Every _x_ year\(s\)**|An integer that defines the number of years between each occurrence of the schedule.|
|**Month of Year**|Check boxes used to select the month or months in which the schedule recurs: **January**, **February**, **March**, **April**, **May**, **June**, **July**, **August**, **September**, **October**, **November**, and **December**.|
|**Schedule On**|The day when the schedule repeats. Select one of the following options: -   *Fixed Day of Month*: Specify the day of the month on which the schedule will recur.
-   *Fixed Day of Week*: Specify the weekday and its order in the month for the schedule to recur.

|
