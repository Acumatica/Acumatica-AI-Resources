# Generate Forecasts {#_c9043b4a-6e6c-487b-9b1f-5b90282dd2f6 .reference}

Form ID: \(AM502000\)

**Attention:** This form is available only if the *Material Requirements Planning* or *Distribution Requirements Planning* feature is enabled on the [Enable/Disable Features](CS_10_00_00.md) \(CS100000\) form.

You use this form to calculate suggested forecasts for the next twelve months, either as an annual amount or for each month, for stock or non-stock items from historical data. Forecasts are used by the inventory regeneration process performed on the [Regenerate Inventory Planning](AM_50_50_00.md) \(AM505000\) form. The historical data used is the released invoices from sales orders or AR invoices and memos in the Inventory Transactions details. You can use multiple years of history and assign a weight to the most current history; provide growth factors positive or negative; select item class, items, warehouses, and/or customers. You can calculate as many times as you like with different parameters. The suggested forecast can be manually adjusted after calculation.

Additionally, this tool can be used to calculate a forecast from existing data for another item, or warehouse, or optionally a customer, to use as a forecast for another item, warehouse, and/or customer. For example, you have a new inventory item that will replace an existing inventory item. You calculate the forecast for the existing item and then update the calculated forecast to assign it to the new inventory item.

After you process the forecast, you can further maintain or adjust it by using the [Forecast](AM_20_20_00.md) \(AM202000\) form.

## Forecast Calculation { .section}

The formula used for calculation is the following:

```
Calculation per Period = ((Last Year * Factor) + (SUM(Previous Years)/
Number of Years With Sales  * (1 - Factor))) * (1 + Growth Rate)
```

The `Factor` is the weight assigned to past 12 months data. If the `Factor` is 1, then older data is not used.

|Factor|Period in Prior Year or Prior Year|Past Period or Year|Forecast Quantity|Change in Units|Change in Percentage|
|------|----------------------------------|-------------------|-----------------|---------------|--------------------|
|0.25|2450|6125|3368.75|-2756.25|-0.45|
|0.50|2450|6125|4287.5|-1837.5|-0.3|
|0.75|2450|6125|5206.25|-918.75|-0.15|
|1.00|2450|6125|6125|0|0|

In the above table for a factor of .50 the calculation is \(6125 \* .5\) + \(2450 \* 1-.50\) = 4287.5 which will be round up to 4288. Forecasted quantities cannot have decimals.

## Seasonality { .section}

If seasonality is used, the forecast is ONLY calculated for the date ranges specified on the [Replenishment Seasonality](IN_20_66_00.md) \(IN206600\) form. We recommend that you create seasonality date ranges that cover the entire forecast 12 months to ensure you will have a forecast.

## Form Toolbar { .section}

The form toolbar includes the buttons described below.

|Button|Description|
|------|-----------|
|**Process**|Inserts the selected calculated lines into the forecast tables.|
|**Process All**|Inserts all of the calculated lines into the forecast tables.|
|**Calculate**|Calculates a suggested forecast for all or selected elements.|

## Selection Area { .section}

These determine which stock items, warehouses, and a customer are selected for calculation.

|Element|Description|
|-------|-----------|
|**Forecast Date**|The starting date for the forecasts to be generated. The default value is the business date. Historical data is selected starting from this date backwards for the past 12 months or if the **Years of History** is specified, the number of years \* 12.|
|**Type**|The type of the forecast. You can select one of the following options:

 -   *Regular*: No seasonality is used for the generated forecast.
-   *Seasonality*: The seasonality specified on the current form is used for the generated forecast.

 This box is shown only if the *Inventory Replenishment* feature is enabled on the [Enable/Disable Features](CS_10_00_00.md) \(CS100000\) form.

|
|**Seasonality**|The seasonality settings used for the forecast. You can select the option in this box if *Seasonality* is selected in the **Type** box.

 With a seasonality selected, the system will use time periods defined on the [Replenishment Seasonality](IN_20_66_00.md) \(IN206600\) form.

 **Attention:** The forecast is only calculated for the date ranges specified in seasonality.

 This box is shown only if the *Inventory Replenishment* feature is enabled on the [Enable/Disable Features](CS_10_00_00.md) \(CS100000\) form.

|
|**Dependent**|This sets the default to the calculated forecast. The *Dependent* check box indicates to the MRP generation that sales orders consume the forecast.|
|**Process by Customer**|A check box that indicates \(if selected and the **Customer** box is empty\) that the forecast is calculated for all customers when they have data.|

|Element|Description|
|-------|-----------|
|**Growth Rate**|The growth rate applied to the calculated forecast.

 This value can positive or negative. For example, for a 10% growth rate, you should enter `.1`; for a negative 15%, you should enter `-.15`. Growth rates are linear, that is the growth rate is applied to each period.

|
|**Factor**|The weight you wish to give for the most current sales history. The value cannot be greater than 1.|
|**Calculate by Month**|A check box that indicates \(if selected\) that the forecast is calculated for each month. Otherwise, the forecast is one per year.|
|**Years of History**|The number of history years to use. When using history, you should assign a weight in the **Factor** box, otherwise only the most recent history \(the past year or the past 12 periods\) is used.|

|Element|Description|
|-------|-----------|
|**Customer**|Optional. The customer for which calculation should be performed. Only the shipments for this customer will be included in calculation.|
|**Warehouse**|The warehouse for which the calculation should be performed. If no value is specified, all warehouses will be included in calculation.|
|**Inventory ID**|The inventory ID of the stock item. If no value is selected, all inventory IDs will be included in calculation.|
|**Item Class**|The item class for which calculation should be performed. If no value is selected, all item classes will be included in calculation.|

## Table { .section}

The table contains the calculated forecast. You can update the selected columns.

The table toolbar has only standard buttons.

|Column|Description|
|------|-----------|
|Included|A check box that you select so that this line will update the forecast records.|
|**Inventory ID**|The stock item for the forecasted line. This can be updated; for example, you would like to use the calculated forecast from this item as the forecast for another item.|
|**Subitem**|This column is visible only if the *Inventory Subitems* feature is enabled on the [Enable/Disable Features](../Shared/../UserGuide/CS_10_00_00.md) \(CS100000\) form.

**Important:** The **Inventory Subitems** check box has been removed from the [Enable/Disable Features](../Shared/../UserGuide/CS_10_00_00.md) \(CS100000\) form because the functionality associated with the *Inventory Subitems* feature will be phased out. If you have this feature enabled in your system, the associated functionality remains available. To disable the feature, contact your Acumatica support provider.

|
|**Warehouse**|The warehouse for the forecasted line. This can be updated; for example, you would like to use the calculated forecast from this warehouse as the forecast for another warehouse, or another item, in another warehouse.|
|**Begin Date**|The forecast is effective as of this date.|
|**End Date**|The last day this forecast is effective unless another forecast having the same item and warehouse and optionally customer has a begin date earlier than this end date.|
|**Quantity**|The calculated forecast.|
|**UOM**|The base, or stocking unit, for the quantity. Only the base unit is forecasted.|
|**Customer**|The customer for the forecasted line. This can be updated; for example, you would like to use the calculated forecast from this customer as the forecast for another customer, or another item, in another warehouse.|
|**Change in Units**|The difference between the forecasted **Quantity** and **Last Year Base**.|
|**Percent Change**|The difference between the forecasted **Quantity** and **Last Year Base**.|
|**Dependent**|Defaulted from the selection criteria value and may be updated. This check box indicates to the MRP generation that sales order consume the forecast.|
|**Last Year**|Actual shipments for this line from history for the past year or the same period in the past year factored by the seasonality.

 This column is hidden by default.

|
|**Last Year Base**|Actual shipments for this line from history for the past year or the same period in the past year.

 This column is hidden by default.

|
|**Seasonality**|The replenishment seasonality assigned to this item and warehouse.

 This column is hidden by default.

|
