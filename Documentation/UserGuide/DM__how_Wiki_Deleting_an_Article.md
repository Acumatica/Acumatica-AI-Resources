# To Delete an Article {#_b97a81a1-12c2-4619-b4ba-9da907d52db1 .task}

You can delete an article along with all its versions as long as you have sufficient access rights to do so.

CAUTION:

If you delete a folder, you will delete all of its contents, including all its articles and subfolders. Be sure this is what you want to do before you delete the folder.

## To Delete an Article { .section}

To delete an article, do the following:

1.  Open the article.
2.  On the wiki toolbar, click **Delete Article**.
3.  In the **Delete Article** dialog box, click **Yes** to confirm that you want to delete the article.

The deleted article is moved to the **Deleted Items** folder. This gives you an opportunity to recover accidentally deleted articles and restore them. For more information, see [To Restore a Deleted Article](DM__how_Wiki_Restoring_a_Deleted_Article.md).

## To Permanently Delete an Article { .section}

If you want to permanently delete an article from the wiki, do the following:

1.  In the navigation pane, open the **Deleted Items** folder.
2.  Open the article you want to permanently delete.
3.  On the wiki toolbar, click **Delete Article**.
4.  In the **Delete Article** dialog box, click **Yes** to confirm the deletion.
