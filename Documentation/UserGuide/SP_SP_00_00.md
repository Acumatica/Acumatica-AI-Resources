# Self-Service Portal {#_37516f24-6bf1-4a2c-8554-49665f71db3d .concept}

Acumatica Self-Service Portal provides a solution for you to work and communicate with your customers more efficiently. Self-Service Portal is specifically designed to be the site where your customers can view all the relevant information about their interaction with your company and perform the relevant activities online. For more information, see [Overview of the Acumatica Self-Service Portal](SP__SP_Portal_Overview.md).
