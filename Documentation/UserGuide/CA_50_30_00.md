# Recalculate Account Balances {#_8b80ee7f-a2bf-4723-a4a4-2c2456ec9859 .reference}

Form ID: \(CA503000\)

By using this form, you can recalculate the balances of cash accounts. The system recalculates the balances based on the history records, and matches them to the account balances stored in the database.

**Tip:** We recommend that you recalculate the balances of cash accounts in any of the following cases:

1.  After you have modified the options on the [Cash Management Preferences](CA_10_10_00.md) \(CA101000\) form that control how the available balances of cash accounts are calculated.
2.  After you have converted an asset account used for other purposes to a cash account. In this case, the system will replace the GL transactions on the account with appropriate cash transactions and rebuild the account history.
3.  If you have detected a discrepancy between the balance of a particular account and its total credits and debits by using the [Cash Account Details](CA_30_30_00.md) \(CA303000\) form.

To recalculate the balances of all cash accounts, click **Process All** on the form toolbar. To recalculate the balances of only particular cash accounts, select the check boxes next to the accounts in the table and click **Process** on the form toolbar.

## Form Toolbar { .section}

The form toolbar includes the buttons described below.

|Button|Description|
|------|-----------|
|**Process**|Recalculates the balances for the accounts that you have selected.|
|**Process All**|Recalculates the balances for all listed accounts.|

## Selection Area {#Selection .section}

You use the element in this area, described in the following table, to select the financial period for which you want to recalculate cash account balances.

|Element|Description|
|-------|-----------|
|**Fin. Period**|The financial period for which you want to start recalculating cash account balances. By default, it is the earliest financial period that is not locked in any company within the tenant.|

## Table { .section}

The table contains the list of all the cash accounts defined in the system.

The table toolbar has only standard buttons.

|Column|Description|
|------|-----------|
|Included|An unlabeled check box that you select to include the cash account in recalculation if you click **Process**.|
|**Cash Account**|The cash account number.|
|**Description**|The description provided for the cash account.|
