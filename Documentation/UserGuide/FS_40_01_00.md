# Appointment Summary

Form ID: \(FS400100\)

On this form, you can see all appointments created in the system and their details.

## Form Toolbar

The form toolbar has only standard buttons.

## Selection Area

You can use the elements in this area to view in the table the data that is most relevant to your needs for information. Most of these boxes can be left blank to view a broader range of information.
