# Mass Emails: General Information

Mass email marketing is a tool that helps you quickly connect with your marketing audience through newsletters, announcements, or offers. This topic provides information about managing mass emails in Acumatica ERP.

## Learning Objectives

In this chapter, you will learn how to do the following:

-   Create and send a mass email to all your customers
-   Send a mass email to the members of a marketing list
-   Send a mass email to the members of a marketing campaign

## Applicable Scenarios

You may want to learn how to manage mass emails in Acumatica ERP in scenarios that include the following:

-   You have launched a marketing campaign and need to send a special offer to a group of leads.
-   You need to send a company newsletter to your company’s potential clients.
-   You need to inform your marketing audience about your company’s new products or services.
-   You need to organize mass emails by marketing categories to apply the recipient's category-level unsubscribe preferences while mass emails are sent.

## Mass Emails

In Acumatica ERP, you can create a mass email and send it to the intended audience—marketing list members, marketing campaign members, or selected leads, contacts, or employees—by using the [Mass Emails](../Shared/../UserGuide/CR_30_80_00.md) \(CR308000\) form. You can set up a mass email to be sent to all members of a particular marketing campaign or to only those members who haven’t previously been sent emails as part of the campaign.

You can also assign a marketing category, such as promotions or newsletters, to a mass email. During processing, the system applies each recipient’s global unsubscribe preferences. If you selected a category and the recipient has unsubscribe preferences for that category, the system applies those preferences as well. For details, see [Mass Emails: Marketing Categories](../Shared/../UserGuide/CRM_Mktg_Managing_Mass_Emails_Marketing_Categories.md).

You can create an email from scratch, or you can use a template prepared using the [Email Templates](../Shared/../UserGuide/SM_20_40_03.md) \(SM204003\) form. By using predefined templates, you can save time. To personalize emails, you can insert the recipient’s data into the text of the email by using placeholders. For details, see [Email Templates](../Shared/../UserGuide/EM__con_Notification_Templates.md).

Before sending a mass email, you can test the mass email by sending a preview email to one of your email accounts. For details, see [Mass Emails: To Create and Send a Mass Email](../Shared/../UserGuide/CRM_Mktg_Managing_Mass_Emails_Create_Mass_Email.md).
