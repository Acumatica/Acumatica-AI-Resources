# Cash Flow Forecast {#_db97fee2-44e5-4cc8-a4e3-485a3d2ad2cb .reference}

This report shows the details of a 30-day cash flow forecast. You do not navigate to a report form and run this report; the report is opened from the [Cash Flow Forecast](CA_40_10_00.md) \(CA401000\) form and is generated based on the settings selected on the inquiry form. To open the [Cash Flow Forecast](CA_65_80_00.md) report, you click **View as a Report** on the form toolbar of the [Cash Flow Forecast](CA_40_10_00.md) form.

The forecast shows the following information for each day of a 30-day period for each of the accounts:

-   The projected balance of the cash account in the **Cash Summary** section
-   The total amount of customer payments to be received this day in the **Cash Receipts** section
-   The total amount of outgoing payments to be sent this day in the **Cash Paid Out** section

In the current report and on the [Cash Flow Forecast](CA_40_10_00.md) form, the amount of a credit memo with credit terms is shown in the column whose date is the same as the due date of the credit memo. \(A credit memo without credit terms has a due date that is the same as the document date.\) If the due date is earlier than the **Start Date** specified in the Selection area of the [Cash Flow Forecast](CA_40_10_00.md) form \(from which this report is generated\), the credit memo’s amount is included in the column whose date is the same as the **Start Date**.

The **Cash Summary** section provides the following information:

-   *Cash On Hand*: The cash position at the beginning of the day; namely, a forecast balance carried forward from the previous day
-   *Cash Available*: The sum of the projected receipts and the cash position at the beginning of the day \(Cash On Hand + total of Cash Receipts\)
-   *Cash Position*: The cash position at the end of the day; namely, the difference between the available cash and the total amount of the projected outgoing payments \(Cash Available – total of Cash Paid Out\)
