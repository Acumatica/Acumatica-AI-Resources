# Late Assignment

Form ID: \(AM312000\)

You use this form for assigning \(or allocating\) the lot or serial numbers of the produced item to its materials after the materials have been issued for a production order. If *On Completion* is selected in the **Require Parent Lot/Serial Number** box for the production order on the [Production Order Maintenance](AM_20_15_00.md) \(AM201500\) form, you must assign the lot or serial number of the produced item to each lot- or serial-tracked material before you move the produced items in stock through the release of the appropriate transaction on the last operation. Otherwise, the system will display an error message, and the transaction will not be released. You record the movement of produced items by using a move transaction on the [Move](AM_30_20_00.md) \(AM302000\) form or a labor transaction with a positive produced quantity on the [Labor](AM_30_10_00.md) \(AM301000\) form.

The form toolbar has only standard buttons.

## Allocated Components Table

In this table, you can view the list of materials allocated for the selected lot or serial number and unallocate the materials if needed.

|Button|Description|
|------|-----------|
|**Unallocate**|Removes the allocation of the material in the selected row \(which you click before clicking this button\) from the lot or serial number and moves the material row to the Unallocated Materials table.|

|Column|Description|
|------|-----------|
|**Inventory ID**|The identifier of the item used as the material.|
|**Description**|The item description.|
|**Lot/Serial Nbr.**|The lot or serial number of the material.|
|**Qty. Allocated**|The material quantity allocated to the lot or serial number selected in the **Lot/Serial Nbr.** box of the Summary area.|
|**UOM**|The unit of measure for the allocated quantity.|

## Unallocated Components Table

In this table, you can view the list of materials that were issued for the production order specified in the **Production Nbr.** box of the Summary area but that are not allocated to any lot or serial number of the produced item. You can allocate the materials to the lot or serial number specified in the **Lot/Serial Nbr.** box of the Summary area.

|Button|Description|
|------|-----------|
|**Allocate**|Allocates the material in the selected row \(which you click before clicking this button\) to the lot or serial number and moves the material row to the Allocated Materials table.|

|Column|Description|
|------|-----------|
|**Inventory ID**|The identifier of the item used as the material.|
|**Description**|The item description.|
|**Lot/Serial Nbr.**|The lot or serial number of the material.|
|**Qty. Unallocated**|The material quantity that has been issued for the production order specified in the **Production Nbr.** box of the Summary area but has not been allocated to any lot or serial number of the produced item.|
|**UOM**|The unit of measure for the unallocated quantity.|
|**Qty. Required**|The material quantity with the specific lot or serial number required to produce the parent item.|
|**Qty. to Allocate**|The material quantity with the specific lot or serial number that must be allocated to the parent lot or serial number.|
