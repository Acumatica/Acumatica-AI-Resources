# Working with AI Assistant

*AI Assistant* is a chat window inside Acumatica ERP where you can ask questions in plain language and quickly explore your data. Instead of checking multiple forms and reports to find answers, you can get answers immediately and act on them with just a click.

AI Assistant can return answers as text summaries, tables, lists, charts, pivot tables, and widgets. You can jump directly to the relevant forms and records from the response, review the source data used for generated widgets, refine widgets in follow-up requests, and add widgets to existing dashboards. You can also ask how to use the product, where to find functionality, or how to troubleshoot an issue. For details about supported widget types and their settings, see [Configuring Widgets](RPT_Configuring_Widgets_Mapref.md).
