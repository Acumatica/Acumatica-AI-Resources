# To Activate the Predefined Periodic Schedule {#_22612fde-175c-49e8-b642-df55b570be4f .task}

To activate the periodic schedule that runs the *Failed &amp; Missed Data Resync* process every five minutes, do the following:

1.  On the [Salesforce Data Resync](SF_20_50_35.md) \(SF205035\) form, click **Schedules** &gt; **View**.

    The [Automation Schedules](SM_20_50_20.md) \(SM205020\) form opens in a pop-up pane with the details of the *Failed &amp; Missed Data Resync every 5 minutes* schedule filled in.

2.  In the Summary area, select the **Active** check box.
3.  On the form toolbar, click **Save &amp; Close**.
