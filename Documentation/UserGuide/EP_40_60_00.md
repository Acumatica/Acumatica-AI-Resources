# Employee Time Cards {#_637f27b7-14ee-4e6c-8d27-72ff377f41fa .reference}

Form ID: \(EP406000\)

On this form, you can enter new employee time cards or view \(and edit, if needed\) existing ones. A time card is a document that an employee uses to record the weekly work hours and overtime hours spent by this employee on a project.

**Important:** You can create and view time cards, if your username you are signed in with is associated with an employee—that is, the employee has the username specified in the **Employee Login** box on the **General** tab of the [Employees](EP_20_30_00.md) \(EP203000\) form.

This form is available only if the *Advanced Financials* feature is enabled on the [Enable/Disable Features](CS_10_00_00.md) \(CS100000\) form.

## Form Toolbar { .section}

The form toolbar has only standard buttons.

## Selection Area { .section}

Use the elements in this area to select the employee whose time cards you want to manage.

|Element|Description|
|-------|-----------|
|**Employee**|The identifier of the employee whose time cards you want to manage. The ID of the currently signed-in employee is specified by default. You can also select an employee in a workgroup at a lower level in the company tree than your workgroup.If the box is blank, the following time cards are listed in the table if the **All Records** filter is applied:

-   Your time cards
-   Time cards of the employees for whom you are an appointed delegate
-   Time cards of the employees who report to you
-   Time cards of employees in the workgroups at lower levels in the company tree than your workgroup

|

## Table { .section}

This table displays a list of time cards that belong to the selected employee.

|Column|Description|
|------|-----------|
|**Employee**|The identifier of the employee whose time card is listed in the line.|
|**Employee Name**|The name of the employee whose time cards is listed in the line.|
|**Period**|The period for which the time card was created.|
|**Frequency**|The time card frequency, which can be one of the following: *Weekly*, *Biweekly*, *Semi-monthly*, or *Monthly*.|
|**Status**|The status of the time card, which can be one of the following options: *Open*, *On Hold*, *Approved*, *Rejected*, or *Released*.|
|**Ref. Nbr.**|The unique reference number of the time card.|
|**Time Spent**|The work hours spent by the employee during the week on activities with the *Regular Hours* earning type.|
|**Overtime Spent**|The work hours spent by the employee during the week on activities with the *Overtime* earning type.|
|**Total Time Spent**|The total working time \(regular and overtime\) for the week.|
|**Billable**|The billable work hours spent by the employee during the week.|
|**Billable Overtime**|The billable overtime spent by the employee during the week.|
|**Total Billable**|The total billable working time \(regular and overtime\) for the week.|
|**Billing Ratio**|The ratio of the total billable time to the total working time in per cent.|

|Filter|Description|
|------|-----------|
|**All Records**|Displays the complete list of employee time cards, regardless of their status.|
|**On Hold**|Displays only the time cards that have the *On Hold* status.|
|**Rejected**|Displays only the time cards that have the *Rejected* status.|
|**Pending Approval**|Displays only the time cards that have the *Pending Approval* status.|
|**Completed**|Displays only the time cards that have the *Approved* or *Released* status.|
