# Activities {#_8b7cac26-6610-4c65-87c4-d87aa94edd73 .reference}

Form ID: \(EP404300\)

This form displays activities assigned to you or your workgroup and the activities assigned to users of other workgroups.

## Form Toolbar { .section}

The form toolbar has only standard buttons.

## Table {#2 .section}

This table shows the activities matching the criteria you specified in the Selection area.

|Column|Description|
|------|-----------|
|**Summary**|The subject of the activity.|
|**Status**|The status of the time activity.|
|**Date**|The date the activity was created.|
|**Time Spent**|The time spent on the activity.|
|**Overtime**|The amount of overtime spent on the activity.|
|**Project**|The project with which the activity is associated \(if any\).|
|**Project Task**|The project task with which the activity is associated \(if any\).|
|**Related Entity**|A link that you click to view the details of the entity associated with the activity \(if any\).|

|Filter|Description|
|------|-----------|
|**All Activities**|If the signed-in user is associated with an employee account, the tab displays the following activities:

 -   Activities created by the members of workgroups that are related to the associated employee account
-   Activities created by the associated employee or the employee's subordinates
-   Activities created by the signed-in user

 Otherwise, the tab displays the complete list of activities, regardless of their owners.

|
|**My Activities**|Displays the list of activities that are assigned to you.|
