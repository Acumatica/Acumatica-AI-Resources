# Multiple Bill Payments: Related Reports and Inquiries {#_20a4d134-b0d0-403a-ba79-06119ab91cfd .concept}

This topic describes the reports, inquiries, and forms you may review to gather information about vendor documents.

**Attention:** If you do not see a report or inquiry, this could mean that you have signed in to the system with a user account that does not have access rights to the form. Sign in as the *admin* user, or contact your system administrator.

## Reviewing Checks that Are Pending Printing {#section_twk_njv_vxb .section}

You can use the [Checks Pending Printing](../Shared/../UserGuide/AP_40_40_00.md) \(AP404000\) form to review which checks have not been printed yet as of a specified pay date. You can initiate check printing on this form by clicking **Print Checks** on the form toolbar. The system navigates to the [Process Payments / Print Checks](../Shared/../UserGuide/AP_50_50_00.md) \(AP505000\) form, where you can print checks.

## Reviewing Vendor Documents {#section_uwk_njv_vxb .section}

You use the [Vendor Details](AP_40_20_00.md) \(AP402000\) form to review the accounts payable documents \(bills, debit and credit adjustments, and payments\) for a particular vendor.

By default, the form displays the list of open vendor documents \(that is, those with the *Open* status\), but you can add to the list closed documents and unreleased documents by selecting the **Show All Documents** and **Include Unreleased Documents** check boxes, respectively.

## Reviewing Vendor Balances {#section_xwk_njv_vxb .section}

You use the [Vendor Summary](AP_40_10_00.md) \(AP401000\) form to review the balances of all vendors for a specified financial period and the total balance that is due to vendors.
