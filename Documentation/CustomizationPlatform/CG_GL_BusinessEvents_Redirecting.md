# To Navigate to the Business Events Form {#_beb8765f-34fb-4e86-af99-c69d3b47e077 .task}

You might need to add a business event to Acumatica ERP during customization. To manage business events in Acumatica ERP, you use the [Business Events](../UserGuide/SM_30_20_50.md) \(SM302050\) form. For more information about business events, see [Using Business Events](../UserGuide/SA_Using_Business_Events_Mapref.md) in the User Guide.

To open this form directly from the [Customization Project Editor](../UserGuide/SM_20_45_10.md), perform the following actions:

1.  In the navigation pane, click **Business Events** to open the Business Events page.
2.  On the page toolbar, click **Manage Business Events**.

This opens the [Business Events](../UserGuide/SM_30_20_50.md) form where you can select a particular business event or create a new one.

**Note:** As soon as you add a new business event to the system or change an existing business event on the [Business Events](../UserGuide/SM_30_20_50.md) form, the business event is saved in the database for the current tenant. You can add the business event to the customization project, as described in [To Add Business Events to a Project](CG_GL_Items_BusinessEvents_Adding.md). If you have changed a business event that is already included in a customization project, you have to update the appropriate item in the project, as described in [To Update Business Events in a Project](CG_GL_Items_BusinessEvents_Updating.md).
