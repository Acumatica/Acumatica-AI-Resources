# Independent witnesses

Keep these gates separate. One screenshot or successful response cannot close all
of them. Independence here means observing each claimed outcome through the
appropriate surface; it does not require a separate person for every check.

Select only applicable gates. Form mapping applies to contextual Help. Package
durability applies to articles distributed by a customization project. A standalone
editor-owned article can complete without either; verify its publication state,
identity, content, reader and requested Wiki navigation instead.

| Gate | Evidence | Does not prove it |
|---|---|---|
| Source/ownership | Current article source and owner, reviewed package or editor diff | Old content matching an old source |
| Publication | Installed item/content matches intended revision and publication state | Import success |
| Identity | Wiki, Article ID, persistent PageID and locale agree | Title alone |
| Form mapping, when contextual | Actual form Help resolves the intended article | Direct article URL, HTTP 200 or nonzero but wrong ID |
| Render | Inner article content, formatting and link destinations inspected | Outer Help popup or spinner |
| Reader | Intended role can read the article through the required route | Administrator result or screen Select rights |
| Package durability, when distributed | Revised content and stable identity/Help/rights after normal republish | Source reproducibility alone |

Use a compact evidence record: gate, expected, observed, source/build/role and
pass/fail/unavailable. An unavailable ordinary-reader session leaves reader
acceptance open while authoring, package comparison and Administrator render
can proceed. A failing gate must remain failing until a fresh witness closes it.

No extra cold restart, every-role census, indexing run, locale matrix or new
publication is required for an unrelated text correction. Select witnesses from
the affected contract. A changed package-owned article still needs its next normal
publication and reader/Help verification before deployment acceptance.
